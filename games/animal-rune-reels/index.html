Animal Rune Reels

TACTICAL REEL COMBAT

Spin animal runes to command attacks. Read the monster's intent, hold one reel, and let Mimi's rabbit rune restore your health.

How to play

Three center runes resolve from left to right. Fia strikes, Leo guards, Orla weakens the enemy, Mimi heals, crystals charge Focus, and claws empower the monster. Hold one reel for the next spin, but every turn ends with the shown enemy intent.
