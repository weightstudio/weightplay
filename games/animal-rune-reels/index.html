Animal Rune Reels

TACTICAL REEL COMBAT

Spin animal runes to command attacks. Read the monster's intent, hold one reel, and let Mimi's rabbit rune restore your health.

How to play

The reels visibly spin and stop one by one. Lion symbols multiply attack, turtle symbols multiply defense, rabbit gems heal, and dormant stones have no effect. Results play as attack, defense, healing, then the enemy squad strikes.
