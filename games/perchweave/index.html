Perchweave

Recruit birds, arrange their seats and perform before the last beat.

Game guide

Goal

Build a bird chorus that completes the concert commission before the last beat.

How to play

Recruit with reeds and dew. Arrange birds in seat order and perform a line for notes and supplies.

Controls and rules

Tap a face-up card and an empty seat. Each placement or performance costs a beat. Neighboring birds with different tones add a note. In the second chapter, read the wind window or spend the Windbell.

Finishing and feedback

Complete every part of the commission to open the next concert. Retry for a different lineup, or spend fewer beats for more stars. Campaign progress is saved in this browser.

FAQ

What if I run out of supplies?

Gather or perform in the Grove or Brook to get supplies, unless gathering is closed. Some birds also return supplies.

Do directors cost diamonds?

No. Earn the four directors by clearing concerts. The amber mat is an optional look.
