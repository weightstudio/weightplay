Space Signals

Send commands ahead of time and tune the dish to catch their delayed echoes.

Game guide

Turn-based space strategyDelayed signal timing

Goal

Recover enough samples by the final beat. Select a request to see when its echo will return.

How to play

Tap a request to aim the dish, then send for two power. Listen to regain one power. Match the return sector to catch a packet. Silence and terrain affect individual beats.

FAQ

Why use the relay?
The second chapter lets you hear two sectors together for one power. Use it when the due packets share a beat.
How do I earn three stars?
Recover every sample without dropping a packet. Replay cleared missions with a different tool to find new solutions.
When and where should I listen?
Select a request and read its return beat and sector. Listen in that sector on the indicated beat, or cover it with a relay when available.
Why was a packet missed?
Terrain shadow can block the listed sector. On a receive-only beat, listen or relay instead of sending. On other beats, missing the sector or listening at the wrong time loses the packet.

News inspiration

NASA projected that Voyager 1 would reach one light-day from Earth on November 18, 2026. A radio signal takes about 24 hours to travel that distance one way.

At the fictional Blue Ridge station, echoes return after a fixed number of turns. Sending early is useful only if you can listen when they arrive.
