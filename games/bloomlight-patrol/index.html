Bloomlight Patrol

Lead a flock of little lights into the flower stands. Gather stragglers before crossing the wind gates.

How to play

Goal

Lead the lights into the flower stand.

Play

Keep the flock close to the lantern.

Controls and rules

Drag on the garden or use the arrow keys. Slow down to keep the lights in reach. Open gates have green edges.

Finish

Fill the flower stands before time runs out. Replay a route to improve your stars.

The story behind the game

Taipei’s Shilin chrysanthemum festival opened on November 27, 2026 with fifteen displays and an evening light installation. In this fictional garden, you lead floating lights through windy paths.
