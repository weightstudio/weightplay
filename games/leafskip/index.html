Goal

Collect every stamped packet and land inside the greenhouse at the end.

How to play

Tap to hop forward. Each landing refills two extra air hops. Let the cutter descend before hopping again to reach low packets.

Controls and rules

Tap the scene, click Hop, or press Space. Holding does not repeat. Press B or tap the sail to brake after Stage 30.

Red teeth and ceiling beams are hazards. Wind arrows show direction. Jade bridges fold after landing. Lifts carry their packets. Blue packets open gates; collect them first.

In Chapter 2, tap the sail or B while airborne to slow and descend. Each blue dock refills one use. Rising leaves mark lift wells.

On the chapter finale, land in each small greenhouse before flying onward. Its leaves light up after delivery.

Finishing and feedback

Clear a course to unlock the next. Optional golden pollen improves your stars; all stages and functional tools are free.
