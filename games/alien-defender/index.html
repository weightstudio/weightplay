Defend the signal station across 30 authored Stages. Each Stage is one Battle formation; credits buy firepower and survival upgrades.

How to play

Move, fire, and clear one Stage at a time.

Controls

  1. Move with arrows or A/D and fire with Space.
  2. On mobile, hold the left/right pads and tap Fire.
  3. Clear each formation before the invaders reach the defense line.

Chapter rules

30 Stages are grouped into six chapters of five: patrol, volleys, armored captains, relay formation, crossfire, then the signal-core finale.
