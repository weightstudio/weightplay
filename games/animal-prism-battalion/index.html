Captain Fia powers a prism launcher at the edge of the Starlight Citadel. Every recruit that crosses a crystal gate changes the strength of the army, so the safest lane is not always the strongest lane.

How to play

  1. Drag or use Left and Right to aim the launcher.
  2. Route the spirit stream through helpful gates and away from drain gates.
  3. Tap Overdrive when charged to break a dangerous formation.
