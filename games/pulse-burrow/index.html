Pulse Burrow

Move to the beat and drive the burrow spirits back.

Game guide

Goal

Clear the spirits, then step on the lit exit.

How to play

Tap a nearby tile or press an arrow key. Step toward a spirit to strike it.

Timing and terrain

Queue one move per beat. Land it in the bright window to charge the drum. Blinking tiles show the next threat.

Clearing a room

Stay healthy and land clean beats to earn more stars. Craft drum upgrades between stages.
