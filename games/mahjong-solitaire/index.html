Mahjong Solitaire

Match open identical tiles, recover from a mismatch, and clear a short six-pair board before trying the optional Mastery round.

GameplayClassic Puzzle
GenreClassic · Puzzle · Strategy
Session2–5 minutes

How to play

  1. Select two visible open tiles with the same symbol.
  2. If the pair matches, both tiles disappear and Remaining updates immediately.
  3. If the symbols differ, keep the newly selected tile and choose its matching partner.
  4. Use Hint for a live pair suggestion without auto-playing a tile.
  5. Clear the six-pair standard round, then choose the optional 12-pair Mastery round or return to Main.
