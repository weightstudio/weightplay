Gust Rig

Adjust two winches to guide a festival balloon through changing wind and gate shapes.

Game guide

Goal

Adjust two winches to guide a festival balloon through changing wind and gate shapes.

How to play

Select a winch change to preview it. Confirm to move the balloon through this gate.

Controls and rules

Reeling in raises the balloon. Pulling harder on one side tilts it toward that side. The arrow shows the wind’s extra tilt.

Finishing the route

Clear every gate and collect every paired ribbon for three stars. Undo or retry to improve your route.

Gusts draw attention before a milestone parade

New York scheduled a public balloon inflation for November 25, 2026, ahead of its 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade the next day.

Ahead of the parade, forecasts put attention on whether strong winds would affect the giant balloons. The decision was still pending during preparations.

In the fictional festival of Gust Rig, predict the next gust and balance two winches to thread a moth balloon through town gates.

FAQ

How do the ribbons work?
Pass both marked gates in a row. If you miss the first one, undo before attempting the second.
Do I need to buy any tools?
Every route can earn three stars with the basic winches. The workshop gives you extra ways to solve a gate.
