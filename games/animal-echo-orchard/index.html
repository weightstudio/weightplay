Orchard Sound Memory

Orchard Sound Memory is a 30-stage sequence puzzle set in a moonlit orchard. Watch four chime-fruits glow, then tap the same order to help the orchard keeper tune every path.

GameplaySequence memory puzzle

World and mission

The orchard is arranged as six named arcs: Dawn Canopy, Lantern Bend, Creek Crossing, Wind Garden, Stone Circle, and Moon Crown. Each stage presents a fixed pattern of amber, mint, coral, and violet fruits. Your task is to observe the light sequence, reproduce it accurately, and gradually restore the whole orchard's rhythm.

How the systems work

Press Show the sequence when ready: the fruits light one at a time. During your turn, the four fruit buttons become active and the pattern slots show your progress. A correct full sequence marks the stage solved, saves the result, and unlocks the next stage. After a wrong tap, press Replay glow to review the sequence and start a new answer; there is no life counter or timer.

How to play

Choose an unlocked stage from the rail, watch every glow, and tap the four fruit buttons in exactly the same order. Use Replay glow when you want to review the pattern, then continue from the first fruit. After a clear, use Next stage or return to the stage map; the map also lets you revisit completed stages.

Strategy tips

Treat the pattern as a small rhythm rather than a string of isolated colours. Group repeated fruits, notice alternation and symmetry, and keep your eyes on the pattern slots while entering the answer. On longer stages, pause for a breath between watching and tapping; a careful replay is faster than guessing.

Campaign and difficulty

Stages 1–5 introduce short three-to-five-fruit patterns. Stages 6–10 lengthen the sequences and introduce more alternation. Stages 11–20 ask you to hold seven-to-nine positions while the focus shifts through repetition and rhythm. Stages 21–30 use nine to eleven fruits (nine in 21–25, ten in 26–29, and eleven in 30), add symmetry checks, and end with the Moon Crown mastery arc. Stages 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 are checkpoints.

Developer design note

We built the orchard as a quiet observation loop: the game gives the player time to watch, then makes the answer legible through four large, consistent controls. The six arcs let difficulty grow by pattern length and structure rather than by adding a clock or punishment. Stage, Battle, and Result remain separate so a player can understand what is unlocked, what is being remembered, and what was just completed on touch or mouse.

Player and save information

No account or purchase is required. The game supports touch and mouse input, and sound can be changed in its settings. Unlocked stages, solved stages, and the best full-orchard tap result are stored locally in this browser. Clearing site data or changing browsers may remove that local progress; it is not a cloud save.

FAQ

How many stages are included?
Thirty authored stages are arranged in six arcs of five, with checkpoints at stages 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30.
What do I remember?
Watch the amber, mint, coral, and violet chime-fruits, then tap the same sequence.
What happens after a wrong tap?
Press Replay glow when ready. The answer then clears and the pattern replays; unlocked stages stay saved.
Can I replay a completed stage?
Yes. Completed stages stay selectable, and Replay glow shows the current pattern again.
Is progress saved?
Unlocked and solved stages plus the best full-orchard tap result save only in this browser; there is no account or cloud save.
