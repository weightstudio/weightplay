Goal

Take the lead at the final whistle.

How to play

Choose a lane, then a move. Find an open shot, read the next attack and rally before the last possession.

Controls and rules

Shoot or drive from an open lane. On defense match the incoming lane. Rest to recover stamina.

Finishing and feedback

Finish ahead and meet the match objective. Earn extra stars by reaching the winning margin and stopping enough possessions.

News inspiration

On November 11 in Chicago, the visiting team came back from 22 points down to win 124–119. In the fictional league, an open shot can close the gap, while a stop or steal changes what is possible on the next possession.

Campaign and playbook

The Terrace League introduces shot selection, then pressing and passing. Under the Lights adds traps on two lanes and higher stamina costs for some wing shots. Win matches 1, 15 and 30 to unlock new tactics. Equip one in the playbook before a match.

Player and save information

Play solo with touch, mouse or keyboard. Use 1, 2 or 3 to select a lane, and Tab and Enter to choose an action. This browser stores stars and equipped tactics. The optional night jersey costs 8 diamonds and changes the uniform.

Why does a wing shot score three?
An open wing shot is worth three. A shot from the middle or a drive is worth two. Check the defender and the stamina cost first.
What does pressing change?
Press the lane they attack from to steal the ball. The next made basket gains one point, but the defender spends two stamina.
How do I get three stars?
Finish ahead and meet the match objective. Earn extra stars by reaching the winning margin and stopping enough possessions.
