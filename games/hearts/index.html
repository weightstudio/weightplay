Hearts Guide

Four players follow suit through thirteen tricks. Hearts are penalty cards, the Queen of Spades is worth 13 points, and taking every penalty card enables Shooting the Moon.

How to play

Choose three cards to pass, then play a legal card from your hand. The first trick starts with the Two of Clubs; the winner leads the next trick.
