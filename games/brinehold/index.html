Game guide

Sail to a supply berth, rotate the cargo to fit your hold, and unload it at the marked harbor.

Help

Tap an adjacent pier or focus it and press Enter. A shallow route shows its draft limit and tide window. Repack without spending a turn.

Cargo delivered

Unloading makes the boat lighter. Clear voyages to unlock tools and revisit a voyage for a more efficient route.

Do I need diamonds?

Every voyage can be completed with the standard boat. Diamonds only buy a permanent color scheme.

How do I use a shallow route?

Wait for the indicated tide and keep your draft within the limit. Unloading at a harbor can free up draft.
