Flight manual

Move with WASD, arrows or the joystick. Weapons aim and fire automatically. Fly near crystals to collect them. Carry pods to the central beacon. Shield blocks damage for 2 seconds; Pulse clears nearby threats. Both recharge. Clear missions to unlock stages. Stars: completion, full hull, full hull with half the time remaining. Progress is stored in this browser.
