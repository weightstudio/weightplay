How to play

Each hand deals ten cards to you and the AI. On every turn, draw one card from the stock or discard, then discard one card.

  1. Build sets of three or more cards with the same rank, or runs of three or more consecutive cards in one suit.
  2. Deadwood is the value of cards outside detected melds: Aces count as 1 and face cards as 10.
  3. Knock after drawing with 10 or less Deadwood, or reach 0 Deadwood for Gin. If the stock runs out, lower Deadwood wins.

Decision guide

Read the visible discard before drawing. Keep connected cards that can become a set or suited run, and trim isolated high-value cards first. A low-Deadwood Knock is safer than waiting for Gin when the stock is short.

Progress and replay

There is no campaign ladder. Each new hand deals a fresh position against the AI, so replay value comes from reading a different discard order and choosing a better finish.

Developer note

Gin Rummy makes one small action carry the strategy: take from the stock or discard, expose a discard, and decide whether to keep improving or end the hand. Battle shows melds, Deadwood, stock, discard, and the current plan path; Result explains Gin, Knock, or an empty-stock finish.

Player and save facts

The round score and preferences stay in this browser. The current hand is not saved to the cloud, and the free public game needs no account or purchase.

FAQ

What counts as a meld?
A set has at least three cards of one rank; a run has at least three consecutive cards of one suit.
What is Deadwood?
It is the value of cards that are not part of a detected meld.
When can I Knock?
After drawing, Knock is available at 10 or less Deadwood; at 0 you finish with Gin.
