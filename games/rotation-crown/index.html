Rotation Crown

Choose a captain and partner for each arena. Save the right teammates for the final round.

How to play

Team rotation strategy

Goal

Win enough arena seals to clear each match. For three stars, claim every seal.

How to play

Pick a captain by their score for the current arena. From match six, add a partner for support and a possible link bonus. Preview the score before sending the lineup.

Controls and rules

Tap or click a roster tile to fill the selected slot. With a keyboard, use Tab and Enter. Tap a round at the top to see its rival counter or partner restrictions. A player rests after competing. In the second cup, a winning partner can leave an imprint for the next captain.

Results and progression

The result marks each seal. Win to unlock the next match. Clear matches 1, 15 and 35 to unlock different coach cards. Change cards from Matches before retrying. Progress is stored in this browser and the game has no timer.

FAQ

Which team should I save?
Save a strong specialist for a later high target if a different captain already clears this round. Opponent counters and mixed-pair bonuses can change who fits best.
How do I get three stars?
Win each round of the same match. Any unlocked stage can be replayed without spending diamonds.

Inspired by the news

The 2026 world championship final in Brooklyn ended 3–1 in favor of BLG over Gen.G. The opening game went to Gen.G.

That change of lineups across a series inspired the fictional arena: each competitor normally appears once, so the last round depends on who you kept on the bench.
