Animal Reef Fisher - Game Guide

Animal Reef Fisher is a 30-mission fishing campaign. Bubble Fin the otter charges each cast, controls line tension, records 12 creatures, and upgrades six pieces of gear with Reef Notes. Six regions change the safe-zone rule, while every fifth mission ends with a named Guardian whose pull, armor, or current pattern must be controlled.

The Reef Survey Story

The Reef Archive charts six connected waters: Sunny Shore, Kelp Garden, Coral Gate, Moon Tide, the Storm Shelf, and the Abyssal Blue. Unusual tides have moved familiar fish away from their recorded lanes. Bubble Fin takes a research boat through each route, using catches to confirm which species still travel there and how the water now pulls against a line.

Reef Notes are field credits for the rod, reel, line, bait, boat, and scanner. Every fifth mission reaches a habitat marker guarded by Sun Crown Guardian, Kelp Leviathan, Coral Ram Ray, Eclipse Manta, Storm Lantern Warden, or Crystal Crown Sovereign. Landing one completes its five-mission chart. Mission 30 restores all six charts and records the final deep-water Guardian.

How an Expedition Works

  • Choose any unlocked mission from the horizontal reef map. The card states its sea condition, catch goal, and whether a Guardian is waiting. Winning unlocks exactly the next mission; earlier missions remain replayable.
  • An expedition starts with 90 seconds. Boat Range adds five seconds per level above Level 1. Hold the water or Space to charge, then release. A stronger cast shortens the wait before the bite, so a quick full cast leaves more expedition time for line control.
  • After a bite, the red tension marker reacts to the fish's pull. Drag the marker, slide on the sea, or use Left and Right. Keeping it inside the green SAFE band reduces fish power. Staying outside too long breaks the line, returns the run to casting, and leaves the expedition clock running.
  • Normal fish use steady, darting, or heavy pull identities. Steady fish reward small corrections, darting fish reverse direction in bursts, and heavy fish hold one direction longer. The current fish identity is shown in the hook instruction and Sonar readout.
  • Each catch adds species points, Reef Notes, and a first-discovery bonus when its album entry is new. Reaching the mission goal before time expires completes the expedition. Result lists every landed fish, total score, earned Notes, and new album entries.

Sea Conditions and Guardian Rules

  • Open Water keeps one steady safe band so Missions 1-4 teach casting, recovery, and the three normal fish pulls. Mission 5's Sun Crown Guardian uses three smooth but stronger pulse lengths.
  • Kelp Drift moves the entire safe band from side to side. The Kelp Leviathan adds a long heavy pull, so following the band matters as much as correcting the marker.
  • Coral Snags makes the safe band breathe between wide and narrow windows. Coral Ram Ray closes its armor every other two-second phase; line control still prevents a break while damage waits for the next opening.
  • Moon Tide moves the safe band between opposite sides every four seconds. Eclipse Manta feints before changing pull direction, punishing a large correction made too early.
  • Storm Gust narrows the normal band and strikes the line every three seconds. Storm Lantern Warden combines those visible gusts with a persistent heavy pull.
  • Abyss Trial combines a drifting narrow band with timed gusts. Crystal Crown Sovereign also alternates guarded and open phases, making Mission 30 a test of every late-campaign signal.

Gear, Album, and Optional Tools

Rod Strength removes fish power faster. Reel Control makes the marker follow the chosen position more smoothly. Line Durability both widens the safe band and improves control. Bait Quality increases catch Notes and contributes to landing speed. Boat Range extends the mission clock. Reef Scanner expands the species pool that can appear in earlier regions, which helps fill missing album entries without changing the mission goal. Each item has five saved levels and uses Reef Notes rather than Diamonds.

A prepared Rare Lure costs three Diamonds and guarantees one rare fish on the next eligible cast of the next expedition. A two-Diamond Sonar Ping can be used while aiming to reveal and lock the next fish, including its rarity and pull identity, before casting. Both purchases require a separate confirmation press, and neither tool is required to unlock missions, land a Guardian, upgrade gear, or complete the album through ordinary play.

How to Play

  1. Press Start Game, swipe the mission rail, and select an unlocked card after reading its sea-condition line.
  2. Prepare a Rare Lure or Sonar only if wanted, then spend Reef Notes on the gear that addresses the current problem.
  3. Hold the water or Space, release the cast, and wait for the named fish to bite.
  4. Drag the red knob, slide across the sea, or use Left and Right to follow the moving green band.
  5. When the line breaks, cast again immediately. On Guardian missions, read the boss cue before making a large correction.
  6. Reach the displayed catch goal before the clock ends, review the catch list, then return to the Reef Map for the newly unlocked mission.

Strategy Notes

  • A full cast does not choose a rarer species; it shortens bite delay. Use it when the mission clock is the main problem.
  • If ordinary fish escape, improve Reel Control or Line Durability. If catches are safe but too slow, Rod Strength and Bait Quality shorten each fight.
  • Follow the green band instead of forcing every marker back to 50. Kelp, Moon, and Abyss missions deliberately make the safe center move.
  • On Coral Ram Ray and Crystal Crown Sovereign, an armored phase is not wasted time. Stabilize the line so the next open phase begins without accumulated danger.
  • Storm gusts occur on a three-second rhythm. Hold near the middle of the current safe band before the next flash, then correct after the push.
  • Use Sonar when a specific pull identity matters. Use the one-cast Rare Lure when a rare album entry is more valuable than advance information.

Development and Design Notes

The core loop stays consistent: charge a cast, then manage tension. Difficulty comes from readable water rules. A drifting or narrowing band changes where to aim; a gust changes when to hold; Guardian armor changes when safe control creates progress. Five-mission chapters teach one condition before its Guardian adds a distinct pull profile.

Phone, desktop, tablet, and short landscape use the same logical Battle composition. Pointer and keyboard share one state, hidden-page time is suspended, and Result remains inside Battle above the physical reserve. Album progress requires repeated tension decisions, while saved gear and mission selection give return visits a concrete planning choice.

Player and Save Information

No account is required. Unlocked missions, selected mission, Reef Notes, best catch count, 12 album entries, six gear levels, and prepared Lure or Sonar state are stored in this browser. Clearing site storage or changing browsers may start a separate save. The shared Diamond balance is optional and is used only for the confirmed Lure and Sonar preparations described above. Result gives tactical next-cast advice from the current expedition and does not assess the player.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many missions and Guardian fish are there?
There are 30 missions. Missions 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 contain six different guaranteed Guardian encounters.
Why does the green safe band move or change size?
That is the mission's sea condition. Kelp drifts, Coral breathes narrower, Moon switches sides, Storm adds gusts, and Abyss combines several rules.
What happens when the line breaks?
The current fish escapes and the game returns to casting. The expedition continues, but its clock does not reset.
Does cast power select a better fish?
No. More power shortens the bite wait. Species come from the mission pool, scanner level, one prepared lure, or a locked Sonar result.
Can Guardian armor be damaged while closed?
No. Coral Ram Ray and Crystal Crown Sovereign require SAFE control during an open phase; staying stable while closed prevents a line break.
Are Diamonds required?
No. Diamonds only prepare an optional one-cast Rare Lure or a next-fish Sonar lock. All missions, Guardians, gear levels, and ordinary album progress work without them.
Which progress is saved?
The browser stores mission unlocks, Notes, album discoveries, gear levels, best catches, selected mission, and prepared optional tools.
Can the game be played with a keyboard?
Yes. Hold and release Space to cast, then use Left and Right on the fishing area or tension slider. Touch and mouse use the same rules.
