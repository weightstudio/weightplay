Twin Signal Jump

Twin Switchyard is an original synchronized platform puzzle. Guide an otter and a mountain goat through split platform lanes, coordinate their shared moves, collect every shard, activate each switch, and reach their paired lantern exits.

World and Mission

Two route keepers share one signal line: the otter travels the low lane while the mountain goat takes the high lane. Every puzzle asks both travelers to move through the same moment without leaving one behind.

How the Systems Work

Left and Right move both travelers across their lanes, and Jump gives both the same hop. Platforms, floor heights, shards, fixed switches, and static hazards create different route decisions. Both lantern gates open together after every switch in the stage has been reached.

Collect each shard with its assigned traveler. A collision with a fixed hazard or a missed fall resets the current attempt. Once all shards and switches are complete, guide both travelers to their paired exits. Progress and best move counts stay in this browser.

How to Play

  1. Choose any unlocked stage. Its card shows the stage number and a best move count when available.
  2. Use Left and Right or the on-screen controls to move both travelers; Jump makes them hop together.
  3. Collect each shard with the traveler in its lane. Reach every switch in a stage to open both gates together.
  4. Guide both travelers to their paired exits. A collision or missed fall restarts the attempt; a clear opens Result and normally unlocks the next stage.

Strategy Tips

  • Plan both landing positions before Jump; the shared hop can put the travelers on different shelves.
  • Use short direction presses near pickups and hazards so both travelers do not drift past a safe position.
  • Read both lanes' platforms, shards, switches, and hazards together; the nearest shard is not always the safest next pickup.
  • After a failure, note which lane reached a fixed hazard first and adjust the shared timing.

Campaign and Difficulty Growth

Thirty authored stages form six five-stage arcs. Checkpoints at Stages 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 combine the documented shared movement and jumps, offset platforms and shards, fixed switches, static hazards, and paired exits. There are no alternating gates, moving hazards, or moving platforms.

Developer Design Note

Twin Switchyard keeps one logical play space across phone, landscape, and desktop. Touch, mouse, and keyboard controls resolve to the same shared movement state, while Main, Stage, Battle, and Result remain separate screens with direct returns.

FAQ

Can the two travelers move independently?
No. Each input moves the pair together, so the puzzle is about shared timing and lane planning.
When do the gates open?
Both lantern gates open together after every switch in that stage has been reached; stages without switches start with both open.
What causes a failed stage?
A traveler touching a fixed hazard or falling off the playable route resets the current attempt.
Does progress save?
Cleared stages and local best move counts are saved in this browser. Thirty authored stages are arranged in six five-stage arcs.
