Reefwatch Tides

Read changing currents, collect bloom samples and deploy cultures to keep the coral nurseries safe.

Game guide

Protect the nurseries

Keep every coral nursery alive, deploy the required cultures and finish within the bloom limit.

Collect and cultivate

Pick Sample, then tap a site with bloom. Two samples incubate for one tide. Place a ready culture where the bloom will pass.

Read the arrows

You have two actions per tide. Use the arrows to see where bloom will move. Nets hold bloom, valves redirect it and anchors pin the main route. Advance when ready.

The last tide

A nursery loses a heart at two or more bloom. Two clean tides restore one heart. After the last tide you can review the result, retry or continue.

Do I need diamonds to clear the campaign?

Every stage can be cleared with the standard kit. Diamonds buy a permanent reef appearance.

How does the return current work?

In Chapter 2, return tides replace the outgoing routes. An anchor preserves the current main route for two tides.
