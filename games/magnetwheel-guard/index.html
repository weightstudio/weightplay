Magnetwheel Guard

Catch parts from a rotating wheel and combine them to repel workshop spirits.

Game guide

Goal

Catch parts from a rotating wheel and combine them to repel workshop spirits.

How to play

Read the spirit’s next move. Tap parts as they pass a lit gate. A full tray fires its combination automatically.

Parts and timing

A blade deals damage. A plate blocks the next hit. A cell plus a blade pierces armor. On keyboard, select a part with ← or → and take it with Space.

Clearing a round

Drive out the spirit before the workshop breaks. Revisit cleared stages with new magnetic heads.
