Pong

Read the highlighted ball, align the paddle, and choose when to Serve across a short five-rally match.

GameplayClassic Arcade
GenreClassic · Arcade · Action
Session2–5 minutes

How to play

  1. Start a match and read the highlighted ball lane for the current rally.
  2. Use Left and Right to move the paddle under that target.
  3. Press Serve to settle one rally; alignment wins the rally and adds points.
  4. Read the next ball location and repeat until all five rallies are settled.
  5. Use the Result goal to choose a cleaner rematch, or return to Main.

Progression and difficulty

Each rally changes the target lane. A deliberate move can win the rally, while an extra move still counts toward the Result goal, making the short match easy to understand but worth replaying.

Practical tips

  • Move only as far as needed to align the paddle before Serve.
  • Use Hint when you want the same target instruction stated plainly; the decision remains yours.
  • Compare Score and Moves on Result, then try Play again for a cleaner match.

Player, device, and save information

Touch, mouse, and keyboard controls use the same Left, Right, and Serve decisions. Best results and sound preference stay in this browser; no account or purchase is required.

FAQ

What does the highlighted ball mean?
It shows the target lane for the current rally.
What happens after a missed Serve?
The rally is settled without a point, and the next target appears.
Is progress saved?
Best results and preferences are saved only in this browser.
