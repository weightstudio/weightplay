Cinderburrow

Carve a way through the ridge, bring glow cores home, and brace the gates before the ash surges.

Game guide

Mining strategyStrategy Game

Your goal

Bring enough glow cores back to the depot and keep the ridge beacon alive through the ash surges.

How to play

Dig into adjacent rock and haul ore home. Spend copper on a stronger gate or a shortcut that saves turns.

Controls and rules

Tap a neighbouring cell or use the arrows. Moving and digging advance the forecast. At the depot, choose a gate before leaving.

Finishing a shift

After the last surge, clear the shift with the beacon intact and the required cores delivered. The recap shows what happened at each surge.

Player and save information

Play solo with touch or a keyboard. Your best marks, gear and suspended shift stay in this browser; clearing site data erases them. There is no account or online ranking.

FAQ

How do I unload?

Return to the depot on the top row. Cores and copper unload automatically.

What do lifts do?

In the second chapter, build a lift from the depot and ride it from a tunnel below that column.

Are diamonds required?

No. Diamonds buy permanent jacket colours. Every shift has a free route to completion.
