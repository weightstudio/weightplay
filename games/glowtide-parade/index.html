Goal

Get enough groups into the lantern shows by the final round. The green counter shows the target for that evening.

How to play

Select a tower and read how many groups would get in. Two-letter passes can go to either tower, while groups already waiting stay at their current tower.

Controls and rules

Tap a tower or use 1–3, then choose Start round. A group marked ! is about to leave if it cannot get in. After evening 30 you can echo between adjacent towers.

Results

The result shows how many groups entered and left. Win to open the next evening, or retry to improve the result. Progress stays in this browser.

News inspiration

On November 13, 2026, New Taipei opened a winter festival by lighting its main display and seven supporting areas. In this fictional festival, lighting a tower changes which paths arriving groups take.

How do I open two towers?
In chapter two, choose adjacent towers after lighting either one in the previous round. An Echo key lets you do this once from cold towers.
What happens when I rest?
Rest restores two battery points. The waiting groups still move one round closer to leaving, so check the forecast.
What do the stars mean?
Reaching the admission target wins. Higher admissions and fewer departures earn more stars.
