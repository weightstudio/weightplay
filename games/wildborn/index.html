How to survive

Walk over food to eat. Slow down in tall grass to hide. Water slows land creatures. Watch for a predator’s warning before its lunge.

Move · WASD / arrows / drag. Strike · Space. Dash · Shift.

One life

Eat and hunt to evolve along related random branches. Evolution saves automatically. Death starts a different small creature with 20% of this life’s earned power and vitality. Inheritance accumulates; there are no stages or final form.
