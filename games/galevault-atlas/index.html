Galevault Atlas

Choose rooms to build a route through the floating archive. Collect chart pages and save enough wind to bring them home.

Game guide

Goal

Explore a floating archive and return its missing chart pages to the dock.

How to play

Tap a connected island. Before crossing a new door, compare the three plans. Leave an exit toward the next page or choose a useful room for the trip home.

Controls and rules

Tap or use arrow keys to move, and select a plan with 1, 2, or 3. Each move spends wind. Wind chambers refill once; stamp presses open stamped gates. In Chapter 2, wait to change phase or spend a local anchor to hold it.

Finishing and feedback

Pages only count once they reach the dock. If the wind runs out, try a shorter route or bring a supply room into your plan. You can replay stages for a better return record. Your progress and optional appearances are saved on this device.

Can I return by a shorter route?

Yes. A junction has more exits and costs an extra wind to build. Connect it to an already built room with a matching door to make a shortcut. A cheaper passage may send you back the way you came.

How do phase gates work?

The phase changes after each crossing. A gate's dawn or dusk mark tells you when it opens. Wait to change phase, or use an anchor from a forge for a crossing.

Do I need diamonds?

No. Every stage can be cleared with the free plans. Diamonds buy permanent door and vault looks in Collection. Changing your look never changes a door, a phase, or your wind supply.
