How to Play

Choose a guest, then slide it along clear directed lanes to the diner counter or a blue waiting bay. Build its pictured order with the available snacks. Serve guests in the posted queue order.

Lane and Order Rules

Guests block occupied stops, one-way arrows cannot be crossed backward, and the number of blue bays is limited. The lane between stops is for travel, not parking. A wrong ingredient is rejected; Undo and unlimited Retry help you recover.

Why the Traffic Theme?

Holiday traffic can turn a simple trip into a long wait. Taro’s fictional diner turns that familiar queue into a routing puzzle: clear the lane, save a small bay, and build each neighbor’s snack order.

Campaign and Save

Thirty authored stages introduce lanes, waiting bays, order choices, shared pantry stock, and one-use gates. Clear a stage to unlock the next; replay any open stage to improve your move record. Progress is saved in this browser.
