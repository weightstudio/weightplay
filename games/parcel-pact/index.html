Goal

Fill every list before sealing its batch.

How to play

Open a crate, pick a good, then tap a neighbor who needs it. Check the next batch before saving or sealing.

Controls and rules

One crate per batch. A storage slot carries goods forward; citrus needs cold storage. A clean return can refund a deposit.

Finishing and feedback

Win by filling every list. Waste nothing and meet the funds goal for three stars.

News inspiration

Some shoppers interviewed during the November 11 online sales event weighed discounts against what they actually needed. The fictional co-op turns that choice into a puzzle about opening, sharing and saving goods.

Player and save information

Solo browser puzzle for touch or mouse. Campaign progress and workshop equipment stay in this browser. No account is needed. Try Shift 3 with the divider tray to make a cheaper second batch possible.

FAQ

Can I buy more than one crate?
Only one crate can be opened per batch. Read its contents before paying.
How do I carry goods forward?
Equip a tool or use the public shelf. Select a spare item and save it before sealing.
How do I earn three stars?
Win by filling every list. Waste nothing and meet the funds goal for three stars.
