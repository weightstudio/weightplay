Your goal

Clear every crate through a dock of the same color and pattern. Each crate has a real shape: its entire cross-section must fit inside the opening. There is no countdown or life limit. Choosing which crate to move first matters more than moving quickly.

Plan a clear route

Tap a crate, then drag along a row or column. You may also tap a destination square after selecting the crate. Keyboard users can focus a crate and move it one square with the arrow keys. Leave a useful gap near the middle: a crate that is closest to its dock may still need to move inward before its neighbor can leave.

Crates, rails and turntables

The first chapter combines posts, rail-constrained crates, keys and shipping lists. A locked crate can still be moved; only its dock stays closed until the key crate leaves. In the second chapter, move a whole crate onto a teal turntable and use the free turning tool. Rotation checks the complete new footprint, so clear enough room first.

A harbor worth returning to

A clear unlocks the next of 60 fixed shipments and saves your best move count. The benchmark is a verified solution, not a claim of the fewest possible moves. Revisit cleared shipments to improve your route or collect chapter stamps. Permanent dock themes change presentation; every board, hint and tool remains free.

Player and save information

Progress and best moves stay in this browser. Clearing a stage unlocks the next one. There is no timer or paid solution.
