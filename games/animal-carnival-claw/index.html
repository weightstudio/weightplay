Turn every drop into a deliberate rescue

Rux built thirty fair skill machines for Starbell Carnival. Nori needs your timing to retrieve the requested memory charms.

How to play

  1. Drag the aiming reticle over a visible target charm.
  2. Release or press Drop when the swaying claw lines up.
  3. Drag against the load swing while it rises to protect the grip.

Rules and results

Each mission gives three free drops and shows every target, weight and grip need. Retrieve the complete target set to clear. No payment, random rarity or real prize is involved.

Thirty carnival missions

Six machines add bumpers, shelves, magnetic drift, fragile stacks and mixed championship layouts. Every clear unlocks the next authored mission.

Practical tips

Start with exposed light charms, watch the sway cone, and make small opposite corrections during the lift. A centered heavy charm needs more steadying than a light one.

Save and privacy

Unlocked missions, medals and cabinet charms stay only in this browser. No account, upload or personal information is required.
