仙旅傳說

Explore distinct regions, read enemy attacks and shape your build. Leo, the lion swordsman, is the launch character; the roster can expand.

Restore the paths

Restore each map’s objectives and challenge regional guardians. After completing the goals, keep hunting or take the marked exit. Optional roaming bosses can appear on every map, with only one active at a time. Story objectives are saved once per map. Revisit cleared maps to hunt without repeating quests or first-clear rewards. Picked-up items go directly to your inventory without combat notices.

How to play

Move with the joystick or WASD. Read the ground warnings and dodge with Space. Auto hunt fights reachable visible enemies, collects nearby loot and returns only when the screen is clear. Manual movement takes priority. Prepare skills and connected talent branches in Character; choose and independently upgrade gear in Equipment.

Controls and rules

Space: dash · F: interact / pick up · E: potion · R: anchored auto hunt · Tab: target · Esc: pause. Automatic skills need a valid target, SP and a ready cooldown.

Local progress

Progress is saved automatically in this browser. Clearing site data can remove it. Sixty authored maps span twelve regions; this internal build is still being tested.
