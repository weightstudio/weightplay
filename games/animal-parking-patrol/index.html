Unjam the forest plaza

Moon Cap Orla studies the crossing from above. Every wooden animal cart can leave only in the direction it faces, so the safest route depends on which paths are already clear.

How to play

  1. Look at the direction shown on each cart.
  2. Tap a cart whose path to the edge is clear.
  3. Clear signal and convoy rules before moving marked carts.

For families

Recommended for ages 9+. The puzzles practise visual scanning and planning; they are not an ability test.
