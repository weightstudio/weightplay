Connect every matching seed without crossing paths. Fill the whole garden to bloom.

How to Play

  1. Choose an unlocked garden from the horizontal Stage rail.
  2. Drag from either colored flower seed to its matching seed.
  3. Keep every path separate and fill every board cell.
  4. Use Undo, Hint, or Reset whenever a route blocks another color.
  5. Complete the garden to unlock the next of 30 stages.

Rules and Controls

Paths move only up, down, left, or right. They cannot cross, share a cell, enter another color's seed, or loop over themselves. Touch, mouse, and keyboard use the same puzzle state; arrow keys extend a selected path.

Connecting every color pair is not enough: every cell must belong to a path. Hint places one correct unresolved route and first clears any drawn route that conflicts with it. Undo removes the latest completed path; Reset restarts only the current garden.

Planning Tips

  • Look for edge seeds and narrow corridors before filling the center.
  • Leave room for long pairs instead of closing short pairs immediately.
  • Backtrack one cell or use Undo before resetting the whole garden.

Campaign

The 30 authored gardens grow from 5×5 boards with four colors to 7×7 boards with six colors. Every puzzle has a verified full-board solution and no timer or losing screen.

Player and Save Information

Recommended for ages 9+ and family puzzle play. Progress is saved only in this browser. No account or advertising is used, and completion feedback is not a formal assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do all cells need a path?
Yes. Connecting every pair is not enough; the complete board must be filled.
Can paths cross?
No. Each cell belongs to exactly one color path.
Are hints limited?
No. Hints do not spend currency. Each hint adds one safe route for a pair that is not yet connected. If all pairs are connected but empty cells remain, Hint cannot fill them; undo or redraw a route first.
Does the game have ads?
No. Color Link Garden is part of the ad-free Kids lobby.
Does progress save?
Yes. Unlocks are stored locally in this browser.
