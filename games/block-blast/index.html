How to play

  1. Inspect all three fixed shapes before placing one on the 8×8 board.
  2. Use the lifted preview to confirm the landing, and preserve long lanes or broad open pockets for larger shapes.
  3. Complete rows or columns to clear them. When all three shapes are used, a new set arrives if the run can continue.

Plan the whole three-shape set before the first placement. Fixed shapes cannot rotate; if none of the unused shapes has a legal position, the run ends.

Stages and progression

Choose the 30-mission Campaign or an Endless score run. Campaign goals change between lines, dirt, ice and stone, colored blocks, gems and locks, and mixed objectives. Some missions limit the number of three-shape sets. Cover a gem to open a lock; ice and dirt clear with a completed line, while stone cannot be occupied.

Planning your next move

Before dropping a small piece into an open area, check whether a larger unused shape needs that space. Lines disappear, but isolated gaps remain awkward to fill. In Endless, wider pieces and a 3×3 square can appear, so avoid dividing every large empty area into narrow pockets.

How the puzzle works

Shapes keep their displayed orientation and do not fall automatically. Clearing a row or column changes the spaces available to the other shapes in the tray. Points come from completed lines, not from placing a shape; simultaneous clears and consecutive clearing moves increase the score.

Player and save information

Drag a shape into place, or select it and choose a board cell. No account is needed. Campaign unlocks, preferences and the Endless best score stay in this browser. Restart begins a new attempt rather than restoring the unfinished board. Clearing browser data can erase these records; another device has its own separate progress.

Frequently asked questions

Can shapes rotate?
No. Every shape keeps the orientation shown in the tray.
When do points increase?
Completed rows and columns award points. Placing a shape without clearing a line does not.
How many stages are included?
The Campaign has 30 missions. Endless continues until the remaining shapes have no legal placement.
What do ice and stone do?
You can place a block over ice; the ice stays until its row or column clears. Stone cells cannot be occupied.
Why did the attempt end?
It ends when no unused shape fits. A Campaign mission can also end if its set limit is reached before the objective is complete.
What is saved?
Campaign unlocks, preferences and the Endless best score are stored in this browser. Clearing browser data may erase them; they do not automatically transfer to another device.
