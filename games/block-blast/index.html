Block Blast — How to Play

Block Blast is an 8×8 spatial puzzle with 30 authored missions and an Endless score mode. Every turn offers three fixed shapes. Place them without rotating, complete full rows or columns, and preserve enough open space for the whole set.

Recommended age9+
DifficultyEasy to Challenging
Play time2–10 minutes
SkillsPlanning · Spatial Reasoning

How to Play

  1. Choose one of the three shapes below the board.
  2. Tap an open cell or drag the shape to a legal fit.
  3. Complete a full row or column to clear it and score.
  4. Use all three shapes to receive the next set.
  5. The run ends when no remaining shape can fit.

Campaign and Endless

Campaign stages add dirt, ice, stone, gems, locks, color goals, and set limits across thirty missions. Endless mode removes the mission objective and mixes small, medium, large, five-cell, and occasional 3×3 shapes so space eventually becomes scarce.

Strategy Tips

  • Keep several regions open instead of filling one corner perfectly.
  • Check all three shapes before committing the first placement.
  • Prepare intersecting row and column clears for larger scores.
  • Do not depend on tiny pieces; large shapes are deliberately part of Endless mode.

Player and Save Information

Block Blast is free to play in a modern phone or desktop browser and does not require an account. Campaign unlocks and preferences are stored only in this browser. Clearing site data or changing devices can remove local progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can shapes rotate?
No. Each tray shape keeps its displayed orientation.
When do points increase?
Only completed rows or columns award points; simply placing a shape does not.
How many stages are included?
There are 30 authored Campaign missions plus an unlimited Endless run.
What do ice and stone do?
Ice remains visible beneath a placed block until its line clears; stone cannot be occupied.
