Beast Guardian - How to Play

Beast Guardian is a 13+ hero tower-defense game. Build ordinary animal soldiers and elite WeightPlay heroes on a tactical forest grid, shape enemy routes, stop boss waves, and protect the crystal core across 10 forest stages.

How to Play

  1. Choose a stage from the swipe stage rail and read the threat, plan, and reward notes.
  2. Spend coins to place soldiers or elite heroes on normal ground tiles.
  3. Keep at least one path open, or blocked enemies will attack the nearest defender until the route reopens.
  4. Upgrade defenders, unlock permanent tech, and use optional diamonds only for safe support choices such as revive, reroll, or cosmetic frames.
  5. Win the wave, collect stars, then use the next-stage action to continue the campaign.

Strategy Tips

  • Use cheap soldiers to shape the path before spending on elite heroes.
  • Moss Shell Taro and Boom Mane Leo are strong blockers, but over-blocking can make enemies attack them directly.
  • Moon Cap Orla and Spark Paw Fia help clear pressure before fast enemies reach the core.
  • Save diamonds for optional revive, reward reroll, or cosmetics; normal stage progress does not require spending.

Parent Note

This game is designed for teenagers who enjoy planning, route control, timing, and resource decisions. Progress is saved locally in the browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Beast Guardian free to play?
Yes. WeightPlay games run in the browser for free.
How do I start a stage?
Choose an unlocked stage from the swipe rail, then begin the defense. Cleared non-final waves start automatically after a five-second countdown.
Does Beast Guardian save progress?
Yes. Stage progress, stars, permanent tech, and optional cosmetics are saved locally in the browser.
What can Diamonds do?
Diamonds are optional and may support revive, reward reroll, or cosmetics. They are not required for normal play.
