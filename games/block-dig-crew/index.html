Block Dig Crew

Sweep over tools, uncover gems and build your own digging crew.

Game guide

Goal

Build your digging crew and uncover gems across five lands.

How to play

Sweep across ready tools to dig, then spend coins on tools and upgrades.

Controls and rules

Hover with a mouse, slide or tap with touch, or focus a tool and press Enter. Each tool moves and cools down independently.

Progress and feedback

Coins are saved when you dig. Revisit unlocked lands or complete a survey to unlock the next one.

Frequently asked questions

Do tools dig while I’m away?
No. Clockwork talents and drills work while you are in the game.
How can I find rarer gems?
Try the Crystal Cavern for sapphires or the Ember Mine for rubies. See each tool’s chances in the Gem bag.
Can I revisit an earlier land?
Yes. Open Lands to revisit any unlocked island. Tools, coins and discoveries follow you.
Should I buy a tool or upgrade one?
Buy a second tool so you can dig while the spoon cools. Upgrade a favorite to earn more per hit and reach its next evolution.
