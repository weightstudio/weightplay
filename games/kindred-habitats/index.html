How to play

Read a species clue, choose its food and habitat feature, then choose a gentle approach during the encounter.

Controls and rules

Tap or click choices. Explore to earn supplies. After another expedition, care for a companion according to its need. Three care points make it an adult. No combat, real money or waiting timer.

Progress and feedback

Correct preparations build trust. A mismatch explains what to change. Collection, growth and permanent upgrades save on this device.
