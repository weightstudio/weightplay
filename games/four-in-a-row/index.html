Four in a Row

Drop discs into a column and connect four horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

GameplayGravity Strategy Game
GenreClassic · Puzzle · Strategy · Family

How to play

  1. Choose a difficulty, then select a column to drop your disc.
  2. Read the highlighted legal move and plan for a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal four.
  3. Use Hint for one tactical suggestion without auto-playing the move.
  4. Reach four in a row before the AI, then use Replay or Main to continue.
