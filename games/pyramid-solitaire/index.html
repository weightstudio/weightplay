Pyramid Solitaire Guide

Turn exposed cards into pairs that total 13. Kings clear alone; the stock gives you another card when the pyramid has no immediate pair.

How to play

Only cards with no uncovered cards resting on them are available. Tap one card and then another card that completes 13, or use the waste card as one half of a pair.

Helpful habits

Free the bottom row first, save flexible ranks, and avoid spending the stock when a useful pair is still visible.
