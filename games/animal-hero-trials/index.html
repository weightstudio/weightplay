Hero Trials

Hero Trials is a 30-trial action campaign with four heroes who differ in health, speed, attack range, and active skills. Tap or drag to move while each hero attacks the nearest enemy in range. Every trial has three rooms: clear the first two to choose a free Power, Cooldown, or Recovery blessing, then face an elite captain or a checkpoint Boss. Victories award Trial Marks, unlock the next trial, and build permanent Heart Mastery.

Tap-and-drag action combatThree-room roguelite trialsTimed hero skills

Move with the shared joystick, click a destination, or use WASD / arrow keys. Space activates your hero skill; Shift or Dodge makes a short protected evade, then recharges for 3.1 seconds. Leave amber warning areas and attack during the green recovery window. Use Heroes & Mastery on the stage screen to change your hero.

World and Mission

Six shadow gates have opened along the road from Rootwood to the Void Crown. Each gate distorts one part of the animal realm: Rootwood sends packs after travelers, Prism Ravine gives wings and shields to its scouts, Ember Forge drives boars into warned charges, Moon Range arms hunters and ravens with distance attacks, Abyss Shell reinforces armored guards, and the final Crown mixes every surviving rule.

Boom Mane Leo, Spark Paw Fia, Moon Cap Orla, and Moss Shell Taro enter the gates as a rotating hero team. A cleared trial stabilizes one step of the road and saves the next card. Every fifth trial is a regional checkpoint. Defeating the Void Crown Emperor in Trial 30 closes the last gate; there is no hidden Trial 31.

How the Systems Work

  • Four combat roles: Leo is a pack breaker: each basic strike cleaves enemies around its target, while his roar disrupts a crowd. Fia is a skirmisher with low health, an invulnerable damaging dash, and the fastest basic attacks. Orla is a long-range hunter whose mark lasts for three empowered follow-up shots. Taro is a bulwark: he takes less damage, slows foes with basic strikes, and can raise a timed guard.
  • Tap or drag to move: On touch screens, tap a point in the arena to send the hero there, or keep a finger down and drag to steer continuously. Arrow keys and WASD remain available on a keyboard. The selected hero automatically attacks a target inside that hero's range; the separate skill button or Space uses the active skill when its cooldown is ready.
  • Three-room runs: Rooms 1 and 2 contain authored enemy combinations. Clearing each opens a mandatory choice of Roaring Power, Comet Rhythm, or Moon Recovery. Room 3 is an elite captain on ordinary trials and a named Boss on checkpoint trials.
  • Enemy rules: Shadow Scouts chase directly. Prism Ravens approach on curved flight. Ember Boars show a warning before charging. Moon Hunters maintain distance and fire periodically. Abyss Guards reduce ordinary damage until hero skills break their guard.
  • Progression: Victories award four to nine Trial Marks and unlock exactly the next trial. Heart Mastery costs five Marks for the first level and four more for each later level; every level gives all heroes 12 additional maximum health.
  • Optional reroll: Between rooms, three free blessings are always available. Spending three Diamonds replaces all three with stronger versions once during that run. The action shows the exact balance change and requires a second confirmation.

How to Play

  1. Choose an unlocked card from the horizontal 30-trial rail and read its region rule, checkpoint Boss, and suggested hero.
  2. Select Leo, Fia, Orla, or Taro. Hero selection and unlocked progress are saved locally.
  3. Move to control distance while automatic attacks target the nearest enemy in range.
  4. Use the hero skill to damage groups, cross danger, mark a priority target, or guard against a heavy pattern.
  5. After Rooms 1 and 2, choose one free blessing. A Diamond reroll is optional and never needed to continue.
  6. Clear Room 3 to earn Trial Marks and unlock the next trial. A defeat keeps all previously saved progress and offers Retry.

Strategy Tips

  • Leo is strongest when several Scouts group inside the same roar radius.
  • Use Fia's dash after a yellow Boar or Colossus warning so the invulnerable movement crosses the charge line.
  • Orla can stay outside most contact ranges; mark an armored or high-health target before the next automatic shot.
  • Taro's guard is valuable against Archowl volleys and the Emperor's late phase, where avoiding every hit is difficult.
  • Skills remove armor faster than ordinary attacks. Save one before a Prism or Abyss guard closes.
  • Power ends rooms sooner, cooldown improves skill access, and recovery protects a damaged run; choose for current health and the next room rule.

Campaign and Difficulty Growth

Trials 1-5, Rootwood: Direct Scout packs teach movement, automatic range, skills, and two blessing choices. Shadow Prowler ends the region with a warned close shockwave.

Trials 6-10, Prism Ravine: Curving Ravens and guarded enemies change target priority. Prism Basilisk alternates protected and open windows; skills break its guard faster.

Trials 11-15, Ember Forge: Boars pause under a yellow warning, then cross the arena quickly. Magma Tusk Colossus resists damage until its charge creates a short opening.

Trials 16-20, Moon Range: Hunters try to hold distance while Ravens change approach angles. Eclipse Archowl slows its pursuit and fires timed volleys across open space.

Trials 21-25, Abyss Shell: Armored Guards, Boars, and Hunters overlap. Abyss Shell Leviathan calls two reinforcements after losing roughly half its health.

Trials 26-30, Void Crown: All five enemy families can appear together. Void Crown Emperor gains a guarded middle phase, then calls Raven and armored support below one-third health while its area pressure accelerates.

Developer Design Note

Each trial keeps the same three-room rhythm so a player can learn one hero without facing an endurance run. The first two rooms create a small build through blessings; the third asks whether that build and the chosen hero answer a visible enemy rule. Difficulty grows through movement shapes, range, warning timing, armor, reinforcements, and Boss phases rather than only larger health totals. The fixed logical Battle canvas scales uniformly across phones, tablets, desktop, and short landscape. Touch and keyboard share the same movement and skill state, page hiding suspends the battle simulation, and Result remains inside Battle above the separate physical reserve. Unlike Animal Auto Squad, this game keeps live movement and skill timing under the player's control.

Player and Save Information

The browser stores the highest unlocked trial, Trial Marks, Heart Mastery level, and selected hero locally. Clearing site data or using another browser may begin a separate save. Diamonds are optional platform currency used only for one confirmed blessing reroll per run. All 30 trials, five enemy families, six Bosses, free blessings, and permanent mastery remain playable without that reroll. Skill feedback is entertainment, not a ranking or formal ability assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many trials and Bosses are there?
There are 30 three-room trials. Trials 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 each end with a different checkpoint Boss.
What happens after the first two rooms?
After Rooms 1 and 2, choose one free Power, Cooldown, or Recovery blessing. These choices last for that trial; Room 3 ends the run.
How do the four heroes differ?
Leo stuns nearby enemies with a roar, Fia dashes through danger, Orla marks targets for stronger follow-up shots, and Taro reduces incoming damage with a timed guard.
Why does an enemy take so little damage?
Abyss Guards, Prism Basilisk, and the Void Crown Emperor can use guard phases. Hero skills break guard faster than automatic attacks.
How do I move and attack?
Tap an arena point or hold and drag to steer. Arrow keys or WASD move on a keyboard; attacks happen automatically when an enemy is in range. Use the skill button or Space when the skill is ready.
What is saved between trials?
Unlocked trials, Trial Marks, Heart Mastery, and the selected hero are saved in this browser. Temporary blessings reset. Diamonds are optional for one confirmed blessing reroll per trial.
What happens after defeat?
Retry starts the same trial again. Permanent unlocks, Marks, and Heart Mastery remain.
