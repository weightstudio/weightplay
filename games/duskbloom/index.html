Your goal

Fill every socket with an amber acorn or a teal sprout. Each row and column needs equal numbers. There is no timer: look for a line that is nearly balanced, or two matching lanterns that force the next one.

Plan each lantern

Tap a cell to cycle acorn, sprout, empty. Arrow keys move focus; 1 places an acorn, 2 a sprout, and Backspace clears. Undo is free. Hints point to a forced cell without filling it. Fixed clues cannot be changed.

Rules and controls

Never put three matching lanterns in a horizontal or vertical line. An = link joins matching lanterns; × joins different ones. Flower seals count acorns inside marked cells. Chapter two adds distant numbered echo pairs. The free lens highlights a pair without solving it.

Finish and return

Fill the board and meet every rule to clear a garden. Contradictions highlight affected cells; change them or Undo. Clears, best moves and unlocked stages stay in this browser. Dusk stone is free, Moss stone is earned at stage 30, and Amber glass is a permanent optional appearance for 5 shared diamonds. Frames do not alter puzzle rules.

Questions

Do frames change the answer?
No. Frames only change appearance. All stages and tools are free.
Where is progress saved?
In this browser. Clearing site data can remove progress. There is no account transfer.
