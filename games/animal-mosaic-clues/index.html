Mosaic Clues

Read the runs, reveal the animal

Solve 30 unique animal nonograms by reading row and column runs, painting certain cells, and marking the gaps that complete each hidden picture.

Puzzle steps

  1. Choose the centered unlocked mosaic on the Stage rail. The first five boards are 5×5; later chapters introduce larger grids.
  2. Read the row clues from left to right and the column clues from top to bottom. Treat each number as a consecutive filled run, not a score.
  3. Paint cells you can prove are filled, mark cells you can prove are empty, and leave the rest blank while you compare crossing clues.

How the chapters grow

The five puzzles in each chapter build on earlier deductions. Woodland Signs starts with 3×3–5×5 single-run clues; River Tracks moves to 7×7 multi-run lines; Moon Garden uses 8×8 patterns and edge deductions. Coral Archive and Sky Atlas use 10×10 boards with separated runs, masks, anchors, and longer crossings. Grand Menagerie brings those ideas together on 12×12 animal portraits. All 30 authored mosaics have one solution.
