Rainline Chase

Pick a gap, time your swing and beat the rain line before play stops.

Cricket batting

Cricket battingRain lineGround

Goal

Reach the rain line and keep a batter in play when the match ends.

How to play

Find the gap, choose a ground shot or a loft, then swing as the dot enters the green band. A ground shot steadies your stance for the next powerful loft.

Controls and rules

Tap or click the field or a sector button. On a keyboard use 1–3 to aim, G and H to choose, and Space to bowl or swing. Wet ground slows a roller; a loft drifts with the wind and can be caught. In the second chapter a field flag can draw a deep defender forward once.

Finishing and feedback

Clear 60 stages across two chapters. One star is for a win, one for winning by two runs and one for no outs. Replay freely; your best record stays saved in this browser.

News inspiration

Rain cut short a cricket match in Wellington on 7 November 2026. India won by an adjusted score. This fictional match borrows the race against rain and uses its own scoring rule.

FAQ

Does a loft always score more?

No. Loft twice in a row and the second is weaker. Wind and a deep defender can turn a loft into an out.

Do I need diamonds?

No. Silver Rain is an optional permanent appearance. Earn the Amber Kit by collecting five stars.
