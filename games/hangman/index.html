Hangman

GenreClassic · Word · Puzzle
Session2–5 minutes

Goal

Crack 60 English words across six themes. Reveal the word before six misses.

How to play

Choose a letter or type on your keyboard. The theme is your first clue.

Controls and rules

One hint reveals a letter, adds one move and costs 25 points. Repeated letters are free. Restart keeps the word. Solved words and best scores save in this browser.

Finishing and feedback

Solve the word to win. Six misses reveal the answer and end the round. Next word continues; Return to Main leaves the round.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Hint reveal?
Once per round, Hint reveals the next unrevealed letter, adds one move, and deducts 25 points.
How many words are there?
There are 60 English words across six themes.
What happens after six misses?
The answer is revealed and the round ends.
Are repeated guesses penalized?
No. Repeating a guess does not add a miss.
What progress is saved?
Your solved-word count and best score are saved only in this browser.
