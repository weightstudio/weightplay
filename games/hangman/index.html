Hangman

Read a hidden word one letter at a time, weigh the theme clue against the six-miss limit, and finish a complete short round.

English word guessingLetter-by-letter cluesSix-miss limit
GameplayVocabulary deduction
GenreClassic · Word · Puzzle
Session2–5 minutes

How to Play

Start a round and inspect the covered pattern.

Choose an unused letter from the visible alphabet. Correct letters reveal every matching position; a wrong letter adds one miss.

Use Hint to reveal the word length and theme. It costs one move, so spend it when the clue is worth the trade.

Solve the word to reach Result. Play again starts a fresh word; Back to main leaves the game.

Reading the Round

The deck has six fixed English answers, with puzzle, habitat, space, place, food, and vehicle themes. The word pattern is visible from the start; Hint reveals the current theme to help narrow the answer.

A duplicate letter is harmless: it stays marked as used and does not add a miss. A loss after six misses reveals the word so the round remains learnable.

Progression and Replay

There is no hidden skill tree or account progression. Each short replay rotates through the authored word deck, so the challenge comes from recognizing a new pattern and theme.

Strategy Tips

Read the visible letter pattern first. If it does not narrow your choices enough, use Hint to reveal the theme; it adds one move but no miss.

Prefer a letter that tests several visible positions when the pattern is still open.

After five misses, favor a high-confidence letter or use the hint rather than guessing broadly.

Design Note

Hangman keeps the classic deduction loop readable: one visible pattern, one theme clue, harmless duplicate input, and an immediate Result. The same round works with the on-screen alphabet or a keyboard.

Player and Save Information

No account or purchase is required. Best results and preferences stay in this browser only. A round is designed for a quick session and can be replayed from Result.

FAQ

What does Hint reveal?
It reveals the word length and theme, and costs one move.
What happens after six misses?
The round ends, reveals the word, and offers a new round or a return to Main.
Does a duplicate letter cost a miss?
No. It is marked as already used without adding a miss.
Does progress save?
Only best results and preferences are saved locally in this browser.
How many words are in the deck?
The authored deck contains six words, each with its own theme and hidden pattern.
