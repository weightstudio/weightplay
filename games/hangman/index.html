Hangman

Read a hidden word one letter at a time, spend a hint only when the clue is worth the trade, and solve a complete short round before six misses finish the drawing.

How to Play

Start a round and inspect the covered word. Choose an unused letter from the visible alphabet; correct letters stay revealed and wrong letters advance the six-miss drawing. Hint reveals the word length and theme, but it costs a move, so use it when the remaining pattern narrows your choice. Duplicate letters are acknowledged without adding another miss. Solve the word to reach Result, then choose Play again for a new theme or return to the main screen.

Player and Save Information

No account or purchase is required. Best results and preferences stay in this browser only. The round is short, the word is fully revealed on success, and a six-miss Result explains the loss before offering a clear retry.

FAQ

Is progress saved?
Yes, only in this browser.
What happens after six misses?
The round ends, reveals the word, and offers a new round or a return to Main.
Does a duplicate letter cost a miss?
No. The status explains that the letter was already used.
