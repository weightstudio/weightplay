Animal Moonlight Heist Game Guide

Animal Moonlight Heist is a 30-mission stealth route adventure starring Spark Paw Fia and Moon Cap Orla. Each mission asks the player to observe moving patrols, preview one deliberate route at a time, recover an archive object, and reach extraction before the Alert meter fills. Optional treasure can earn a third medal and more Moon Coins, but later missions reshape that choice with shadow shelters, ordered seals, shifting markers, clockwork patrol rhythms, warning bells, and six named archive guardians.

The Moon Archive Lockdown

The Moon Archive is a chain of galleries, vaults, courier halls, observatories, and sealed collections that preserve navigation records from across the WeightPlay animal world. A broken eclipse mechanism has pushed its security system into permanent lockdown. Ordinary patrols now treat every moving figure as an intruder. Mirror pedestals exchange the labels on live artifacts, clockwork halls alternate between slow inspection and rapid pursuit, and old extraction gates refuse to open until their seal routines are completed.

Moon Cap Orla plans each entry from the safehouse while Spark Paw Fia enters the archive. Fia is not there to attack the guards. The task is to recover misplaced moon seals, courier tokens, star maps, clockwork lenses, and district relics without escalating the lockdown. Clearing a mission restores one route on Orla's archive map. Passing the Lantern Auditor, Bell Warden, Mirror Keeper, Clockwork Marshal, Vault Sealkeeper, and Eclipse Curator proves that an entire five-mission wing is safe again. Completing Mission 30 resolves the Curator's eclipse route and ends the current campaign; the result does not pretend that a nonexistent Mission 31 is available.

How a Mission Works

  1. Open the horizontal Moon Archive rail and select any unlocked mission. Missions 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 are visibly marked guardian checkpoints.
  2. Read the mission rule and guardian portrait before choosing Lightning Dash, Star Decoy, or Smoke Leaf. Each gadget offer shows its current Level 1 to Level 3 effect.
  3. Hold or drag inside the archive to preview a dashed route. Release to move Fia to that point. On desktop, WASD and the arrow keys provide shorter bounded steps.
  4. Watch the moving sight circles and Alert meter. A patrol that remains close to Fia raises Alert; reaching clear space or entering a cyan shadow shelter lets the meter fall.
  5. Recover the mission object and then reach the active extraction marker. If a rule identifies treasure as the first seal, the mission object cannot be collected until that treasure seal is open.
  6. Choose whether the optional treasure is worth the detour. It awards the third medal and extra Moon Coins, but ordinary mission progress never requires it unless the current rule explicitly turns it into a seal.

Routes, Alert, Gadgets, and Rewards

Movement happens through a series of short commitments rather than one continuous run. The preview line shows the destination and changes color when that point is dangerously close to a patrol, but the player must still judge how long Fia will be exposed while travelling there. Patrols continue to move during planning. Their visible sight circles communicate the current detection distance, including the widening light carried by special guardians. If Alert reaches full, the attempt ends in capture; Retry is always free and returns to the same mission.

Lightning Dash shortens a committed move, making it useful for crossing a patrol line. Star Decoy pauses patrol movement for a level-based duration and is strongest where several routes overlap. Smoke Leaf resets Alert and adds a brief cover window, so it can rescue a run that has already become dangerous. A confirmed three-Diamond reroll changes the three offered gadget strengths. A confirmed five-Diamond insurance policy preserves optional treasure after one capture. These are optional platform choices: neither Diamonds nor insurance unlock missions, guardian encounters, medals, or free retries.

Extraction provides one medal. Avoiding capture provides a second, and returning with optional treasure provides the third. The best medal count for each mission, highest unlocked mission, Moon Coins, and Safehouse level are saved locally. Safehouse level rises as groups of five different missions are cleared. Earlier cards stay replayable, and the Result substate offers Retry, Missions, and Next Mission only when another campaign mission actually exists.

Six Mission Wings and Guardian Rules

  • Missions 1-5 — Entry Routes: Direct paths, crossing patrols, optional treasure, and extraction teach the complete base loop. Lantern Auditor ends the wing with a searchlight whose visible sight circle repeatedly expands and contracts.
  • Missions 6-10 — Shadow Alcoves: Cyan shadow circles hide Fia from patrol sight. Bell Warden gives a visible warning before each pulse; Fia gains Alert if she remains outside shadow when the bell rings.
  • Missions 11-15 — Mirror Galleries: A shimmer warns that the live mission object and treasure are about to exchange positions. False North also requires treasure first. Mirror Keeper shortens the interval, demanding decisions based on the markers' new locations.
  • Missions 16-20 — Clockwork Pursuit: Patrols alternate between a blue slow phase and an amber surge. Clockwork Marshal glows before full-speed pursuit, encouraging players to cross during the readable slow window instead of trying to outrun every guard.
  • Missions 21-25 — Sealed Vaults: Treasure becomes the first of two seals, changing collection order. Crossed paths, shadow shelters, and changing patrol speed are combined before Vault Sealkeeper guards the central route at the checkpoint.
  • Missions 26-30 — Eclipse Mastery: Spotlight, bell, mirror, clockwork, shadow, and seal rules overlap. Triple Lock relocates extraction after the relic. Eclipse Curator combines the full vocabulary, reverses patrol direction after the mission object, and moves the final exit.

Strategy Tips

  • Judge the whole movement line, not only the destination. A safe endpoint can still require too much exposed travel.
  • Use Lightning Dash for crossings, Star Decoy for overlapping patrol paths, and Smoke Leaf when Alert is already high. Equal gadget levels do not make their purposes interchangeable.
  • Treat bell and guardian glow warnings as decisions. Enter shadow before the pulse or pursuit begins, then plan the next route from cover.
  • In mirror missions, watch the shimmer instead of chasing the old marker. Waiting briefly may turn a long dangerous route into a short one.
  • Cross clockwork halls during the blue slow phase. The amber field border and guardian glow indicate that the faster sweep is active.
  • For sealed missions, plan three legs in advance: treasure seal, mission object, and extraction. Missions 29 and 30 relocate the last leg, so preserve Alert capacity for the new exit.

Development and Design Note

We built Moonlight Heist around repeated small commitments instead of continuous joystick movement. Route preview gives touch players time to read the field, but moving patrols mean that waiting still has consequences. Touch, pointer, and keyboard input all operate the same fixed logical play area, and the Stage and Battle canvases keep their geometry across phone, landscape, and desktop viewports. Six five-mission wings introduce one decision language at a time. Each guardian has separate finished character art, a visible warning, a rule unavailable in ordinary opening missions, and counterplay that uses something the player has already learned.

Player and Save Information

Animal Moonlight Heist runs in a web browser without requiring an account for basic play. Mission unlocks, best medals, Moon Coins, and Safehouse level are stored in this browser. Clearing site data or moving to another browser may start a separate profile. Normal progression, all 30 missions, every guardian, and Retry are free. Diamonds are optional platform currency used only for a clearly confirmed gadget-strength reroll or one-capture treasure insurance. The game does not present its planning or focus feedback as a formal ability test.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many missions and guardians are included?
There are 30 authored missions in six wings. Missions 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 use six separately illustrated guardians with different rules and counters.
Why can I not collect the mission object?
In treasure-first seal missions, the treasure marker is the first seal. Collect it before returning to the mission object.
What do the cyan circles do?
They are shadow shelters. Fia is hidden from patrol sight and protected from bell pulses while inside one.
Why did the object and treasure move?
Mirror missions warn with a shimmer and then exchange the two live markers. Their new positions are real, not a decorative effect.
What changes in clockwork missions?
Patrols alternate between a blue slow phase and an amber surge. The field border and guardian glow provide the warning.
Are Diamonds required?
No. Diamonds only reroll gadget strengths or insure optional treasure after explicit confirmation. They do not unlock missions, guardians, medals, or Retry.
Does progress save without an account?
Yes. Unlocks, best medals, Moon Coins, and Safehouse level are stored locally in this browser. Clearing local site data may remove them.
Can Mission 30 be replayed?
Yes. Every unlocked mission remains replayable. Finishing Eclipse Curator ends the current campaign and does not show a nonexistent next mission.
