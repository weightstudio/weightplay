Overview

Chameleon Blend Trials is an observation puzzle about reading a living garden before its scan. Each stage presents a short sequence of colored shapes and habitat patterns, then asks you to rebuild the safe camouflage from memory. The campaign has six authored arcs and a replayable stage 30 finale.

How the garden works

Lock in the target color and pattern before the spotlight scans. A wrong pair or a timeout counts as a missed round. Review the feedback and choose Next Round. After the last round, the results show whether you matched enough pairs to clear the stage.

Progression and difficulty

Across 30 stages, color-and-pattern sequences, changing positions, reverse-order recall, and scan timing recur and combine in authored profiles. Checkpoints are at 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30; cleared stages remain replayable.

Strategy tips

Name the color and pattern aloud during the preview, then group the pairs by order rather than by position. On reverse stages, read the last safe pair first. On three-item stages, keep a small sequence in working memory and lock decisively instead of tapping through guesses. Use Retry to learn a miss without losing your unlocks.

Player and Save Information

No account or purchase is required. Unlocked stages and the local best result are stored only in this browser; clearing site data or changing browsers can remove that record. The spotlight is a game rule, not a safety or environmental assessment.

FAQ

How many stages are included?
