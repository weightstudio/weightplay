How to frame

Use the arrow buttons or keyboard arrows to move one cell. A swipe on the scene also moves one cell. Rotate rectangular frames with the Rotate button or R. Capture with the button or Enter while the scene is focused. Every requested subject must be inside; every excluded subject must be outside. A corner clue means that subject must occupy a corner cell of the crop.

Progress and stars

The album moves from square compositions to rotating panoramas, crowded scenes and corner constraints. Three stars require the ideal move count, no failed captures and no hints. Two stars allow at most one mistake and three extra moves. A hint caps the reward at two stars. Every successful crop earns at least one star. There is no countdown or life limit.

Planning and saving

Compare the target positions before moving; a change of orientation may save several steps. A hint performs the first action of a shortest valid route from your current frame. Completed cards and their best star ratings are stored in this browser, not in a cloud account. Restarting or leaving resets the current attempt but never removes earned stars. Clearing browser data removes the album.

Frequently asked questions

Does this use my camera or photos?
No. It is an illustrated grid puzzle, not a photo editor. The game does not request camera access or image uploads.
How are moves counted?
Every successful one-cell move or rotation costs one move. Moves blocked by the edge cost nothing. Restarting begins a new attempt; replaying cannot lower your saved star rating.
