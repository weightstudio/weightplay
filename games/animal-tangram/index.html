Panko's Tangram Trail

Fit all seven classic pieces into each animal silhouette.

How to Play

  1. Choose an unlocked animal shape from the Stage rail.
  2. Drag a colored piece onto the matching faint outline.
  3. Tap a piece to rotate it in 45-degree steps.
  4. Place all seven pieces correctly to complete the shape.
  5. Finish a shape to unlock the next of 30 puzzles.

Controls and Rules

Use touch or a mouse to drag pieces. Tap or keyboard-activate a piece to rotate it. A piece snaps only when both its center and angle match the target, so every completed animal uses the real seven-piece tangram arrangement.

Puzzle Tips

  • Place the two large triangles first to establish the silhouette.
  • Use corners and straight edges to identify the square and parallelogram.
  • If a piece looks correct but does not snap, check its 45-degree rotation.

Campaign

Thirty authored silhouettes introduce turtles, cats, birds, foxes, fish, rabbits, and increasingly compact arrangements. Progress is saved in this browser.

Player and Save Information

Recommended for ages 9+ and family puzzle play. The Kids game contains no advertising, account requirement, score report, or formal assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Must every piece be used?
Yes. Every puzzle is completed with all seven tangram pieces.
Why did a piece not snap?
Its center or rotation does not yet match the faint target.
Are hints limited?
No. Hint places one unresolved piece correctly.
Can I replay a shape?
Yes. Every unlocked shape remains available from Stages.
