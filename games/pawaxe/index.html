Axe & Ally

Tap to fight through 30 forest routes, collect four companions and 15 equipment pieces, and turn duplicate drops into upgrades.

Controls and combat

Tap the battlefield or Slash to attack. On a keyboard, Space attacks, F toggles Auto, J calls your companion, A/D or the left/right arrows select a target, and Esc pauses. Every eighth slash sweeps the enemies.

Choose one of your collected companions before entering a route. Activate its skill when the energy meter is full; collecting all four does not put four companions into the same battle.

Routes and completion

There are 30 routes: routes 1–10 have 24 waves, 11–20 have 32, and 21–30 have 40. Every fifth route is a boss checkpoint. Later routes bring longer encounters and denser enemy groups.

At 100%, the route is recorded as cleared and the next route unlocks, except after route 30. Tap the completion control to open the result, or keep fighting for more drops. Defeat or leaving does not erase a route already cleared.

Companions and equipment

There are 15 equipment pieces. Drops depend on the current route and chance; reaching an unlock route does not guarantee an item. Duplicate equipment becomes shards. The first upgrade costs 10 shards, and later upgrades cost more. Equip or upgrade in Stage before starting another run.

Player and Save Information

When browser storage is available, completed routes, collected equipment and companions, upgrades and preferences are stored in this browser. An unfinished encounter does not resume after a reload. Clearing site data, changing browsers or using storage that is blocked or temporary can lose progress; there is no cloud-save transfer.
