Your mission

Rux’s woodland market has 30 hand-authored contracts. Fill each order with exactly the requested number of acorns while staying within its token budget and crate limit. Some orders also require a minimum number of golden acorns. Golden acorns are included in the total count; they are not extra. Market tokens belong to each puzzle and are separate from the shared diamond wallet.

Making a trade

Open the market and choose an unlocked contract. Tap the 3D crates or their A, B and C cards to add or remove whole lots. The basket shows your current quantity and total price. You can change your selection freely. Place bid checks the complete basket. An invalid bid explains which condition failed and adds one miss. Three misses close the market for that attempt. Retry restores all lots, prices and bells.

Bells and rivals

There is no real-time countdown. Prices change only when you press Ring bell, up to the displayed limit. Each lot shows its discount per bell. A rival notice tells you exactly which bell will sell that lot; ringing that bell removes it even if you selected it. Consider buying immediately or waiting and combining other crates. A clear basket removes selections without resetting prices, bells or misses. If a necessary lot is gone, return to the contracts and start again.

Progress and stars

The six five-contract chapters introduce single-lot comparison, combinations, golden quality, price changes, packing limits and mixed final challenges. Contracts 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 close each chapter. Clearing a contract unlocks the next. Earn one star for completion, another for no misses, and another for the lowest achievable price across all valid bell timings. Replaying can improve your saved star score and price; a worse attempt never lowers your record.

Your stall

The forest-oak stall is available from the start. Clear 10 contracts to use harvest amber. Moonlight costs 8 shared diamonds and permanently changes the stall’s colours in this browser. Cosmetics do not change prices, answers, stars or difficulty. The stall panel displays your balance and the price before purchase. Every contract is solvable without a purchase.

Controls and saving

Use touch, mouse or keyboard. The three lot buttons are an accessible alternative to the 3D scene; Tab and Enter work on every action. Reduced-motion preferences shorten visual effects, without changing rules. Completed contracts, best stars, best prices and stall colours save locally. An unfinished basket is not saved. The old three-request best-pick record is preserved; a valid completed legacy run receives one completion star for each of the first three contracts. Storage failures are reported and progress continues for the session. There is no cloud save.

FAQ

Does the cheapest lot always win? No: quantity, golden acorns and crate capacity must also fit. Does waiting always help? No: a rival may buy a needed lot. Can I recover from three misses? Yes, retry the same contract with a fresh market. Does leaving erase completed contracts? No, only the unfinished attempt is discarded. What if 3D is unavailable? All three lot buttons and rules still work.
