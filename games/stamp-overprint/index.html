Stamp Overprint

Align the plates, press each color, and keep wet ink from smearing.

Game guide

SimulationTiming

Goal

Fill the order with correctly printed stamps before the shift ends.

How to play

Press as the moving mark enters the alignment window. Match the pressure and plate direction shown on the order.

Controls and rules

Tap the controls or use Space to press, 1 to change pressure, 2 to turn the plate and 3 to fan. Heavy pressure smears wet ink. Wait for the ink to dry or use one of the available fan charges.

Finishing and feedback

Printed stamps go to the stack. A bad impression uses spare paper. Your stars depend on wasted paper and time.

Story behind the game

The Taipei world stamp exhibition opened on November 20, 2026, and featured a rare stamp with an upside-down image. That printing error inspired the plate direction and color alignment in the game.

How do I avoid smearing the ink?

Wait for the first color to dry, use a fan charge or use light pressure when the order allows it.

What does the wide guide do?

After shift 5, equip it to widen the alignment window.
