Circuit Relay Defense

Route power to make cells and fire the turrets protecting your relay.

Game guide

Goal

Route power to make cells and fire the turrets protecting your relay.

How to play

Read the incoming lane. Build a turret and route power to it when drones approach. Send power to the workshop in a break.

Controls and rules

Tap a port to build or upgrade. Tap a circuit, then send the pulse. A shot spends one cell. Armored drones require a coil.

Finishing and rewards

Keep the relay standing through every pulse. A relay without damage earns three stars. Retry freely or equip earned modules between shifts.
