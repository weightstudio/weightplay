Traffic and River Crossing

Deliver lanterns through 30 handcrafted crossings. Read the traffic, ride the current, and find your own route.

How to play

Make one safe step at a time.

Deliver lanterns through 30 handcrafted crossings. Read the traffic, ride the current, and find your own route.

Controls

  1. Use arrow keys or WASD to move one lane.
  2. Swipe or tap the direction pads on mobile.
  3. Watch car lanes and float on a moving log in the river.
  4. Lit outlines show occupied vehicle space. The next-step outline suggests a landing: solid means open, dashed means wait or move sideways.

Stages

  1. 1–5 · Moonlit lanes
  2. 6–10 · River school
  3. 11–15 · Signal district
  4. 16–20 · Lantern post
  5. 21–25 · Island routes
  6. 26–30 · Star harbour

Ride the current and transfer between logs.

Each traffic light controls its own lane.

Collect the cyan-ringed deliveries before reaching the dock.

Use the signal lantern once to stop traffic for 2.4 seconds. Water keeps moving.

Mastery

Medals: clear; collect all 3 lanterns; meet the step budget (1–15) or save your signal lantern (16–30). All medals are optional.
