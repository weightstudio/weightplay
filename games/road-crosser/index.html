Controls

  2. Swipe or tap the direction pads on mobile.
  3. Watch car lanes and float on a moving log in the river.

Crossing rhythm

Stage 1 teaches the route. Stages 2 and 3 add denser traffic, faster water, rocks, and tighter safe windows.
