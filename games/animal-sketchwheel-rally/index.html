World and Mission

Thirty rally courses cross flat road, stairs, mud, tunnels, gaps, ice and wind. The vehicle moves continuously, but progress depends on whether the current drawn wheel suits the upcoming terrain. Each course uses an authored terrain sequence and obstacle position.

How the Systems Work

A round wheel clears flat road; stairs need height; mud and ice need grip; tunnels and gaps check width. Gap clearance requires width; wheel speed affects performance during the crossing, and a strong pass in later chapters can grant a brief boost. Wind checks for a narrow wheel.

A poor match slows, slips, collides or fails the obstacle while the rival continues. Workshop upgrades provide modest speed, tolerance or control support, but no single wheel is correct for every terrain.

How to Play

  1. Choose an unlocked rally and read the terrain sequence.
  2. Draw a closed wheel shape for the first section or choose a blueprint.
  3. Watch the wheel rotate and compare the vehicle response with the terrain cue.
  4. Redraw when the required profile changes and reach the finish before the rival.

Strategy Tips

  • Use a simple deliberate silhouette because decorative bumps reduce measurement stability.
  • For stairs, emphasize vertical height; for tunnels, lower the top while keeping enough width.
  • A wide gap wheel can be poor in wind, so adapt instead of forcing one design through both.
  • Observe which measured trait failed and adjust only that trait next time.

Progression and Difficulty

The first courses isolate flat road and stairs. Later chapters add mud, ice, wind, tunnels, gaps and mixed courses that require several redraws. Thirty authored sequences change terrain order, obstacle spacing, course shape and rival pace.

Developer Design Note

The wheel is visibly attached to the vehicle, rotates with travel, meets the ground line and faces the direction of motion. Draw coordinates and rendered shape share one scale on phone and desktop, so the result of each sketch is readable.

Player and Save Information

No account, purchase or public leaderboard is required. Clears, Forge Bolts, upgrades, Lens state and local best times stay in this browser; site-data changes may create a separate save.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many races are in Sketchwheel Rally?
There are 30 authored races across six chapters, with five races in each chapter.
What wheel works on ice?
Use a grippy profile: the game checks grip for ice. A smooth round wheel is not automatically the best match.
Where is my progress saved?
Race clears, bolts, upgrades, lens state, and best times are saved in this browser. They do not sync to another browser or device.
Can I redraw during a rally?
Yes. Redraw before the next terrain section when its needs change.
Does the rival use my wheel?
No. The rival sets race pressure; your result depends on the shape you draw.
