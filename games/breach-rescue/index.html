Breach Rescue

Open a way through cracked walls and guide stranded birds to shelter before the wind sweeps in.

Game guide

Rescue actionDestructible walls

Goal

Guide every stranded bird to the teal shelter. In later rescues, reach safety before the timer runs out.

How to play

Tap a cracked amber wall to break it. Tap a bird to gather it, then tap the shelter. Intact walls block the wind.

Controls and rules

Tap the yard to move or use arrows and Enter. In wind stages, watch the lit lane and use the Seal button to cover opened gaps.

Finishing and feedback

A clear earns a star. Keep the group together and finish under the target time for the other two.

Do I have to break every wall?

No. The outer paths stay open. A breach helps when the shortcut is worth opening another wind path.

What if a bird falls behind?

Wait for the gust to pass, then tap the bird to bring it back into the group.

How do I unlock new gear?

Clear rescues 5, 15 and 31. Equip the new tools in Gear before entering a rescue.
