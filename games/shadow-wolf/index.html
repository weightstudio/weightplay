Shadow Wolf - How to Play

Shadow Wolf is a 2D Action Platformer RPG. Navigate stone platforms, double jump over spike traps, dash past shadow monsters, and spend two exact attribute points whenever you level up. Cross all eight regions and defeat the giant Behemoth.

How to Play

  1. Use A/D or Arrow keys to run left and right.
  2. Press W, Up Arrow, or Space to jump. Press it again in mid-air to double jump.
  3. Press J or left click to execute a claw attack. Press K or Shift to dash.
  4. Defeat shadow beasts and collect experience until you level up.
  5. Spend both points on Strength, Agility, Constitution, or Luck, using the exact current-to-next preview to shape the run.

Strategy Tips

  • Time your double jumps carefully to avoid landing on spike traps, which deal constant damage.
  • If you fall off a platform, don't panic! You will respawn on the nearest stable ground with a minor penalty of -5 HP.
  • Dash grants brief invincibility frames. Use it to dodge bats shooting red projectiles.
  • Unlock the permanent Mist Amulet from the Diamond Shop to start the platform run with +10 Max HP.

Parent Note

This game helps teenagers practice spatial calculations, real-time motor reflexes, hand-eye coordination, and inventory optimization. All progression metrics are saved locally and are non-clinical.
