Defend the winter wall

Fusekeep is a single-player browser defense game. Enemies march down from the upper portal while animal soldiers fire automatically from nine floor tiles. Choose five of eight troop types, open an unlocked mission card, and hold the wall through fifteen waves. Spend round gold to summon or strengthen your formation.

Same rank, a new random troop

Drag the 3D soldier onto another of the same type and rank. Different types cannot merge: the source tile becomes empty, the target gains one rank, and its new type is selected from your five equipped types with a 20% chance each. Rank 6 is the maximum. Moving to an empty tile is free. Tap source and destination or use Tab, arrow keys and Enter/Space for the same actions.

Build a five-type squad

Foxes fire quickly; bears deal splash damage; owls slow enemies; rabbits chain attacks; wolves ignore armor; raccoons poison; deer boost orthogonally adjacent troops; cats earn wave-end gold. A summon starts at rank 1 and makes the next summon more expensive. Formation upgrades improve all soldiers. Keep enough active attacks before risking a merge that could replace an important role.

Thirty missions, six checkpoint bosses

The chapter contains thirty named missions in six arcs. Entrance patterns, runners, armor, healers, splitting enemies, frozen tiles and limited gold change your decisions. Missions 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 have distinct bosses: charge, alternating armor, summoned helpers, row freeze, gold theft and combined attacks. Defeat a boss before it reaches the wall. Frost Burst damages, slows and interrupts a boss wind-up.

Win, lose and choose your next move

Clear all fifteen waves with wall health remaining, including every spawned descendant and the checkpoint boss. A boss reaching the wall ends the defense. At least 90% wall health earns three stars; 50% earns two; another clear earns one. Frozen tiles cannot move or attack until thawed: move during the warning. Front targets the enemy nearest the wall; Strongest targets the highest current health. The 1x/2x switch changes both sides equally.

Local progress and browser recovery

Unlocked missions, best stars, best times and your five-type squad are stored in this browser. Gold, soldiers and formation upgrades reset for each new battle. Refreshing does not restore an active battle; unavailable storage keeps progress only for this session. Hidden pages pause without time catch-up. Back asks before leaving. Graphics recovery preserves the current simulation until you leave or reload. There is no gameplay account, purchase or advertising slot; shared site services follow WeightPlay policy.

Questions about Fusekeep

Can different troop types merge?

No. Only soldiers of the same type and rank can merge. The new type comes only from your five equipped types, each with a 20% chance. Rank 6 cannot merge.

Does refreshing preserve a battle?

No. Campaign unlocks, stars, best times and squad are saved locally when storage is available, but refreshing resets the current battle.

How do I clear a mission?

Clear all 15 waves and their descendants while keeping the wall alive. On a checkpoint mission, defeat its boss before it reaches the wall.
