How to read the clues

Every lookout gives you two small environmental clues before you make a choice. One clue describes where the sun or moon sits in the canopy; the other describes the breeze that reaches the perch. Read them together instead of treating either sentence as a hidden code. The clues are written in ordinary language so the puzzle rewards careful observation, not memorization. If the sun is on the left and the breeze arrives from the east, look for the compass direction that matches the east-facing route. The written hint is guidance, while the compass and the four direction buttons keep the decision visible.

How to play

  1. Open a lookout and read both environmental clues from top to bottom.
  2. Choose North, East, South, or West on the compass rack, then check the perch.
  3. Use the visible facing label to confirm your choice before checking again.
  4. When the breeze is safe, continue to the next lookout and keep reading the new clues.

The route is intentionally calm. There is no countdown, no life meter, and no purchase gate. Touch, mouse, and keyboard players use the same four-direction decision, so the game remains readable at a small screen and on a larger desktop canvas.

Mistakes and retries

A wrong facing is safe. The game leaves the lookout available, explains that the breeze is not safe yet, and lets you change direction without losing the route. Use Reset direction when you want to return to North and reconsider both clues from the beginning. A miss is useful feedback rather than a punishment: compare the facing you selected with the environmental wording, then try the direction that better fits the breeze. You can also use the arrow keys to make a new choice and press Enter or Space when the compass has focus.

The three-lookout route

One complete session has three authored lookouts. The first introduces the sun-and-wind relationship, the second changes the clue pattern with a crosswind, and the third asks you to finish with a quiet night breeze. Each stage has one intended safe direction, but the route never hides the current target behind a timer or an inaccessible gesture. Complete the first lookout to reach its result card, then choose Next lookout. The Result panel identifies the aligned lookout and keeps the next action close to the play surface.

Results, replay, and saves

After the third lookout, the route result confirms that the canopy path is complete and shows the turn count for the session. Replay starts the route again from the first lookout, while Canopy map returns to the selection surface and Back to start returns to Main. The best turn count is stored only in this browser when local storage is available. Clearing browser data can remove that value. No account, online profile, or network service is required for play, and the saved result does not change the authored clues or the direction rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is progress saved?
Only the best completed turn count is saved locally in this browser. The route can always be replayed.
What happens after a wrong direction?
The current lookout stays open, a calm message explains the miss, and you can choose another direction or reset to North.
Can I play with a keyboard?
Yes. Focus the compass, use the arrow keys to choose a facing, then press Enter or Space to check the perch.
How long is a route?
Three short lookouts form one complete route. There is no timer, and replay is free.
