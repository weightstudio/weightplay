Free every wooden animal

Gear Horn Rux restores wooden animal signs inside the forest workshop. Select a screw, choose an empty hole, and watch unsupported plates drop away.

How to play

  1. Tap a brass screw to select it.
  2. Tap an empty hole to move the screw.
  3. A plate drops when none of its holes still hold a screw.

For families

Recommended for ages 9+. The puzzles practise visual planning and flexible problem solving; they are not an ability test.
