Flip Foundry - Game Guide

Flip Foundry is a swipe-and-land arcade game: launch a crystal canister, read its flight, and tune your next swipe to land upright across a six-chapter workshop route.

World and Mission

Each chapter contains four workshop platforms. A successful landing advances the canister and extends the streak.

The challenge is matching each released swipe to the platform's allowed force range. A miss returns you to that platform so you can adjust and retry.

How the Systems Work

  • Press the canister and drag upward in one continuous gesture. Swipe length changes launch force and flight; the visible rotation makes the flight readable, but landing success is checked against the current platform's allowed force range when you release.
  • A successful landing advances to the next platform and extends the current streak. A miss offers Retry for the same platform or Stages to choose a chapter. Each chapter has four platforms, and clearing all six makes a 24-platform route.
  • Only the best streak is saved in this browser. The current streak lasts only for the active run; no account is needed.

How to Play

  1. Choose any chapter from the foundry map and wait for the ready cue.
  2. Press on the canister and drag upward in one smooth gesture; swipe length changes the launch force and flight.
  3. Release to launch. Watch the rotation for feedback; success depends on whether the released swipe's force fits the current platform's allowed range.
  4. On a miss, use Retry to repeat that platform and adjust your swipe force. Clear all four platforms to finish the chapter; choose the next chapter or replay from the result.

Strategy Tips

  • Start with a controlled upward swipe and use the landing result to learn the current platform's force range.
  • After a miss, change the swipe length a little and compare the next result; distance does not set the success rule.
  • Keep the gesture mostly vertical, then tune force from the platform's feedback.

Campaign and Difficulty Growth

The foundry has six chapters with four platforms each. Every successful landing advances to the next platform and extends the streak; a miss offers a retry on the same platform or a return to the chapter map.

Developer Design Note

Flip Foundry keeps its main decision in one visible gesture: how much upward force to use for the next gap. The same swipe works with touch or mouse, while flight rotation and immediate landing feedback help players connect input to outcome. Four-platform chapters make it easy to practise one section at a time; the result lets a player retry the same platform or return to the chapter map. Best-streak storage stays in the current browser.

Player and Save Information

Only the best landing streak is saved in this browser. The current streak lasts only for the active run; clearing site data may remove the saved best. No account is needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I launch the canister?
Press the canister and drag upward in one continuous gesture; swipe length changes launch force.
What determines a successful landing?
When you release, the swipe's force must fall within the current platform's allowed range. Rotation is visual feedback, not a separate angle check.
Can I use a mouse?
Yes. Mouse dragging and touch swiping use the same launch gesture.
Is progress saved?
Only your best landing streak is saved in this browser. The current streak lasts only for the active run.
How many surfaces are in a chapter?
Each of the six chapters has four workshop platforms; a successful landing advances to the next and a miss can be retried.
What should I adjust after a miss?
Change the swipe length slightly to tune the released force, then compare the result on that same platform.
Can I retry the same platform?
Yes. Choose Retry after a miss to replay that platform, or choose Stages to return to the chapter map.
What does the streak count?
It counts consecutive successful upright landings in the current run. Only the best streak is saved in this browser.
