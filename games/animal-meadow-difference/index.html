Meadow Find

Compare two visible meadow panels and tap the one changed tile in each calm observation round.

How to play

  1. Compare the two visible meadow panels row by row.
  2. Tap the changed tile on the right; wrong taps simply invite another look.
  3. Complete all three rounds to see your checks and replay.

Progress and saves

There is no timer or lives. Your best check count is saved only in this browser.
