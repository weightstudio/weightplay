Place, feel, repeat

Pick a fabric, line up with the golden landing mark, and drop. The transparent cushion shows where the wind will carry your landing. Enough overlap keeps it on the tower; a perfect alignment builds your combo. Watch the alignment percentage as the cushion moves across the workshop. You can wait for the right moment and tap Drop, or drag anywhere in the scene to position it yourself. Release your finger or mouse to place it. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys to aim, the number keys to choose a fabric, and Space to drop.

Three fabrics. Real choices.

Moss felt accepts a smaller overlap and is a friendly place to start. Golden linen becomes narrower and awards a higher base score, so it rewards careful placement. Rose cloud is wider and can preserve a generous surface for the next layer, but it needs more support underneath. Every successful cushion becomes the support for the next one. There is no secret correct answer: the visible width and actual overlap determine whether it stays. Later lookouts request a particular fabric. That request is shown above the tray, and the requested fabric is selected for you.

A trail worth returning to

The full trail contains 30 stages arranged in six arcs, with a checkpoint every fifth stage. Start with the meadow foundations, discover alternating breezes, follow the lantern trail, build narrow cloud spires, fulfill patchwork orders, and finish the sky palace. Each lookout combines a particular height, movement speed, wind direction and landing pattern. The gold mark may move away from the tower center, so look again before each drop. Completing a lookout unlocks the next one. You can browse later cards before they unlock, and replay any cleared lookout to improve its score and stars.

Take your time

There is no countdown and no need to rush. A missed cushion falls away while your settled layers remain. You have three misses within each attempt; after the third, Replay starts a fresh tower. Opening Help, Pause or Settings stops the live challenge, and switching away from the page pauses it too. Continuing resumes the same tower. Returning to the stage map discards only the unfinished attempt, with a confirmation before you leave. Your earlier clears, best scores, stars and owned fabrics remain saved. Sound and reduced-motion settings are respected throughout the game.

Make a landing your own

Every completed tower earns one star. Perfect landings on at least forty percent of its layers earn a second. To earn all three, finish without a miss and place at least seventy percent of the layers perfectly. Consecutive perfect placements add a growing combo bonus, up to five bonus steps. A steady landing still advances the tower, even when it breaks the combo. Replaying never lowers a saved best score or removes an earned star. Progress is stored in this browser; existing cleared stages from the earlier game are kept, while the new stars begin as fresh challenges.

A small fabric collection

The Fabric studio is on the stage map. Garden fabrics are free and always available. The optional Moonlight palette costs twelve diamonds from your shared WeightPlay wallet and is a permanent collection item. Review the price and confirm once to unlock it, then switch between owned palettes whenever you like. Colors do not change cushion widths, support rules, scores or stage access. The studio shows your balance and ownership. If storage is unavailable, paid purchasing is disabled and the game explains that new progress cannot be saved; the free challenge remains playable.
