Animal Habitat Mahjong - How to Play

Match identical animal tiles that are uncovered and open on at least one side. Clear 15 layered boards to explore forest, safari, ocean, and arctic habitats.

How to Play

  1. Find two identical animal tiles.
  2. Choose tiles with nothing above them and at least one open side.
  3. Use Hint, Undo, or Shuffle when you need help.
  4. Clear every pair to unlock the next habitat board.

Parent Note

This calm, ad-free Kids puzzle supports focus, logic, planning, and problem solving. Best scores and progress stay on this device.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many boards are included?
There are 15 layered habitat boards.
What makes a tile free?
A free tile has nothing covering it and has an open left or right side.
Is there a timer limit?
No. Time is recorded only for your local best result.
