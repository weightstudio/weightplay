Breakout

Clear a compact field of twelve bricks by reading the highlighted lane, moving the paddle, and choosing when to serve.

GameplayClassic Arcade
GenreClassic · Arcade · Action
Session2–5 minutes

How to play

  1. Start a round and read the highlighted brick and the current target lane.
  2. Use Left and Right to align the paddle with the target column.
  3. Press Serve to spend one shot; an aligned shot clears its brick and raises the score.
  4. When a column is clear, follow the next-target direction cue before serving again.
  5. Clear all twelve bricks, then use Play again or return to Main to begin another round.

Progression and difficulty

The short route stays focused on aim, timing, and readable consequences. Missed serves still count as shots, while cleared bricks remain cleared, so careful lane changes improve the rematch goal.

Practical tips

  • Do not auto-serve after clearing a lane; the next cue may ask for a different column.
  • Use the highlighted target as advice, while keeping control of every paddle move and Serve.
  • Check the Result shot count to decide whether to replay for a cleaner clear.

Player, device, and save information

Touch, mouse, and keyboard controls use the same lane decisions. Your best result and sound preference stay in this browser; no account or purchase is required.

FAQ

What does a highlighted brick mean?
It marks the current legal target for the paddle lane.
What happens after a missed Serve?
The shot count increases without clearing a brick, and the status explains the next route cue.
Is progress saved?
Best results and preferences are saved only in this browser.
