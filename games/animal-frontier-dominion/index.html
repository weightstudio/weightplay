Explore. Expand. Secure the frontier.

Choose each neighbouring territory carefully. Scouting reveals danger, formations counter enemy roles, and captured sites provide the supplies needed for the next march.

How to play

  1. Choose an adjacent territory.
  2. Scout its hidden modifier or save command points.
  3. Choose the formation that counters its defender, then march.
