FAQ

How many pictures are included?
There are twelve original animal pages.
What is the difference between Fill and Brush?
Fill colors one enclosed region; Brush draws a free stroke inside the artwork.
Can I undo or redo an edit?
Yes. Undo and redo are available for recent changes.
Does Clear erase the current picture?
Clear restarts the page after you confirm.
Where are saved pictures stored?
In the gallery on this device, in the current browser.
Does the game upload my drawings?
No. The game does not upload pictures or require an account.
