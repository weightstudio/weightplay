River Rescue

Read the currents, tow stranded crews, and bring them back to a safe dock.

Game guide

River RescueTowing in currents

Goal

Tow the required rescue floats into a safe dock before time runs out.

How to play

Move near a float, attach the line, then steer back to a dock. A second float makes the boat slower to turn.

Controls and rules

Point on the water or use arrow keys. Tow near a float, then unload at a dock. Brace helps in crosscurrents.

Finishing and feedback

Slow down in the dock ring and unload. An intact hull and an efficient rescue earn extra stars.

What if the rope comes loose?

Slow down, steer back to the float, and reconnect the rope from its ring.

How do I unlock rescue gear?

Clear Rescues 5, 15 and 30. The workshop shows each tool and lets you switch before a run.
