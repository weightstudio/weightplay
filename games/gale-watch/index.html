Gale Watch

Move your crew into cover, power the workshop and scatter wind spirits.

Game guide

Goal

Move your crew into cover, power the workshop and scatter wind spirits.

How to play

Tap a teammate, a lit tile to move, or a spirit in reach to attack. Opponents act after each order.

Supplies and cover

Work on a blue bench to charge two cells. Spend two cells on a strong gust, shelter panel or stalled bench.

Clearing a shift

Scatter at least half the spirits and keep the beacon lit. Scatter them all without damage to the beacon or losing a teammate for three stars.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get more cells?
Move to a blue workbench and tap Work. The enemy gets a turn while you recharge.
Do I need diamonds to win?
No. Shifts and earned tactical gear are free. Diamonds buy a permanent beacon color.
