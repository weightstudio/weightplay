Silhouette Scout

Match each dawn outline to the right animal card and light the lookout.

GameplayOutline recognition
GenrePuzzle · Observation · Family · Animal

How to play

  1. Study the outline and the clue.
  2. Tap the matching animal card.
  3. A wrong pick clears gently; no timer or lives are used.

Results and saves

Three lookouts form one short session. The best total checks is stored only in this browser when storage is available.

Practical tips

Look for ears, tails, and body shape. The full-colour cards are visible, so this is recognition rather than hidden memory.
