Your goal

Restore the whole picture. Correct neighbouring fragments join into a group that can move together. There is no timer or life limit.

Move and connect

Tap a fragment, then its destination, or drag it. A group keeps its shape and swaps with displaced fragments. Single piece lets you separate a fragment. Arrow keys move focus; Enter selects, R rotates, M mirrors and U undoes.

Direction and anchors

Rotate turns a selected piece clockwise. Gold-pinned pieces stay fixed. From stage31, Mirror reverses a selected fragment horizontally. A seam joins only when both pieces have the correct direction.

Collect and return

Every valid clear unlocks the next stage. Each set of five adds its picture to your permanent gallery. Replay for a better move record. Progress stays in this browser; clearing site data removes it.

Opal is a permanent appearance-only frame for8 shared Diamonds. All puzzles, hints and tools stay free.

Questions

Can a large group get stuck?
Use Single piece to separate one fragment, or Undo to restore the previous arrangement. Neither costs anything.
Do frames or hints change the puzzle?
Frames change appearance only. Hints suggest one reversible action; they do not move a piece or block progress.
