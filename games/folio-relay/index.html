How to play

Lane switching game

Delivery goal

Switch tracks to catch the next folio. Use the repair shelf when the shuttle heats up.

Controls and rules

Use the three track buttons (keys 1–3) to position the shuttle before the folios arrive. From shift 6, press Space or tap the switch to use the repair shelf.

Choose the shelf for a torn book or when the shuttle is hot. A stored book counts when the repair ends.
