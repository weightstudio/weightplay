Turn the tower. Read the landing. Keep the fall alive.

Fia is sealed inside a protective comet sphere while descending thirty unstable skyspires. Drag left or right to rotate every ring. Pass through openings, bounce on safe turquoise stone, and avoid violet cursed sectors.

How to play

  1. Drag across the tower or use Left and Right to rotate the rings.
  2. Place an opening beneath Fia before her next landing.
  3. Fall through consecutive rings to charge Comet Break and smash one danger.

Thirty different descents

Six chapters introduce fragile glass, drifting rings, reversed controls, charge crystals, shield gates, and Crownfall towers that combine every rule.

Reading the spiral

A short safe bounce creates time to rotate. Long falls build power, but the next platform arrives faster. Decide when to aim for a gap and when to land deliberately.

Save and privacy

Stars, best times, shards, and upgrades stay in this browser. No login or personal information is required.

FAQ

How many skyspires are there?
There are 30 authored skyspires across six chapters.
What charges Comet Break?
Passing through consecutive openings builds the charge. A full charge can break through one danger.
Does progress save?
Yes. Cleared towers, stars, best times, sky shards, and Comet Forge upgrades save locally.
Which controls and screen sizes are supported?
The same rules support touch, mouse, and keyboard where the game uses those inputs. The interface scales as one logical layout across the required phone, landscape, and desktop viewports.
Can progress move automatically to another device?
No. This game currently saves to local browser storage only, so another browser profile or device begins with its own separate local progress unless a future account system is added.
