Harbor Crane

Lower cargo onto a moving barge and balance the load before it sails.

Game guide

Crane gameCargo balance

Goal

Load the barge and keep it level so it can leave the harbor.

How to play

Steer the trolley over a cradle, match the landing marker, then release. Later shifts add wind and a rising fender.

Controls and rules

Hold ← or → to steer. Release with Enter when the marker lines up with an open cradle. Spread the weight across the deck. Press C to use your tool.

Finishing and feedback

A falling crate uses a retrieval. Finish with a stable deck to open the next shift. Replay to improve your stars.

How do I land a crate safely?

Slow down and release when the landing marker is over an open cradle.

How do I level the boat?

Split heavy crates between cradles. A low side leaves less space for the next crate.

When can I tie up the barge?

From shift 31, use the mooring line as the barge passes near the pier.
