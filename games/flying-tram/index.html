How to play

  1. Hold Boost to lift off and add a little height during the flight.
  2. Hold left or right to tilt. A full flip scores points, but strong spinning makes the tea slosh.
  3. Counter-steer to steady the tram. At the rooftop pad, keep it nearly level and slow for a safe landing.

Rules and results

Each run lasts about 45 seconds. A safe landing unlocks the next route and saves your best score on this device. Spilling the cup or reaching the pad at a dangerous angle ends the run.

The tram can fly; the bubble tea still needs a steady hand.

Rooftop routes

Start on the calm Launch Roof, then try Crosswind Market and the narrow Cloudline Pad. Each route changes the wind and landing window.

Skills practiced

Timing, focus, and hand-eye coordination through short playful practice. Progress is stored locally on this device.

Frequently asked questions

How long is one flight?
About 45 seconds, depending on the route.
How do I flip?
Hold left or right long enough to complete a rotation.
Why is the cup shaking?
Sharp tilts and fast spins increase the spill meter.
How do I stop a spin?
Hold the opposite direction to counter-steer, then release.
What counts as a safe landing?
Reach the pad with a nearly level tram, gentle vertical speed, and tea still in the cup.
How do I unlock routes?
Land safely to unlock the next rooftop route.
Is progress online?
No. Your best scores and unlocked route stay in local browser storage.
Is this based on a real vehicle?
No. It is an original fictional arcade game.
