Clockwork Boxes

Turn the rings, follow the engraved clues, and open layered boxes.

Game guide

Mechanical PuzzleDeduction

Goal

Turn the rings, follow the engraved clues, and open layered boxes.

How to play

Turn each ring until its pale pointer meets the brass notch. Look on the other faces for missing marks and hidden tools.

Controls and rules

Tap the rings to turn. Press a ring and use Left or Right to turn either way. On touchscreens, swipe the bench or use Turn box to visit another face. Undo restores the last changed part.

Completion

Once the locks are clear, pull the lid. Earn stars by solving with fewer moves and without a direct hint. Reopen any box for a better score.

Frequently asked questions

How can I recover from a mistake?
Use Undo to restore the preceding state, or Restart box in the pause menu. There is no timer.
Where are the missing marks?
Turn the box with the bottom control. Later puzzles use a mirror or wheel found inside the box.
