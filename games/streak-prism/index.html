Streak Prism

Turn the prism to catch incoming starlight, then find a cooling dock before the next streak.

Game guide

Reaction gameLight-catching

Goal

Catch the number of streaks shown at the top of the observatory.

How to play

Move the prism to the glowing socket before the streak arrives. Use blue docks to cool it and move away from striped shutters.

Controls and rules

Tap a socket or drag around the ring. On a keyboard, press the arrow keys or 1–8. The violet shutter delays the prism if you are in its path.

Finishing and progress

Meet the goal to open the next observation. Earn new prisms at observations 5, 20 and 35. Catch extra streaks to earn more stars.

News behind the game

Taipei Astronomical Museum expected the Leonid meteor shower to peak on November 18, 2026. It recommended a dark sky before dawn. The game's prisms and light-catching observatory are fictional.

How do I cool the prism?

Leave it on the blue dock until the heat meter falls.

When is the split prism useful?

After observation 5, equip it to catch adjacent rays from the same pair.
