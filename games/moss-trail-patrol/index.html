Moss Trail Patrol

Build along a forest trail, fight through encounters, and use the shards you earn to prepare the road ahead.

Game guide

Goal

Defeat the marked foes and return to camp.

How to play

Spend shards on the next stop before you walk. Camps heal and lanterns open a fight.

Controls and rules

Tap Walk trail to advance. When a foe winds up, Brace, then Strike to counter. Keys T, G and F do the same.

Finishing and feedback

Defeat enough foes, then return to camp. Spend shards from each foe on the stops ahead.

Questions

Can I move a marker?
Each stop can hold one marker for the current trail. Retry to change the layout.
How do I get gear?
Finish trails 5, 15 and 31, then equip a kit from Gear.
