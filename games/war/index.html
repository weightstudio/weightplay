War

A two-player reveal-and-collision card game: compare ranks, collect the pot, and resolve ties with a War.

Deck52 cards · 26 per side
Core loopFlip → compare → collect
RoundOne player takes every card

How the round works

The deck is split into 26 cards for you and 26 for the AI. Each Flip reveals one card from both sides. The higher rank wins the whole pot; the suit does not matter. A round ends when one side has no cards left.

Resolving a War

Equal ranks trigger a War. Press Flip again to place three cards from each side into the pot, then reveal the next pair. If a side has fewer than four cards when the War begins, the side with at least four cards takes the pot; the result then ends the round.

Strategy and replay

War is mostly a probability-and-patience game: the visible pot shows what is at stake, while the remaining card counts show who is under pressure. A War can swing a round because the winner receives every card in the pot. Use Restart for the current deal or New Game for another deal.

Player and Save Information

War is free to play in a browser with no account or purchase. The current deal, sound preference, and round records stay in this browser; clearing site data or changing browsers can remove them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What wins a normal reveal?
The higher rank wins the pot; suits are ignored.
What happens on a tie?
Three cards are placed from each side, then another pair is revealed.
When does the round end?
The side with no cards left loses; the other side takes the final result.
Can I control the card choices?
No. War is a reveal-and-collision game; your decision is when to Flip and continue a War.
Is progress saved?
Local deal records and preferences may stay in this browser only; no account is required.
