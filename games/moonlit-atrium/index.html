Your goal

Restore Orla’s moonlit conservatory by lighting every floor. A lantern illuminates its own floor and straight lines until a pillar or board edge. No two lanterns may shine on each other. Pillar numbers count only lanterns directly above, below, left and right; diagonal lanterns do not count.

Plan the light

Tap a floor to place or remove a lantern. Use Mark for a reversible empty note, or right click. Start with a zero pillar: none of its neighbouring floors can hold a lantern. Then look for a dark corner with only one possible light source. Undo and hints are free; there is no timer or life limit.

Rules and controls

Arrow keys move focus; Enter or Space places a lantern, X marks and Z undoes. Glass cannot hold lanterns. Shutter floors restrict their own lantern to one axis. Chapter two adds paired flower pedestals: exactly one endpoint needs a lantern. Its free Branch Pin stores one hypothesis for Recall without revealing the solution.

Finish and return

Check highlights a broken rule without clearing your work. A correct board finishes lighting before Result; choose Stages, Next or Replay. Clears, unfinished boards and best edit counts save locally. Storage failure is shown and can be retried. Celadon is earned at stage 5; the optional 8-Diamond Amber lantern is permanently owned through the shared wallet. No appearance changes difficulty.

Questions

Can light beams overlap?
Yes. Several lanterns can light the same floor, but no lantern may stand in another lantern’s beam. A one-axis shutter does not make that conflict safe.
Do hints or appearances change the puzzle?
Hints are free and mark the attempt as assisted. Silver, earned Celadon and the permanent 8-Diamond Amber lantern change appearance only.
