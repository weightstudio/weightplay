Read the opening, fit the shelter

Every short set has one shape match and no timer.

1. Read the opening

Study the filled cells in the burrow opening. Read from the top row to the bottom row, notice the empty spaces around the shape, and treat the outline as one complete little shelter. There is no timer, so pause whenever you need to compare a corner, a short edge, or the direction of a notch.

2. Compare pieces

Choose the shelter piece with the same outline and orientation. Compare every occupied cell rather than guessing from the overall size. A piece can look close while one turn, missing corner, or extra cell makes it wrong. Move your attention around the full perimeter, then check the centre and the narrow edges before committing to a choice.

3. Check gently

A miss stays safe to try again; three fits complete the set. Select one piece, check it, and use the feedback as a calm reading cue. A wrong answer never removes progress or adds a penalty. Reset the choice when you want a clean comparison, then return to the opening and inspect the cells that made the two shapes different.

4. Make a clean match

The best way to solve a round is to build a small visual checklist. First count the filled cells. Next compare the longest edge, the widest row, and the most distinctive corner. Finally confirm that the empty cells line up too; matching filled cells alone is not enough when two pieces share a similar outline. The three rounds use different shelter shapes, so let each opening speak for itself instead of carrying a guess from the previous round. If you change your mind, choose another piece before checking. The interface keeps the decision reversible and the board readable at every step.

5. Finish the set

Hollow Fit is a short, quiet session of three authored openings. After a correct match, the next opening appears automatically and the round counter shows where you are in the set. Your check count records the confirmations you made, including a safe retry, so it is a gentle measure of careful reading rather than a race. When all three openings fit, the Result screen summarizes the session and offers Fit again or Back to menu. Fit again starts at the first opening with a fresh selection, while Back to menu returns to the Main Guide and shared Settings.
