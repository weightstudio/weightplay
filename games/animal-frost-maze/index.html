World and Mission

A small rescue route is sealed by blue ice walls. Collect every berry, change only the barriers that matter, and keep moving before the snow drifter closes the gap.

How the Systems Work

  • Arrow keys, WASD, or the visible direction controls move one grid cell at a time. Space breaks the ice ahead and F builds a short wall in a valid open cell.
  • The snow drifter moves after every eight successful grid steps. Contact ends the room. Collect all berries; in rooms 26–30, return to the gold starting tile after the last berry.
  • In rooms 21–30, every successful break or build also moves the drifter once. Blocked movement and invalid edits do not.

How to Play

  1. Choose a chapter and read the berry count and route hint.
  2. Move one cell at a time and use Break Ice when a blue wall closes the next useful lane.
  3. Use Build Ice to create a short barrier when the enemy needs to be delayed.
  4. Collect every berry, then choose Next Room, Retry, or return to Chapters after the result.

Strategy Tips

  • Plan the next two cells before breaking a wall so the opening creates a real route.
  • Keep the enemy on the far side of a barrier while collecting the exposed berry first.
  • If the route closes, retry quickly and change the order of your breaks instead of repeating the same path.

Campaign and Difficulty Growth

The 30 rooms form six chapters. Rooms 1–20 teach route planning as the drifter advances every eight successful steps. In rooms 21–25, each successful wall edit also advances it; rooms 26–30 keep that pressure and require a return to the gold starting tile after the last berry. A clear earns one star; meeting that room’s edit target and step target can earn one more each. Missing a target does not block a clear.

Developer Design Note

The maze makes wall editing a route choice with a cost: breaking ice opens a lane, while building it redirects the drifter. The eight-step pursuit rhythm is easy to read, then rooms 21–30 make every successful edit part of the timing. The final five rooms add a visible home tile, so collecting the last berry is not always the final move.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to collect?
Collect every berry in the room. In rooms 26–30, reach the gold starting tile after collecting the last berry.
When does the snow drifter move?
It moves after every eight successful grid steps. From room 21 onward, each successful break or build also triggers one move; blocked steps and invalid edits do not.
What does Build Ice do?
It places a short wall in the open cell ahead, when that space is valid, and changes the drifter’s route. In rooms 21–30 the successful edit also moves the drifter.
What changes in rooms 21–25?
Every successful ice edit now advances the drifter, so breaking or building a wall changes both the maze and the pursuit timing. Invalid edits leave it in place.
How are room stars awarded?
Clearing earns one star. Meet the room’s edit limit and its displayed step target to earn up to two more. Rooms 1–20 allow four edits for that target; rooms 21–30 allow two. A missed star target still counts as a clear.
Which rooms require a return to the start?
Rooms 26–30. Collect every berry, then move back to the gold starting tile to clear the room.
What progress is saved?
The best cleared room and best star result for each room stay in this browser. Clearing site data removes them; they are not a cross-device save.
