Your expedition

Drive an original rescue truck through six mountain regions. Transport supplies, tow stranded vehicles, repair bridges and control floodgates across 60 authored missions.

How to drive

Use the arrow keys or WASD to drive and steer. On touch screens, drag on the playfield. Stop at a marked station and use Interact. Switch gear with L or the low-gear button, and attach a nearby winch with Q.

Terrain and cargo

Mud, snow and water reduce traction. More cargo costs energy and slows acceleration. Low gear helps difficult crossings. Road tyres, mud tyres, chains and crawler tyres each suit different routes.

Finishing and rewards

Complete every objective to finish a mission. Preserve 75% vehicle and cargo condition for a second star; finish within the mission target without recovery for the third. Running out of energy, condition or cargo integrity ends the attempt. First clears award parts. Improve workshop research, unlock equipment through missions and replay for better stars. Optional diamond liveries change appearance only. Mission clears and equipment remain on this browser. Leaving a live mission loses that attempt. Base camp, the quarry and the ridge shelter provide charging. Recovery returns the truck to its last safe road for 10 energy.

Expedition questions

Is there a time limit?

No. Time affects only the efficiency star. You can plan routes and wait for safe water.

Do I need diamonds to finish?

No. All driving equipment and missions are available through normal play. Diamonds buy only an optional livery.

How do I get out of mud?

Use low gear, pull toward a nearby anchor with the winch, or choose Recovery from the driving guide.

What changes after mission 30?

The second chapter adds changing water levels, pump stations and deployable anchors.

Why are later missions locked?

Complete each mission to open the next. Previous clears and stars remain saved.
