How to play

Read the note and spot the support order.

Tap stones from the ground up.

Check the visible rules. A wrong stack stays in place: undo a stone and try again.

Trail contract

Load = each inner stone’s weight × its height (1, 2, …). Keep heavier stones low.

Mastery

Earn three mastery seals by solving a stage without mistakes or undoing. A repaired solution earns two; a solution after a failed check earns one. Replay freely to improve your best.

Questions

Is my trail saved?
Completed stages, best picks, mastery and owned finishes are saved in this browser. Clearing browser data removes this progress.
Do I need diamonds to play?
All 30 stages, retries and Undo are free. Alpine morning unlocks after 10 stages; Aurora lantern is an optional permanent appearance for 12 diamonds.
