Balance Grove

Choose stones whose values match the target in 30 forest bridge puzzles. Later stages require exact counts, specific stones or leaving certain stones off the scale.

Subset-sum puzzleWeight-balance puzzleStone-selection puzzleCasual logic game

Mission

Match the number shown on the scale. Later bridges require a specific number of stones, a particular stone, or leaving a stone off the scale.

Play loop

Tap or click stones to select a non-empty set, then choose Check. A wrong total clears the set safely so you can try another combination.

30 bridge puzzles

The first three bridges ask for totals of 5, 7 and 9. Stage 4 changes the target to 6, and later stages add rules about which stones you use.

A quick example

On the first bridge, the target is 5. Stones worth 1 and 4 make 5. Stones worth 2 and 3 also work.

A quiet design

There is no timer or lives system. Each check shows whether the selected values match the target; an incorrect selection clears for a fresh attempt.

Player and Save Information

Your progress and stars stay in this browser. The best-check record counts the first three bridges completed in one session.

FAQ

What totals do the bridges need?
The first three bridges ask for totals of 5, 7 and 9. Stage 4 changes the target to 6, and later stages add rules about which stones you use.
What are the stone values?
Acorn is 1, pebble 2, twig bundle 3, berry basket 4, shell tile 5, and firefly jar 6.
What happens after a wrong check?
The selected set clears safely, so you can choose a different non-empty set and check again.
Is there a timer or lives limit?
No. The game has no timer or lives system.
What is saved?
Your progress and stars stay in this browser. The best-check record counts the first three bridges completed in one session.
