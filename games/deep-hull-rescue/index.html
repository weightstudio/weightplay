Deep Hull Rescue

Assign your crew to seal leaks, repel reef beasts and tow stranded rescue pods to safety.

How to play

Goal

Tow the stranded pod to the exit. Repel any reef beast that blocks the crossing.

How to play

Read the warning, choose up to two jobs, then run the shift. Pump out flooded stations to bring them back into service.

Controls and rules

Tap a compartment to direct patching, pumping or doors. The pulse costs two power. Switch depth to avoid opposing currents or exposed armor.

Finishing and feedback

After towing, reach the exit with your hull intact. Earn more stars by keeping four hull points and meeting the shift target.

Questions

When should I close a door?
Close a door to stop water spreading into a compartment you still need. Open it if you want water to flow toward the pump.
Can I clear every mission with the standard submarine?
Yes. Earn optional equipment by completing missions, then try it on an earlier route from the mission map.
