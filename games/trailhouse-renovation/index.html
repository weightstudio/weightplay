Trailhouse Renovation

Salvage scrap, fix the floor and furnish cabins while keeping the paths clear.

Game guide

Goal

Restore the cabin and leave a route to the furniture your guests need.

How to play

Recover scrap, repair worn boards and furnish the room.

Controls and rules

Tap a tile or focus it with the arrow keys and press Enter. Turn long furniture to leave room in front.

Finishing and feedback

Inspect the paths and fix any blocks. Efficient work earns three stars.
