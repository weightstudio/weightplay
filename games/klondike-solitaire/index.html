How to play

  1. Select a face-up card, then drag it or tap a highlighted destination. Build tableau runs down in alternating colors; only Kings can enter empty columns.
  2. Move each suit to its foundation from Ace to King. Reveal face-down cards by clearing the cards above them.
  3. Tap the deck to draw. Draw 3 exposes three cards, but only the top waste card is playable.

When you get stuck

Hint shows a legal move without playing it. Undo reverses your move. Auto Finish handles safe endgame moves; Restart retries the same deal, New Game deals another.
