Prism Route Garden

How to Play

Connect every matching prism without crossing paths. Prism gates accept only their own color, and every open tile must be filled.

PuzzleStrategyRoute planning

How to play

  1. Drag from a crystal flower to its matching color.
  2. Move up, down, left or right. Routes cannot cross or share tiles.
  3. A crystal gate accepts only the color shown by its border.
  4. Connect all pairs and fill every tile to unlock the next garden.

Planning tips

  1. Reserve narrow edges and gated corridors before filling the center.
  2. Trace longer pairs first when they divide the board.
  3. Use Undo or Hint without losing campaign progress.

Undo restores the previous board; Reset clears your routes. Hint completes one unfinished route. Progress is saved in this browser.

Advanced campaign

Thirty authored layouts expand from 7×7 to 10×10, introduce more color pairs and add prism gates in six chapters.

Frequently Asked Questions

What completes a garden?
Connect every matching pair and fill each open tile without crossing paths.
Can routes cross or share a tile?
No. Routes move orthogonally and each tile can belong to only one route.
How do prism gates work?
A gate can be crossed only by a route matching the color on its border.
What does Hint do?
Hint completes one available unfinished route; it does not choose every route for you.
Is my progress saved?
Unlocked gardens and progress are saved in this browser only, when local storage is available.
