Online box-pushing puzzle: plan a route that stays open

Move one square at a time, push each rune crate onto its dock, and keep a route open for Rux. Thirty warehouses across six chapters add ice rails, signal gates, linked crates, and one-way storm belts. Undo or restart a plan; progress stays in this browser.

Three checks before your next move

  1. First warehouse: move down once, then right three times. The first step gets Rux behind the crate; the next three push it onto the dock. Use this small example to separate walking from pushing.
  2. On an ice rail, check where the crate will stop, not just the next square. Storm belts move Rux when he steps onto them; do not assume that a belt carries the cargo in the same way.
  3. For linked cargo, inspect the destination of every linked crate before pushing. One blocked crate can stop the whole group, even when the space directly in front of Rux looks clear.

Why can a magnetic pull fail even with a charge left?

Rux must be beside the crate, with a free square behind him. Each moving crate also needs a legal destination; a wall, closed gate, or another crate can block the move. A charge does not remove obstacles. Undo a mistaken move or restart the warehouse to try another route.
