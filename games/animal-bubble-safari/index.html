WeightPlay Original Game Guide

Animal Bubble Safari - How to Play

Aim colorful animal bubbles, use wall bounces to reach difficult angles, and rescue trapped safari friends across 12 short puzzle stages.

How to Play

  1. Drag to aim, then release to shoot.
  2. Connect three or more matching animal bubbles.
  3. Use wall bounces and special bubbles to reach blocked targets.
  4. Complete the goal before your bubbles run out.

Parent Note

This picture-led game supports focus, planning, and hand-eye coordination through short, ad-free Kids sessions. Progress stays on this device.
