Sundrift Hunt

Aim and dodge while sunlight sweeps across the arena. Cool down in moving shade and earn gear by defeating guardians.

How to play

Goal

Defeat every guardian in the arena.

First hunt

Tap the outlined guardian to aim and fire. Each pulse that reaches it removes health.

Controls and rules

Move with the joystick, WASD or arrows. Aim and fire by tapping the arena. A held Pulse tracks the nearest guardian. Space dashes, and E uses the fitted shade screen.

Heat and rewards

Leave bright bands to cool down. Clear quickly with at least four health for three stars. Hunts 4, 15 and 30 unlock gear.

What changes with new gear?
