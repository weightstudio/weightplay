Robot Dog Showtime

Plan 30 original choreography stages for a block-built robot pup and fox partner.

GameplaySpatial Planning Puzzle
GenrePuzzle · Planning · Family

How to play

  1. Choose a performer and beat, then place a move or cue.
  2. Plan both routes together and rehearse the formation.
  3. Edit the first invalid beat and try again; your plan remains available.

Rules

Stay on the stage, avoid blocked marks, and never place both performers on the same mark. Some finales need both performers to Bow on the last beat.

Progress and replay

Clear a stage to save it and open the next one. Revisit any unlocked stage. After clearing Stages 2, 3, or 6, try the optional Spotlight Mix with swapped goals.

Match the marked formation

Reach both diamond marks on the numbered beat, then continue to the final goals.

  1. First Marks · Plan separate paths.
  2. Shared Lanes · Meet the marked beat.
  3. Passing Partners · Let a partner pass.
  4. Relay Formations · Exchange through both lanes.
  5. Precision Cues · Arrive on cue.
  6. Opening Night · Combine formation and Bow.

Privacy and settings

Progress stays in this browser. No account is required. Use the shared Settings control for language and sound choices.

FAQ

Is this a memory game?
No. You plan routes and cues for two performers on a visible beat board.
Does a rehearsal error lose progress?
No. The first invalid beat is shown and the cue plan remains editable.
Does progress move between browsers?
No. Current progress is stored locally in this browser.
