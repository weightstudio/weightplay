HOT TOPIC · ORIGINAL GAMEPLAY

Plan both dancers' moves, then rehearse the formation.

At a competition opening ceremony in Shanghai, dancers performed alongside large robots that changed formation and danced with partners. This puzzle turns that teamwork into planning cues for a fictional robot pup and animal partner.

How to play

Choose a performer and beat, select a move or cue, then rehearse both routes together. Some finales need both performers to Bow on the last beat.

Rules

Stay on the stage, avoid blocked marks, and never place both performers on the same mark. A wrong beat keeps your plan so you can edit it.

Progress

Clear a stage to save it and open the next one. Revisit any stage you've unlocked.

Privacy

Progress stays in this browser. The game does not need an account.

FAQ

Is this a memory game?
No. You plan routes and cues for two performers on a visible beat board.
Does a rehearsal error lose progress?
No. The first invalid beat is shown and the cue plan remains editable.
Does progress move between browsers?
No. Current progress is stored locally in this browser.
