How to Play

Hold. Find the green zone. Release. Finish ahead after the final shot. A basket earns 1; a perfect release earns 2 (3 on Bank Court). Keep the marker inside the green zone. The thin gold line is perfect. Space / Enter also work. Leaving the tab cancels your charge. From court 7, choose Direct or Bank before holding Shoot. A rim shutter blocks Direct; a board shutter blocks Bank. The green zone and dotted flight guide change with your route. Focus slows one released shot to 62% speed. Tokens are limited per match. Cancelling a charge spends no token. From 31, Pass spends one attempt and gives the rival its scheduled points, then stores two relay charges (capacity two). Assist spends one charge even on a miss, crosses every shutter and scores 2, or 3 for perfect. Pass breaks your scoring chain. Win by finishing ahead and meeting every displayed route, perfect, chain and final-route goal. A tie or missing goal loses. Records and unlocks persist; court 60 is the final court.
