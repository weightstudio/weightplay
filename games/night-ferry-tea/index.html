Night Ferry Teahouse

Set up your teahouse, brew for both piers, and wash cups before the next ferry.

Game guide

Goal

Deliver the required cups before the ferries depart.

How to play

Pick a stand site, brew the right tea, then tap the ferry order to send Nori. Wash returned cups to replenish the rack.

Controls and rules

Tap or use Tab and Enter. Each service action advances one beat and every busy station works in parallel. Check ferry windows, travel and freshness.

Results

On-time orders earn two coins. Meet the shift goal to unlock the next shift, or replay with a different layout for three stars.
