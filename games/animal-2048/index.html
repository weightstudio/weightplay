Animal 2048: Forest Evolution

Animal 2048 is a 30-mission grid strategy game hosted by Moon Cap Orla. Every swipe moves the complete board. When two equal animal tiles meet, they evolve into the next tier. The challenge is deciding which direction protects open spaces while building the mission target.

How to Play

  1. Choose an unlocked forest mission.
  2. Swipe the board or use the direction controls.
  3. Equal animals that meet merge once into the next tier.
  4. Keep empty spaces available so the board can continue moving.
  5. Reach the shown tile or score goal before the move limit.

Thirty Missions

Six five-stage chapters introduce basic slides, chain reactions, moss-stone blockers, score lanterns, rare-animal nests, and grand evolution challenges. Every mission has a deterministic start and spawn seed, so a retry remains fair. Undo restores the exact previous turn.

Player and Save Information

This game practices planning and sequencing through play. Progress, stars, best scores, and unlocked missions are stored only in this browser. No account is required, and the Result report is not a formal ability assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does every swipe add a tile?
Only a swipe that changes the board consumes a move and adds one new low-tier animal.
Can one tile merge twice in one swipe?
No. A tile created during the current swipe cannot merge again until a later turn.
What does Undo restore?
It restores the complete previous board, score, move count, objective state, and deterministic spawn state.
Does progress save?
Yes, locally in the current browser.
