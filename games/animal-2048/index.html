Animal 2048: Forest Evolution

Animal 2048 is a 30-mission grid strategy game hosted by Moon Cap Orla. Every swipe moves the complete board. Matching animals evolve into the next tier, so each direction must protect open spaces while building the mission target.

Gameplay2048 Grid Merge
GenreLogic · Strategy · Animal
DifficultyEasy to Challenging
Play time2–6 minutes per mission
SkillsSpatial Planning · Logic · Sequencing

How to Play

  1. Choose an unlocked forest mission.
  2. Swipe the board or use the direction controls.
  3. Equal animals merge once into the next tier.
  4. Keep empty spaces available so the board can continue moving.
  5. Reach the shown tile, merge, or score goal before the move limit.

Thirty Missions

Six five-stage chapters introduce basic slides, chain reactions, moss-stone blockers, score goals, rare-animal nests, and grand evolution challenges. Every mission has a deterministic start and spawn seed, so retries remain fair. Undo restores the exact previous turn.

Player and Save Information

Progress, stars, best scores, and unlocked missions are stored only in this browser. No account is required, and the planning report is not a formal ability assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does every swipe add a tile?
Only a swipe that changes the board consumes a move and adds one low-tier animal.
Can one tile merge twice in one swipe?
No. A newly merged tile must wait until a later turn.
What does Undo restore?
The complete previous board, score, move count, objective state, and spawn state.
Does progress save?
Yes, locally in the current browser.
