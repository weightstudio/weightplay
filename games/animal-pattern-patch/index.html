How to find the missing tile

Start with the rule shown above the three-by-three board. When rows repeat, compare the gap with the same column in a complete row. In a mirror puzzle, look at the matching spot across the middle. Tap a gap and then a tile from the tray. In later stages, check both the shape and how many copies appear on the tile.

A wrong tile leaves the board in place, so you can try a different answer. A clue highlights the cells worth comparing. Fill every gap to unlock the next stage. The two extra medals track whether you finished without a wrong answer and without a clue. Your medals are saved in this browser.
