How to play

  1. Start a round and enter a five-letter word.
  2. Read the exact, near, and miss feedback on each row.
  3. Use Hint when you want a clue without auto-playing a guess.
  4. Submit up to six guesses, then review the natural Result.
  5. Choose Play again for another short puzzle or return to Main.
