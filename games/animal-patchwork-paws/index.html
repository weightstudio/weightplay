How to play

Turn each habitat tile clockwise until neighbouring trail colours meet.

  1. Open a patch and study the four tile edges.
  2. Tap tiles to rotate them clockwise, then check the weave.
  3. Use the calm retry message or Reset patch when the edges do not match.
