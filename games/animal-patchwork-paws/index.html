Patchwork Paws

Restore 30 authored habitat stages across six arcs while learning a new trail rule in each chapter.

GameplayEdge-Matching Habitat Puzzle

How to play

  1. Open an unlocked stage and study its four tile edges and arc cue.
  2. Turn each tile clockwise, then check the weave when the neighbouring trails line up.
  3. Read the result, use Reset when useful, and choose the next stage or return to the map.

Campaign and checkpoints

The authored campaign has 30 stages arranged as six five-stage arcs: Meadow Beginnings, Bramble Winds, Echoing Creeks, Compass Canopy, Braided Moonrise, and Guardian Finale. Stages 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 are checkpoints that introduce the next rule family and celebrate a completed arc. Each stage card names its animal, chapter, and current open or woven state, so the map always explains where to go next.

Reading the board

Every patch is a four-tile grid. Compare the east and west edges across each row and the south and north edges down each column. Matching trail colours are the base rule; later arcs add a visible wind, echo, compass, braid, or guardian cue after the weave connects. Study the cue before turning anything, then predict which face should point toward its neighbour. The marked tile and the rule note stay visible while you work.

Safe recovery

A wrong check never removes progress or locks a stage. The feedback names the mismatch, so turn a tile and check again, or press Reset patch to restore the authored starting orientation. A completed Result keeps Next stage and Back to stages available for a calm continuation. There is no timer, lives meter, or destructive penalty: every attempt is a small, readable experiment that can be reversed.

Progress and replay

Clearing a stage unlocks the next stage and records the woven map in this browser. Completed stages remain open for replay, and the best campaign run records its turn count locally. Replaying a clear is useful for practising a later arc rule or finding a shorter rotation path. No account, purchase, or server save is required, and malformed local values are safely normalized back to the first available stage.

Accessibility and settings

Tiles and actions are native buttons with readable labels, and the shared Settings control keeps language and sound choices available in Main, Stage, and Battle without adding a second overlapping control. Arabic routes set RTL direction and preserve the same logical trail relationships. The board remains a compact Canvas with clear status feedback, while the guide and map can be read before entering a stage. Focus stays on the active scene, return arrows remain reachable, and the calm layout leaves room for both short phone sessions and wide desktop planning.

FAQ

How many stages are included?
Thirty authored stages form six five-stage arcs with six checkpoints.
What happens after a mismatch?
The board stays available with clear feedback; turn or reset and try again without penalty.
Is progress saved?
Cleared stages and the best run are stored only in this browser.
Can completed stages be replayed?
Yes. Open any cleared stage from the map whenever you want to practise.
