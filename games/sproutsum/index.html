Goal

Orla’s greenhouse has planting beds that share their numbers. Restore each bed by making every crossing equation true. A tile that completes one row may leave the wrong number for a column, so look in both directions before committing.

How to play

Place the available numbers so every row and column equals its target. Multiply and divide before adding and subtracting. Tap a space, then a number. Fixed jade tiles stay in place. Check and Undo are free.

Controls and rules

Odd/even sockets constrain a tile. A vine joins two cells whose numbers must make the shown total. An arrow orders a smaller number before a larger one. Hinged operators can be flipped. Undo restores the last placement, note or operator change. Erase returns one number. From Chapter 2, the pencil stores up to three candidates in an empty space; notes do not consume tray tiles.

Finishing and feedback

When all spaces, equations and extra clues agree, the beds bloom before the result appears. Next Stage opens the next garden; Replay tries the same one. Replaying a cleared stage does not create another first-clear reward.

Two chapters, changing decisions

The 60 gardens form two chapters of 30 stages. The first moves from small addition beds to subtraction, multiplication, exact division, odd/even sockets, vine totals and ordering clues. Chapter two adds hinged operators and optional pencil notes, then combines the rules. The tray is finite: repeated numbers are separate copies, not an unlimited supply.

From the developer

We chose tap-to-select placement so a finger never hides the equation being considered. The fixed camera keeps crossings in view. There is no countdown or life meter: the challenge is to reconcile shared information. Undo, erasing and checking cost nothing, and checking points to a conflict without filling in the answer. Garden finishes are optional visual changes and never alter a puzzle.

Player and save information

The unfinished board, cleared stages and chosen finish are stored in this browser. Clearing site data can remove that progress. No sign-in is needed to play. The dawn garden is included; clearing Stage 5 earns rain glazing. Starlit glazing is a permanent optional purchase for 5 shared diamonds. All 60 puzzles remain available through normal play without that purchase.

Questions about the garden

Why can’t I use a number again?

The tray shows how many copies remain. Erase a placed tile to return that copy to the tray; jade tiles are fixed clues.

Why does a line with the right numbers fail?

Use multiplication and division before addition and subtraction. Division must be exact. A row and its crossing column must both reach their printed targets.

What do the small coordinates and vines mean?

A1 means column A, row 1. A vine such as A1 + C3 = 14 constrains those two numbers together. Odd/even labels and smaller-than signs are additional rules, not decoration.

What happens after my last tile?
