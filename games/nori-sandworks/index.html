Goal

Help Nori fill every tray with sand of the same color. A full tray leaves the board and frees its space. Thirty puzzles form six miniature coastal scenes.

How to play

The four sand chambers feed the ports with matching names: W0, W1, E0 and E1. Symbols identify the available colors. Removing the bottom sand makes the next layer fall. A partly filled tray can still move, so leave a path for the next tray before uncovering its color.

Controls and rules

Drag in a straight line, or select a tray and tap its destination. With a keyboard, preview the move with arrow keys and press Enter; Escape cancels. Each tray cell holds 28 grains. A tray can rotate only when it is fully inside the gold turntable. Gates alternate the open port pair; locks show the tray that opens them. Delivery order restricts full trays, not partial filling. Undo and restart are always free.

Finishing and progress

One star opens the next puzzle; fewer moves earn up to three. Each first clear adds a permanent piece and five pieces complete a scene you can display beside the board. The coast remembers solved puzzles without increasing tray capacity. Two optional diamond finishes change appearance only. Play without a timer on a phone or computer. Progress stays in this browser and does not sync between devices.

How to play

Is there a time limit?

No. Think at your own pace and undo freely.

What do diamonds unlock?

Two permanent visual finishes. All puzzles, tools and coastal scenes are free.

Can I stop during a puzzle?

Yes. When storage is available, this browser saves the puzzle, undo history, clears and collection. Clearing browser data removes local progress.
