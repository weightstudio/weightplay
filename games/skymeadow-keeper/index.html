Skymeadow Keeper

Goal

Bring wildlife home and restore the windtree in each meadow. Your choices of food, route and habitat change how you reach it.

How to play

Collect berries, roots or nectar. Feed a nearby animal its preferred food, then lead it to a matching habitat. Spend produce on paths or deposit it at the windtree.

Controls and rules

Move with WASD or the left stick. Drag the scene to look. Aim before using your tool or interacting. Keep slow animals close and guide twilight foxes along lantern paths.

Progress

Restore the windtree to unlock the next meadow. Research and expedition progress stay on this device. Optional diamond purchases change appearance; every mission can be completed without them.
