About WeightPlay

WeightPlay is a browser-game collection developed and operated by WeightStudio. Open a game on your phone or computer without downloading an app or creating an account.

The general lobby has three halls: Block World for block-style adventures, action, puzzles and strategy; Cards & Boards for card and board games; and Topics for games inspired by current ideas. Use the categories or search to find a game, then read its goal, controls and rules on the game page.

What We Build

  • Playable browser games with their own controls, objectives and rules.
  • A shared block-style world alongside familiar card and board-game formats.
  • Game guides that explain the actual play loop and local-save behavior.
  • Updates to game behavior, artwork, controls and page content when problems are found.

WeightPlay Kids

WeightPlay Kids is a separate, ad-free collection for younger players, grouped into 3+, 6+ and 9+ age bands. Its game pages provide parent-facing information. These groups belong to Kids; the general lobby is organized by how the games play.

Scores and progress are for play and personal progress tracking. They are not IQ tests, diagnoses or formal learning assessments.

Privacy-Friendly Play

Basic play does not require a login. Some games may store progress, settings, scores, coins, diamonds, or language preferences in the browser using local storage so players can continue on the same device.

Advertising and Rewards

WeightPlay Kids and every game listed there are ad-free. Future display advertising is limited to approved placements in general-audience games and stays separate from gameplay controls, daily gifts, coins, diamonds, upgrades, stage progress, and result buttons.

Development and Feedback

WeightStudio maintains the game code, interfaces, artwork and guides. Browser checks help identify loading, control, layout and navigation problems; feedback from players helps us find issues those checks miss. If a game fails to open, a control is unclear or a guide is incorrect, please use the Contact page and include the game name, language and steps that led to the problem.

繁體中文

WeightPlay 是由 WeightStudio 開發與營運的網頁遊戲平台。在手機或電腦打開遊戲，不必下載 App 或建立帳號。

一般大廳

一般大廳分為方塊世界、棋牌與熱梗三個分區。方塊世界提供方塊風格的冒險、動作、益智與策略遊戲；棋牌分區集中紙牌與棋盤對局；熱梗分區收錄由當前話題發想的遊戲。可以用分類和搜尋挑選，再從作品頁了解目標、操作與規則。

獨立的 Kids 大廳

WeightPlay Kids 是另外的無廣告兒童遊戲區，依 3+、6+、9+ 分級，並提供家長閱讀的遊戲資訊。一般大廳依玩法分類，不使用 Kids 的年齡分區。分數與進度用於遊玩和個人紀錄，不是智力測驗、醫療診斷或正式學習評量。

存檔與廣告

有存檔功能的遊戲會將進度或設定保存在目前瀏覽器，不會自動同步到另一台裝置；清除瀏覽器資料可能移除存檔。Kids 維持無廣告；一般大廳未來的廣告位置必須與遊戲操作、獎勵和進度清楚區隔。

製作與問題回報

WeightStudio 維護遊戲程式、介面、美術與指南，並透過瀏覽器檢查和玩家回報修正問題。如果遊戲打不開、操作不清楚或說明有誤，請透過聯絡頁提供遊戲名稱、語言與重現步驟。
