العالم والرسالة

Fruit arcs through a bright treetop arena in short waves. The goal is to read motion quickly, make a clean swipe, and recover when the pattern changes.

كيف تعمل الأنظمة

Drag across fruit in the live arena to collect it; every successful hit raises the score and can extend the chain.

Dark seed pods are hazards. Three misses end the wave, while a clear wave records its result and opens the next replay choice.

طريقة اللعب

  1. Enter a chapter and wait for the ready cue to finish.
  2. Drag across visible safe fruit with one continuous swipe.
  3. Follow the next arc instead of chasing a target that has already left the arena.
  4. Avoid dark seed pods and use Retry or Next after the wave settles.

نصائح استراتيجية

  • Start with the closest fruit, then extend the same stroke only when the next target is clearly safe.
  • Leave space around dark pods; a shorter clean swipe is better than a risky chain.
  • Watch the score and chain feedback to confirm that your gesture changed the board.

تطور المراحل والصعوبة

Six chapters move from readable arcs to faster fruit, moving pads, tight corners, a fast rival pattern, and a final rhythm. Each wave remains short enough to replay immediately.

ملاحظة تصميم المطوّر

Canopy Cut is an original swipe-first arcade game with its own canopy art, target pacing, hazard language, and direct gesture feedback; the visible fruit response is the core player reward.

ملاحظة للوالدين

This General browser game has no account, purchase, chat, leaderboard, or advertising flow. Score, best wave, and local replay progress stay in this browser.
