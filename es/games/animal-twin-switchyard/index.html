Salto de Señales en Pareja

Twin Switchyard is an original synchronized platform puzzle. Guide an otter and a mountain goat through split platform lanes, coordinate their shared moves, collect every shard, activate each switch, and reach their paired lantern exits.

JugabilidadSynchronized Platform Puzzle
GéneroPuzzle · Platform · Animal
DificultadEasy to Challenging
Tiempo estimado3-8 minutes per stage

Mundo y misión

Two route keepers share one signal line: the otter handles the low lane while the mountain goat takes the high lane. Each switchyard asks both travelers to move through the same moment without leaving one behind.

Sistemas del juego

The two characters advance from one shared input. Left and Right move both travelers across their separate lanes, and Jump gives both the same hop. Platforms, floor heights, fixed switches, shards, and static hazards create different route decisions. Both lantern gates open together after every switch in the stage has been reached.

A shard is collected only when its assigned traveler reaches it. A hazard collision or missed fall resets the current stage attempt; collecting every shard and activating every switch lets both travelers finish at their paired exits. Stage progress and best move counts stay in this browser.

Cómo jugar

  1. Choose any unlocked stage from Stage; each card shows its stage number and best move count when available.
  2. Use Left and Right or the on-screen controls to line up both travelers, then use Jump to clear a platform gap or fixed hazard.
  3. Collect each shard with the traveler in its lane. If switches are present, reach all of them; both lantern gates open together.
  4. Guide both travelers to their paired exits. A collision or missed fall restarts the current attempt; a clear opens Result and normally unlocks the next stage.

Consejos

  • Plan both landing positions before pressing Jump; the same hop can clear one lane and land the other on a different shelf.
  • Use short direction presses near pickups and hazards so both travelers do not drift past a safe position.
  • Read the upper and lower platform, shard, switch, and hazard positions together. The nearest shard is not always the safest next pickup.
  • When a stage fails, note which lane reached a fixed hazard first and adjust the shared timing instead of changing every move.

Progreso y dificultad

Thirty authored stages form six five-stage arcs. Checkpoint puzzles at Stages 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 combine the same documented rules: shared movement and jumps, offset platforms and shards, fixed switches, static hazards, and paired exits. No alternating gate, moving-hazard, or moving-platform rule is used.

Nota de diseño

Twin Switchyard mantiene un espacio de juego lógico en el teléfono, el paisaje y el escritorio. Su movimiento compartido, peligros específicos de carril, botones táctiles, activación del mouse y controles de teclado se resuelven en el mismo estado, mientras que Principal, Escenario, Batalla y Resultado siguen siendo pantallas separadas con retornos directos. La interfaz completa usa un único diseño lógico centrado con un máximo de 920 píxeles. Las vistas de móvil, horizontal y escritorio escalan juntos los mismos controles, zonas de impacto, ilustraciones y coordenadas del juego, en lugar de estirar elementos individuales. Por eso el tacto, el ratón y el teclado actúan sobre el mismo estado legal. El póster de Main y la acción de iniciar la partida permanecen separados de la gestión de Stage, mientras que la selección de Stage, Battle, los diálogos y Result conservan su propio contenido acotado y ruta de regreso.

Nota para familias

Este juego de navegador general no tiene cuenta, compra, chat, tabla de clasificación ni flujo publicitario. Los borrados de escenario, los mejores recuentos de movimientos, el idioma, el sonido y las preferencias permanecen en el perfil actual del navegador y pueden restablecerse cuando se borran los datos del sitio. Puedes jugar sin crear una cuenta. El progreso se guarda en el almacenamiento local del perfil actual del navegador, no en una cuenta en línea ni en una base de datos entre dispositivos. Borrar los datos del sitio, usar la navegación privada, cambiar de navegador o pasar a otro dispositivo puede crear un guardado independiente o eliminar el existente. Cuando el navegador permite guardar datos, las preferencias de idioma, sonido y reducción de movimiento siguen los controles compartidos de WeightPlay. Ningún resultado de la guía es una evaluación médica, escolar o profesional.

Preguntas frecuentes

Can the two travelers move independently?
No. Each input moves the pair together, so the puzzle is about shared timing and lane planning.
When do the gates open?
Both lantern gates open together after every switch in that stage has been reached; stages without switches start with both open.
What causes a failed stage?
A traveler touching a fixed hazard or falling off the playable route resets the current attempt.
Does progress save?
Cleared stages and local best move counts are saved in this browser. Thirty authored stages are arranged in six five-stage arcs.
Which controls and screen sizes are supported?
The same rules support touch, mouse, and keyboard where the game uses those inputs. The interface scales as one logical layout across the required phone, landscape, and desktop viewports.
Can progress move automatically to another device?
No. This game currently saves to local browser storage only, so another browser profile or device begins with its own separate local progress unless a future account system is added.
