How to play

  1. Choose two numbers that add up to exactly 10.
  2. They must share a row or column, with no occupied tile between them. Diagonal pairs do not count.
  3. Clear every pair to unlock the next grove.

Hint highlights a legal pair without playing it. Undo restores your last cleared pair. Reorder rearranges remaining tiles; Restart resets this grove. The first four groves use 4×6 boards; groves 11–20 grow to 5×6 and groves 21–30 to 6×6. A common mistake is choosing a pair that totals 10 but is blocked by another tile: the values stay on the board until they share a clear row or column. Reorder costs a move and rebuilds the remaining pairs when no useful line is open.

常見問題

為什麼兩個相同數字不能消除？
只有 5 與 5 能以相同數字配對。每組合法配對都必須相加等於 10，並位於同一條暢通的橫列或直欄視線上。
斜對角的數字可以配對嗎？
不可以。兩張數字牌必須相鄰，或能沿著同一橫列或直欄的空格互相看見。
「重新排列」有什麼作用？
它只會重新排列尚未消除的數字，不會改變相加等於 10 的規則。
每個關卡有時間限制嗎？
沒有。你可以按照自己的步調觀察棋盤。
