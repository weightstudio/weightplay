三峰接龍

Clear the three peaks by building a chain on the current card.

How to play

  1. Tap an exposed card exactly one rank above or below the current card. Suit and color do not matter.
  2. A follows 2 only; K follows Q only. A and K cannot connect. Cards under other cards are unavailable.
  3. Remove cards to uncover more choices. If no useful move remains, draw from the stock; this breaks the combo.

Hint shows a move. Undo reverses a move. Restart repeats the deal; New Game deals another.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the goal?
Clear all three peaks by playing exposed cards one rank above or below the current card.
Can Ace connect to King?
No. Ace connects only to 2, and King connects only to Queen.
What happens when no useful move remains?
Draw from the stock. Drawing breaks the current combo.
Can I undo a move?
Yes. Undo reverses the latest move, while Restart repeats the deal.
