How to play WeightPlay Original Animal Tournament

Shoot, read, and save

Every match alternates your shot with your save. Win the short shootout to unlock the next original forest club.

Controls and rules

Press a numbered goal zone and release to shoot. On defence, wait for the real cue, then choose the matching zone before time runs out.

Win, loss, and recovery

Score more goals after five pairs. Ties enter fair sudden death. A loss keeps every earlier cup and offers Replay or Stages immediately.

30-match campaign

Six stadium chapters add moving power windows, keeper adaptation, readable feints, and faster saves without changing the six-zone rules.

Practical tips

Vary your corners when the keeper learns. On defence, ignore the dim fake ring and move only after the bright paw cue.

Save and privacy

Stars and unlocked matches stay only in this browser. No account, upload, public ranking, purchase, or personal profile is required.

FAQ and related play

Touch, mouse, and keyboard use identical rules. Try Animal Putt Trails later for calm course planning; this cup focuses on attack-and-defence timing.
