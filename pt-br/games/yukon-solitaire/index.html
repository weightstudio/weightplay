Paciência Yukon

Build four foundations from Ace to King. Move any exposed card with every exposed card below it; no Stock is used.

Goal

Build every suit foundation.

Freedom

Move any face-up group.

Assist

Hint, Undo, and Restart.

Paciência Yukon

Reveal hidden cards and build all four suit foundations from Ace to King.

How to play

  1. Choose any face-up card and move it with every card below it, even if that group is not ordered.
  2. Place the selected card on a card one rank higher and of the opposite color. Only a King-led group may fill an empty column.
  3. Send exposed end cards to the matching suit foundation in Ace-to-King order. There is no stock; uncovering hidden cards creates new choices.

Hint shows a move; Undo reverses it. Restart repeats the deal; New Game deals another.

Perguntas frequentes

Is there a Stock?
No. All 52 cards begin on the tableau.
Can any face-up group move?
Yes. Any face-up card may carry every face-up card below it.
What can fill an empty column?
A King or a group whose first card is a King.
Do tableau suits alternate?
Yes. Tableau builds descend by rank in alternating colors.
Can I undo?
Yes. Undo restores the full prior tableau and foundations.
