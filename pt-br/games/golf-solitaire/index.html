Golf Solitaire

Clear the seven columns by building a long chain on the waste card.

How to play

  1. Choose the exposed bottom card of a column, exactly one rank above or below the waste card. Suit and color do not matter.
  2. Ace connects only to 2, King only to Queen. Ace and King cannot connect. Removing a card opens the next card in that column.
  3. Plan a chain before drawing from Stock: a draw breaks the combo. Clear the table before the stock and legal moves run out.

Hint shows a move; Undo reverses it. Restart repeats this deal; New Game deals another.

Perguntas frequentes

How many tableau cards are there?
Seven columns hold five cards each, for 35 tableau cards.
Do suits matter?
No. Only rank matters for the one-above or one-below rule.
Does Ace wrap to King?
No. This strict classic version keeps Ace and King at the ends.
When should I draw Stock?
Draw when no useful exposed tableau card can continue the Waste chain.
Can I undo?
Yes. Undo restores the full prior board and chain.
