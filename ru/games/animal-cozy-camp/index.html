Как играть

1. Read the notes

Start by reading both comfort notes for the current circle. Each note describes a relationship, a preferred neighbour, or a direction that matters around the fire. Treat the words as the puzzle rules, not as decoration. Notice who is named, whether the clue says beside or opposite, and whether a phrase such as left, away, or between fixes the direction. You do not need to remember anything from an earlier circle: every round gives you a fresh, self-contained set of clues.

2. Compare circles

Three circle cards show three complete seat orders. A circular order wraps around, so the first and last names are neighbours too. Compare each card with every clue before checking it. For a beside clue, look at the two seats touching that animal. For an opposite clue, count two seats across the four-seat circle. For a between clue, check the two neighbours on both sides of the named animal. A card can look almost right while still breaking one small relationship, so read the full order instead of judging by the first pair you recognize. The animals are Orla, Moss, Taro, and Pip, and their positions rotate from round to round.

3. Check gently

Select a card by tapping or clicking anywhere on it. The selected card gets a clear highlight, the selection message names its position, and the Check the camp button becomes available. If your choice is not the solution, the camp remains safe: the round does not advance, the clues stay visible, and you can choose another card. A wrong check is useful feedback rather than a penalty. Read the notes again, compare the neighbours, and try a different order. Use Reset choice when you want to remove the highlight and reconsider the round from the beginning. Your checks are counted for the result, so careful reading helps you make a cleaner run.

4. Follow the three-round rhythm

The camp has three authored circles: Lantern circle, Pinecone circle, and Quiet circle. Solve the current circle to move to the next one. After a correct check, the status message confirms the warm result and the next circle appears automatically. The round badge shows where you are, while the changing clues and names keep each step distinct. There is no timer, hidden action, or combat system. Take the time you need to reason about one relationship at a time. The first card is the solution in this calm prototype, but the point is still to verify it from the clues rather than guess. A strong play session explains why the order works, not only that it advances.

5. Finish and replay

When all three circles are correct, the Result screen reports the number of checks used in the session and compares it with the best result saved in this browser. Replay camp starts a new Circle 1 with no old selection, success message, or disabled action left behind. Back to start returns to the Main screen, where the guide and shared controls are available again. Progress is intentionally lightweight: the best check count and your sound preference stay in local browser storage, and there is no account or purchase flow. If you switch languages in Settings, the current route updates its interface copy and preserves the same gentle puzzle rules.

FAQ

How long is a session? Most players can read and solve all three circles in a few minutes, with no pressure to hurry. What happens after a miss? Nothing is lost; the same circle stays open so you can reread the clues, reset the card, and try again. Does the puzzle need a special device? No. The cards and buttons are designed for touch, mouse, and keyboard use, with large action targets and a responsive layout for portrait and landscape screens. Is the order random? The three authored rounds are deterministic, so the same clues always describe the same solution. Can I change sound or language? Open the shared Settings control on Main or Result, choose a language, and use the Sound switch as needed. Why are there no ads in the play area? This internal General prototype reserves the governed space without placing an ad over the puzzle. The important habit is simple: read the current notes, test one complete circle, learn from a miss, and keep the camp comfortable.
