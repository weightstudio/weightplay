How to read the clues

Each phase names a cardinal sun mark and a wind rule. Hold means face the marked direction; clockwise or counterclockwise means turn one compass step from that mark; opposite means face the other way. Read the mark and rule together, then compare the result with the four visible direction choices. Some lookouts have ordered Echo phases: solve the current clue before the next one appears. Later arcs combine the same readable vocabulary rather than hiding an answer behind a timer.

How to play

  1. Choose an unlocked stage on the Canopy map; swipe or use arrow keys to browse the bounded compass rail.
  2. Read the current sun mark and wind rule, then choose North, East, South, or West.
  3. Press Check the perch. A correct Echo phase reveals the next clue; the final correct phase clears the stage.
  4. Use Next stage to continue, or return to the map to replay any cleared stage.

There are no countdowns, lives, purchases, accounts, or online profiles. Touch, mouse, and keyboard use the same four-direction decision; the current clue remains visible throughout.

Mistakes and retries

A wrong facing is safe: the current clue remains open and the game explains the miss without removing progress. Choose another direction and check again. Reset direction returns the compass to North but does not erase earlier Echo phases or the stage's best-turn record. Arrow keys choose a facing; press Enter or Space when the compass has focus to check it.

Chapter 1: six windward arcs

Thirty authored stages form First Dawn, Crosswind Canopy, Mirror Grove, Echoing Branches, High Boughs, and Moonlit Mastery. The first arc teaches direct bearings and a simple wind turn. Later arcs practise clockwise, counterclockwise, and opposite transformations, then combine them in two- and three-phase Echoes. Stages 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 are multi-phase checkpoints that award a named local badge. Stage 30 is the finale; it does not lead to a hidden Stage 31.

Results, replay, and saves

Clearing a stage saves its stable number locally and unlocks exactly the next stage. Cleared stages remain replayable, and returning to the map centers the highest unlocked stage. Each stage keeps its own best turn count; checkpoint badges are also saved. The new campaign uses a separate save key, so the former three-lookout best-turn value is preserved without being reinterpreted as a Chapter 1 score. Clearing browser data may remove local progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is progress saved?
Yes. Cleared stages, the next unlock, stage bests, and checkpoint badges are stored locally when browser storage is available.
What happens after a wrong direction?
The current clue remains available and you can choose another direction without losing the stage.
Can I play with a keyboard?
Yes. Use the arrow keys to choose a facing, then Enter or Space to check the compass.
How long is the chapter?
Chapter 1 has 30 stages in six five-stage arcs. There is no timer, and cleared stages can be replayed.
