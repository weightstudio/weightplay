Results and saves

Thirty stages form six five-stage arcs. Progress unlocks the next stage, cleared stages remain replayable, and completed progress plus your best total moves are stored only in this browser when storage is available.

Practical tips

Read the target as a picture, not a timed task. Check the arc rule before moving, use Reset when a trial gets tangled, and replay any restored stage to refine your route.

FAQ

How many stages are included?
6개의 규칙 장에 걸쳐 30개의 작성된 단계가 있습니다. 스테이지 30은 완료 후에도 다시 플레이할 수 있습니다.
Is progress saved?
Yes, only in this browser.
