Collect your power

Collect golden blades and caches to expand your orbit. Gather crystals from defeated enemies to earn upgrade choices.

Forge your own orbit

Choose speed, power or reach. At level 3 choose Ember, Frost or Storm. Eight blades, Power rank 3 and Speed rank 2 unlock an Awakening choice.

Controls and victory

Drag the shared joystick or use WASD / arrow keys. Space or Dash evades attacks. Read each stage’s survival, cache, beacon, nest or guardian objective.

Progress stays with you

Clears unlock stages. Settled gold buys permanent training. Run upgrades reset each encounter. The optional 5-diamond Starcrystal Inlay changes appearance only; all stages remain free.

Must I tap to attack?

No. Swords orbit and attack automatically. Focus on movement, pickups and upgrade choices.
