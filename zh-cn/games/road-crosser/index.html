星轨过路

星轨过路是 WeightPlay 原创的时机街机游戏。带领星行者穿过车流、移动河流与三段逐步收紧的路线。

玩法Timing Crossing Arcade
类型Classic · Arcade · Action
难度Easy to Challenging
预估游玩时间1-4 minutes per run
练习能力定时 · 专注 · 适应

世界与任务

The Star Runner is carrying a small signal light across a night route. Cars move in readable pulses, river logs follow their own currents, and every safe step reveals the next timing decision.

游戏系统

The runner moves one lane per input. Cars are hazards, logs carry the runner, water removes an unsupported runner, and rocks block later routes. A complete crossing advances to the next authored stage.

玩法说明

  1. 桌面使用方向键或 WASD；手机使用滑动与方向按钮。
  2. 一次观察一条车道，只在下一格安全时移动。
  3. 在河流中踏上移动木头，顺着水流调整位置。
  4. 通过三段路线抵达星门；撞车、落水或被冲出场外会结束本局。

策略提示

  • Observe one full vehicle cycle before committing to a long crossing.
  • Use short steps to pause between traffic lanes.
  • On logs, plan for the current instead of fighting it with repeated taps.
  • Later stages reward patience because faster does not always mean safer.

关卡与难度成长

Stage 1 teaches the road and river rhythm. Stage 2 adds denser traffic and rocks. Stage 3 combines faster water, more vehicles, and tighter safe windows.

开发设计理念

Road Crosser borrows the broad timing-and-crossing idea of classic arcade games while using WeightPlay's own Star Runner, night-route palette, authored hazards, copy, controls, and geometry.

家长说明

This public game has no account, purchase, chat, leaderboard, or advertising flow. Score and Best stay in the current browser.

常见问题

What ends a run?
A vehicle collision, falling into the river, or being carried off the playfield ends the run.
Does the river move randomly?
No. Its logs follow visible, repeatable speed patterns.
Which controls work?
Arrow keys, WASD, touch swipe, and the visible mobile direction pad.
Is progress saved?
Only the local Best score is saved in this browser.
