Advertising Principles

WeightPlay may use normal display advertising in approved general-audience Battle placements in the future. The WeightPlay Kids lobby and every game listed there are permanently ad-free: they do not load display-ad code, request inventory, or reserve advertising space.

Kids Games Are Ad-Free

WeightPlay Kids is a separate 3+, 6+, and 9+ game collection. Its Main, Stage, Battle, and Battle result states use the complete game viewport without ad slots or empty ad placeholders.

Clear Separation

Ads should never look like game cards, inventory items, upgrade choices, stage buttons, daily gifts, coins, diamonds, or result rewards. Players should always be able to tell what is game content and what is advertising.

No Reward Confusion

Normal display ads are not part of the WeightPlay reward system. Players should not be asked to interact with normal ads to receive coins, diamonds, items, upgrades, stages, revives, retries, or progress.

Optional Rewarded Ads in General Games

Some general-audience games may later offer a clearly labeled rewarded ad for an optional benefit, such as doubling a completed-stage reward, reviving once, or claiming an optional daily bonus. This is a separate rewarded-ad format, never a normal display banner.

Before an ad starts, the game must state the required action and the exact in-game reward. The player must actively choose to continue and must also have a clear way to decline. Declining, dismissing, or finding no ad available must not block normal gameplay, the earned base result, retry, stage selection, or return navigation.

Rewards must stay inside WeightPlay, have no cash value, and be non-transferable. A promised reward is granted only after the required rewarded-ad action is completed. Rewarded-ad controls must remain visually distinct from normal Continue, Retry, Claim, and Home controls.

Gameplay Safety

Future ad spaces must stay away from active play areas, drag controls, swipe areas, rapid-tap buttons, shop panels, upgrade choices, result buttons, and other places where accidental taps are likely.

Family-Friendly Review

Each future ad placement should be reviewed on mobile and desktop before release. Parents and visitors can report confusing or unsafe placements through the Contact page.

Quick Questions for Families

Do normal display ads change game rewards?

No. Normal display ads never grant coins, diamonds, items, upgrades, extra stages, revives, retries, or daily rewards. Core play and game progress never require interacting with a normal display ad.

Can an ad appear while a child is playing?

No. The WeightPlay Kids lobby and all Kids games contain no display ads, rewarded ads, ad prompts, ad requests, or empty advertising reserves. This includes Main, Stage, Battle, and Battle result states.

Are rewarded ads required to progress?

No. In general games, rewarded ads are optional choices for a disclosed extra benefit. The normal earned result and ordinary game flow remain available without watching an ad.

Does an empty space mean there is already an ad?

No. A future layout reserve is inert until a specific placement has passed review. It has no ad unit, click action, reward, or gameplay function.

Verification Status

WeightPlay has added the AdSense account verification meta tag, verification script, and root ads.txt publisher record for review. These items only verify site ownership and publisher identity; they do not approve, create, or display live ad units.

No live ad placement is currently approved on WeightPlay. Any future ad slot must pass the separation, spacing, mobile, and family-safety checks described on this page before release.

廣告原則

WeightPlay 未來可能加入一般展示廣告。在加入任何廣告位置以前，版面必須讓廣告與遊戲操作、獎勵及兒童會使用的控制項清楚分開。

清楚區分廣告與遊戲內容

廣告不會設計成遊戲卡片、背包道具、升級選項、關卡按鈕、每日禮物、金幣、鑽石或結算獎勵。玩家應該能立即分辨遊戲內容與廣告。

一般展示廣告不提供遊戲獎勵

一般展示廣告不是 WeightPlay 的獎勵系統。玩家不需要點擊或觀看一般展示廣告來取得金幣、鑽石、道具、升級、額外關卡、復活、重試或遊戲進度。

一般遊戲可提供自願式獎勵廣告

部分一般遊戲未來可能提供清楚標示的獎勵廣告，讓玩家自願觀看後取得額外福利，例如結算獎勵加倍、單次復活，或領取額外的每日獎勵。這是獨立的獎勵型廣告格式，不是一般展示橫幅。

廣告開始前，遊戲必須清楚說明玩家需要完成的動作與確切的遊戲內獎勵，並同時提供接受與拒絕選項。拒絕、關閉或暫時沒有可用廣告時，不能阻擋基本遊玩、原本已取得的結算獎勵、重試、選關或返回操作。

獎勵只能在 WeightPlay 內使用、不可轉讓且沒有現金價值；玩家完成指定的獎勵廣告流程後，系統才發放事前承諾的獎勵。獎勵廣告按鈕必須和一般的繼續、重試、領取及返回大廳按鈕清楚區分。

避免遊戲中的誤觸

未來的廣告位置必須遠離遊玩畫面、拖曳區、滑動區、快速點擊按鈕、商店，以及遊戲控制、獎勵、金幣、鑽石、升級與關卡按鈕等容易誤觸的位置。

提供家長回報管道

每一個未來廣告位置都必須先在手機與電腦版面檢查。家長與訪客可以透過聯絡頁面回報不清楚或不安全的廣告位置。

家長常見問題

一般展示廣告會改變遊戲獎勵嗎？

不會。一般展示廣告不會提供金幣、鑽石、道具、升級、額外關卡、復活、重試或每日獎勵；基本遊玩與遊戲進度也不需要與一般展示廣告互動。

兒童遊玩時會在操作區看到廣告嗎？

不會。WeightPlay Kids 大廳與所有 Kids 遊戲完全不包含一般展示廣告、獎勵廣告、觀看廣告提示、廣告請求或空白廣告預留區；Main、Stage、Battle 與 Battle 結算狀態都適用。

不看獎勵廣告會無法繼續遊戲嗎？

不會。一般遊戲中的獎勵廣告只能提供事前說明的額外福利；玩家不觀看廣告，仍可保留原本取得的獎勵並正常繼續遊戲流程。

遊戲底下的空白區域已經是廣告嗎？

不是。未來廣告預留區在通過指定位置審查以前只是無互動空間，不包含廣告單元、點擊功能、獎勵或遊戲用途。

廣告驗證狀態

WeightPlay 已加入 AdSense 帳戶驗證中繼標記、驗證指令碼，以及根目錄的 ads.txt 發布商紀錄。這些項目只用來驗證網站擁有權與發布商身分，不代表廣告位置已獲核准，也不會自行建立或顯示廣告。

目前 WeightPlay 沒有啟用任何廣告版位，也沒有已核准的即時廣告位置。未來任何廣告位置都必須先通過頁面區隔、間距、手機版面與親子安全檢查，才可以正式加入。
