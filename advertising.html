Advertising Principles

WeightPlay may use normal display advertising in the future. Before any ad placement is added, the layout must keep ads clearly separate from gameplay, rewards, and child-facing controls.

Clear Separation

Ads should never look like game cards, inventory items, upgrade choices, stage buttons, daily gifts, coins, diamonds, or result rewards. Players should always be able to tell what is game content and what is advertising.

No Reward Confusion

Normal display ads are not part of the WeightPlay reward system. Players should not be asked to interact with normal ads to receive coins, diamonds, items, upgrades, stages, revives, retries, or progress.

Gameplay Safety

Future ad spaces must stay away from active play areas, drag controls, swipe areas, rapid-tap buttons, shop panels, upgrade choices, result buttons, and other places where accidental taps are likely.

Family-Friendly Review

Each future ad placement should be reviewed on mobile and desktop before release. Parents and visitors can report confusing or unsafe placements through the Contact page.

Quick Questions for Families

Do normal display ads change game rewards?

No. Normal display ads never grant coins, diamonds, items, upgrades, extra stages, revives, retries, or daily rewards. Core play and game progress never require interacting with a normal display ad.

Can an ad appear while a child is playing?

Not in an active play area or beside game controls. Any future placement must remain outside the play viewport and away from drag, swipe, rapid-tap, shop, upgrade, result, and reward controls.

Does an empty space mean there is already an ad?

No. A future layout reserve is inert until a specific placement has passed review. It has no ad unit, click action, reward, or gameplay function.

Verification Status

WeightPlay has added the AdSense account verification meta tag, verification script, and root ads.txt publisher record for review. These items only verify site ownership and publisher identity; they do not approve, create, or display live ad units.

No live ad placement is currently approved on WeightPlay. Any future ad slot must pass the separation, spacing, mobile, and family-safety checks described on this page before release.

廣告原則

WeightPlay 未來可能加入一般展示廣告。在加入任何廣告位置以前，版面必須讓廣告與遊戲操作、獎勵及兒童會使用的控制項清楚分開。

清楚區分廣告與遊戲內容

廣告不會設計成遊戲卡片、背包道具、升級選項、關卡按鈕、每日禮物、金幣、鑽石或結算獎勵。玩家應該能立即分辨遊戲內容與廣告。

一般展示廣告不提供遊戲獎勵

一般展示廣告不是 WeightPlay 的獎勵系統。玩家不需要點擊或觀看一般展示廣告來取得金幣、鑽石、道具、升級、額外關卡、復活、重試或遊戲進度。

避免遊戲中的誤觸

未來的廣告位置必須遠離遊玩畫面、拖曳區、滑動區、快速點擊按鈕、商店，以及遊戲控制、獎勵、金幣、鑽石、升級與關卡按鈕等容易誤觸的位置。

提供家長回報管道

每一個未來廣告位置都必須先在手機與電腦版面檢查。家長與訪客可以透過聯絡頁面回報不清楚或不安全的廣告位置。

家長常見問題

一般展示廣告會改變遊戲獎勵嗎？

不會。一般展示廣告不會提供金幣、鑽石、道具、升級、額外關卡、復活、重試或每日獎勵；基本遊玩與遊戲進度也不需要與一般展示廣告互動。

兒童遊玩時會在操作區看到廣告嗎？

不會放在遊玩區或操作按鈕旁。任何未來廣告位置都必須位於遊玩視窗之外，並遠離拖曳、滑動、快速點擊、商店、升級、結算與獎勵控制項。

遊戲底下的空白區域已經是廣告嗎？

不是。未來廣告預留區在通過指定位置審查以前只是無互動空間，不包含廣告單元、點擊功能、獎勵或遊戲用途。

廣告驗證狀態

WeightPlay 已加入 AdSense 帳戶驗證中繼標記、驗證指令碼，以及根目錄的 ads.txt 發布商紀錄。這些項目只用來驗證網站擁有權與發布商身分，不代表廣告位置已獲核准，也不會自行建立或顯示廣告。

目前 WeightPlay 沒有啟用任何廣告版位，也沒有已核准的即時廣告位置。未來任何廣告位置都必須先通過頁面區隔、間距、手機版面與親子安全檢查，才可以正式加入。
