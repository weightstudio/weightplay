Balance Grove

Help Taro steady three forest bridges. Read each target, choose a set of named stones that adds to it, and check your balance.

Subset-sum puzzleWeight-balance puzzleStone-selection puzzleCasual logic game

Mission

Balance the stones on each grove's scale to steady its bridge. The visible targets are 5, 7, and 9.

Play loop

Tap or click stones to select a non-empty set, then choose Check. A wrong total clears the set safely so you can try another combination.

Three groves

The first grove uses stone values 1–4 and targets 5. The second uses 1–5 and targets 7. The third uses 1–6 and targets 9. A correct check advances and unlocks the next grove.

A quick example

For target 5, an acorn worth 1 and a shell tile worth 5 would total 6, so that set is too heavy. Try one shell tile by itself, or another set totaling exactly 5.

A quiet design

There is no timer or lives system. Each check shows whether the selected values match the target; an incorrect selection clears for a fresh attempt.

Player and Save Information

Play with taps or a mouse; no account is needed. Only your best check counts are saved in localStorage in this browser, and your audio preference is saved locally. Clearing site data or switching browsers may remove these settings and records.

FAQ

What totals do the bridges need?
The three visible targets are 5, 7, and 9.
What are the stone values?
Acorn is 1, pebble 2, twig bundle 3, berry basket 4, shell tile 5, and firefly jar 6.
What happens after a wrong check?
The selected set clears safely, so you can choose a different non-empty set and check again.
Is there a timer or lives limit?
No. The game has no timer or lives system.
What is saved?
Only best check counts and the audio preference are saved locally in this browser. Site-data clearing or a browser switch may remove them.
