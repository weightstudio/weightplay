Courtyard Dispatch

Fit each tour into a time when every stop has room.

Game guide

Goal

Fit each tour into a time when every stop has room.

How to play

Choose a ticket, then choose a start time.

Rules

Each stop needs space. Guided tours share one guide. You can move a booking before opening the gates.

Results

Welcome all required groups to clear the stage. Fit in walk-ins for three stars.

Behind the game

Shuri Castle's main hall in Okinawa reopened on 23 November 2026 and the first day's timed tickets filled up. Here you assign visiting groups to slots at a fictional courtyard.

FAQ

Why is a free start time blocked?
A later stop can be full or the guide can be occupied. Select the time to see the conflict.
Can I fix a booking?
Select its ticket to move or remove it before opening the gates.
