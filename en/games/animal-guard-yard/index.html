Defend the garden

Stop the approaching enemies before they reach your home.

How to play

  1. Choose a guard, then an empty grass tile. Place a guard before sending the wave.
  2. Watch the incoming lanes. Use ranged guards behind blockers and collect energy to reinforce weak lanes.
  3. Between waves, recall and reposition your guards before starting again.

Tactics

Keep energy in reserve. Use Rally on a threatened lane; stone tiles cannot hold guards.
