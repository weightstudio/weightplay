Goal

Fire every piece the village has ordered. Match its glaze tile, seals and firing heat.

How to play

Tap a mineral to move one tile in the arrow direction. The heat changes with the mineral. At the gold diamond, press Stamp before continuing.

Controls and rules

All orders share the same stock. Use coal and the vent to adjust heat, undo a move or rework the piece. After stage 30, shelf choice changes the firing heat and the next piece’s starting heat.

Finishing and feedback

Once every order is delivered, the next stage opens. Replay to use fewer minerals, or earn new workshop tools at stages 10, 20 and 40.

Questions

How do I reach the gold diamond?
Use the mineral arrows to move onto it, then press Stamp. A second diamond appears only after the first seal is placed.
Can I change which order I fill first?
When an order is selectable, choose it before adding any materials. Keep scarce minerals for the pieces that need them.
Why does firing reject a piece on the right tile?
Read the firing temperature and the seal count. The shelf changes the final heat; coal and the vent can close the remaining gap.
Are diamonds required?
No. Every order can be filled with standard tools. Diamonds only buy optional permanent kiln finishes.
