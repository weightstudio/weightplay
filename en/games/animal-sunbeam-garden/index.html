Sunbeam Garden: Mirror Paths

Guide sunlight through mirrors to awaken the garden’s plant.

How to play

  1. Follow the arrow at each light source. Light travels straight until a mirror turns it.
  2. Tap a mirror to switch between / and \. Trace the new path before turning the next mirror.
  3. Reach the plant with every light source in the same arrangement. A beam leaving the board or looping does not complete the garden.

Hint highlights a mirror without turning it. Undo restores your previous turn; Reset restarts this garden. Clear it to unlock the next garden.

FAQ

Why did the lotus not wake?
Every active beam must enter its exact cell; one completed beam is not enough on a dual-source garden.
Can light pass through a mirror?
No, a reached mirror always reflects according to its current state.
Can a beam loop?
Yes; rotate the first mirror that repeats that route.
Does Hint finish the board?
No, it corrects one useful route mirror.
