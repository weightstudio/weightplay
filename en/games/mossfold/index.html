Goal

Collect all dewdrops, then place the whole block upright in the square nest. The same rules apply on phone and desktop.

How to play

Use the arrows or swipe the board. A standing block tips across two tiles. Collect the dewdrop, then enter the nest standing. Undo is always free.

Controls and rules

Arrow keys or WASD move. Swipe or use the four direction buttons on touch. Space switches cores, J joins, Z undoes. No timer or lives.

A hollow square plate responds to either footprint. A cross plate needs the whole block standing. Entering a plate toggles its bridge; leaving does not.

Pale glassleaf tiles support a lying block or a small core. A whole standing block falls through.

Stand on the glowing fork to separate. Switch between A and B, then bring them beside each other and choose Join cores. A small core cannot press a heavy cross or finish alone.

Numbered dewdrops are collected in order: 1, then 2, then 3. A later dewdrop waits until its turn.

Finishing and feedback

A clear saves the next bridge and your best result. Result offers Stages, Next stage and Replay. A replay can improve your best moves; it does not award the same block again.

Progress and optional appearances

Each fifth clear earns a bridge miniature. The free Mosswood appearance and optional permanent Amberwood can be chosen from Wood cores. Purchases use the shared wallet and never alter puzzle difficulty.

Questions

Do I need diamonds to solve a bridge?
No. All sixty bridges, Undo, Hint and both-core controls are free. Amberwood changes appearance only.
Why did the block fall?
Every occupied tile needs support. Closed bridges and empty spaces cannot carry it; glassleaf tiles cannot carry the whole block standing.
How do the two cores rejoin?
Move them to neighbouring tiles, then choose Join cores. The joining direction sets the long block’s footprint.
Where is progress stored?
Progress stays in this browser. Clearing site data may remove it; there is no account transfer.
