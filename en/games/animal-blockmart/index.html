Animal Blockmart

Keep your neighborhood store stocked and help each sale fund the next improvement.

Goal

Build a stocked, varied animal shop. Reinvest paid sales in supplies, staff, equipment and a store up to22×22.

How to play

  1. Choose products on each shelf and order stock. Check unit cost and sale price; cold goods need a cooler.
  2. Bought fixtures enter Warehouse. Choose a valid tile and confirm placement. Storing and placing owned fixtures again is free.
  3. Keep stock money aside, hire staff, buy useful team upgrades and expand10→13→16→19→22. Landmarks are optional.

Controls and rules

Drag to pan. Enable Rotate camera to rotate by dragging; wheel or pinch to zoom. R rotates a selected fixture. Cancel or Escape cancels pending placement; owned fixtures remain in Warehouse.

Finishing and feedback

Paid sales increase cash and complete shop goals. Keep operating after the goals, or use Return to Main to pause. Progress stays on this device and resumes when you open the shop.

Where is my shop saved?
Your shop is saved in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data can remove it.
Does the shop have stages?
This is one continuous shop. Reinvest paid sales to grow it. Return to the menu to pause, then open the shop to continue.
How do I buy more stock?
Use a product’s order button in Shelves. Orders spend shop cash at the displayed cost, so keep money for your next delivery.
