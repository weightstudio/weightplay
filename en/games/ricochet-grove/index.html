Game guide

Seed drop puzzle

Turn pegs, collect water and bounce seeds into the flowerbeds in order.

Choose a launcher and check the predicted bed. Turn a peg for one water, then drop the seed. A fresh dew peg gives one water. Gold vanes move a seed two spaces; blue hinges flip after contact.

Can I finish without diamonds?

Yes. Clear stages to unlock tools. Diamonds buy permanent appearances. Progress is saved in this browser.
