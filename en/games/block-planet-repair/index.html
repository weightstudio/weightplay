How to restore a planet

Goal

Sweep across repair robots, bring five planets to life and hatch block animals.

How to play

Sweep across a ready robot with your mouse, or slide or tap on a touch screen. It repairs nearby land and moves to a new tile.

Controls and rules

Earn energy to recruit and upgrade robots. Unlock new planets by restoring the previous one. Choose a forest, ocean or city once restoration reaches 25%.

Progress and rewards

Discover fragments and hatch eggs. Your game saves on this device after each repair. Automatic repair works while you are playing.
