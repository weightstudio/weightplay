The moonlit marsh

Nori is bringing the marsh back to life, one little shower at a time. Read the wind and move beneath the glowing drops.

How to play

Drag or tap the water to steer. On a keyboard, use ← → or physical lanes 1–5. The glowing landing marker shows where the next drop will arrive.

Your mission

Meet the drop target before the shower ends. Some stages also require gold, a streak or a leaf limit. Missing rain breaks your streak; leaves break it too. Every shower is free to retry.

One star for the mission, a second for catching at least 85%, and a third for a perfect shower with no missed rain or leaves.

Your nest

Six regions introduce wind, leaf corridors, golden routes and precision showers. Earn stars, unlock the next stage and choose a basket that suits your route.

Local progress

Progress, equipment and your old best stay in this browser. No account is needed. Another device starts its own save. If saving fails, the game tells you.
