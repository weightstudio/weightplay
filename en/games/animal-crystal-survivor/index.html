How the Systems Work

The displayed objective determines how a run ends: survive the countdown, clear the required waves, or defeat the boss before time runs out.

XP crystals fill the battle experience bar. Leveling up pauses the action so you can choose a spell upgrade for that run.

Battle rewards also raise your permanent adventure level and provide talent points. Fully upgrade a prerequisite to reach the next talent; adventure growth opens more element branches. You can equip one pet and reset spent talent points for free.

How to Play

  1. Select an unlocked stage and read its objective.
  2. Drag the virtual joystick to move and release to stop, or use WASD / arrow keys. Attacks fire automatically.
  3. Move out of attack warnings, collect XP crystals, and choose an upgrade when the selection appears.
  4. After the result, return to the Rune workshop to spend permanent talent points or change your equipped pet before the next run.

Strategy Tips

  • Root prison: leave the three green circles.
  • Prism fan: sidestep the five aimed bolts; attack after the shield fades.
  • Briar charge: leave the fixed charge line before the rush.
  • Meteor barrage: dodge three staggered fire impacts.
  • Thunder cross: move diagonally out of both blue lanes.
  • Eclipse wave: escape the expanding purple ring.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the stage objectives?
Read the objective shown before the run: survive the countdown, clear the required waves, or defeat the boss before the deadline.
Does magic attack automatically?
Yes. Move to avoid threats and collect XP crystals; choose a spell when you level up while attacks target enemies automatically.
Is progress saved?
Stage progress and talents are saved in this browser. Clearing browser data removes the save.
How do lasting talents work?
Battle XP raises your adventure level and makes talent points available. Spend points after meeting branch prerequisites; Reset returns allocated points so you can choose again.
