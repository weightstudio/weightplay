World and Mission

Each chapter contains four workshop platforms. A successful landing advances the canister and extends the streak.

The challenge is matching each released swipe to the platform's allowed force range. A miss returns you to that platform so you can adjust and retry.

How the Systems Work

Press the canister and drag upward in one continuous gesture. Swipe length changes launch force and flight; success is checked against the current platform's allowed force range when you release.

A successful landing advances to the next platform and extends your streak. A miss opens Retry for that platform or Stages to return to the chapter map.

How to Play

  1. Choose a chapter from the foundry map and wait for the ready cue.
  2. Press the canister and drag upward in one smooth gesture. Swipe length changes the force of the launch.
  3. Release to launch. Success depends on whether the released force falls within the current platform's allowed range.
  4. After a miss, choose Retry and adjust the swipe force. Clear four platforms to finish the chapter.

Strategy Tips

  • Start with a controlled upward swipe and use the landing result to learn the current platform's force range.
  • After a miss, change the swipe length a little and compare the next result; distance does not set the success rule.
  • Keep the gesture mostly vertical, then tune force from the platform's feedback.

Campaign and Difficulty Growth

The foundry has six chapters with four platforms each. Every successful landing advances to the next platform and extends the streak; a miss offers a retry on the same platform or a return to the chapter map.

Developer Design Note

Where Sketchwheel Rally asks players to draw a wheel that changes how a race handles terrain, Flip Foundry centers on calibrating one upward swipe to the current platform’s launch-force range. Touch and mouse use the same gesture, and the immediate landing result lets players adjust the next attempt on that platform.

Player and Save Information

Flip Foundry is a short general-audience browser game; no account or purchase is required. Only your best landing streak is saved in this browser. Your current streak ends with the active run, and clearing site data may erase the saved best. WeightPlay may use page and gameplay analytics as described in its Privacy Policy.

FAQ

How do I launch the canister?
Press the canister and drag upward in one continuous gesture; swipe length changes launch force.
What determines a successful landing?
When you release, the swipe's force must fall within the current platform's allowed range. Rotation is visual feedback, not a separate angle check.
Can I use a mouse?
Yes. Mouse dragging and touch swiping use the same launch gesture.
Is progress saved?
Only your best landing streak is saved in this browser. The current streak lasts only for the active run.
How many surfaces are in a chapter?
Each of the six chapters has four workshop platforms. A successful landing advances to the next one; a miss can be retried.
What should I adjust after a miss?
Change the swipe length slightly to tune the released force, then compare the result on that same platform.
Can I retry the same platform?
Yes. Choose Retry after a miss to replay that platform, or choose Stages to return to the chapter map.
What does the streak count?
It counts consecutive successful upright landings in the current run. Your best streak is kept in this browser.
