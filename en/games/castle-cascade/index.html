How to Play

Swap two neighboring gems to make a line of three or more. Clear every target before your moves run out. A swap without a match is free.

Power Pieces

Four in a row makes an Arrow. A T or L makes a Bomb. A 2×2 square makes a Bird. Five in a row makes a Prism. Swapping two power pieces combines them.

Thirty Castle Stages

Explore six chapters with crates, chains, keys, color gates, stone blocks, and seals. Keys fall with the gems and count only when they reach an exit.

Strategy

Plan matches near blockers. Arrows clear a row or column, Bombs clear a square, Birds seek useful targets, and Prisms clear a color. Stone needs a power-piece hit. If no move remains, gems reshuffle for free.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a rejected swap use a move?
No. If the swap makes no match, the gems return and your move stays.
What if no legal match remains?
The movable gems reshuffle for free. Blockers stay in place.
Is Castle Cascade based on another game's art or levels?
No. Castle Cascade uses original characters, stages, rules, and code.
