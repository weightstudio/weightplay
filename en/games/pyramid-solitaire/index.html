Pyramid Solitaire

Clear the pyramid by pairing exposed cards that total 13.

How to play

  1. Tap two available cards whose values add to 13. A=1, J=11, Q=12; K clears alone.
  2. A pyramid card is available only when no other cards rest on it. The top waste card can also form a pair.
  3. Draw from the stock when needed. Clearing a pair can expose new choices; drawing breaks the current combo.

Hint points out a move; Undo reverses a move. Restart repeats this deal; New Game deals another.

Score, Stock, and recovery

A standard 52-card deck deals 28 face-up cards into seven pyramid rows and leaves 24 in the Stock. Only uncovered pyramid cards and the top Waste card are usable. Kings can clear alone, including a King on top of the Waste.

Score counts pyramid cards removed, up to 28: a pyramid pair adds two, while a pyramid–Waste pair or pyramid King adds one. Each clear extends Combo; drawing a Stock card ends that combo. There is no Stock recycle.

Hint marks the first legal clear or pair in board order; if none is available while Stock remains, it points to Stock. It is a legal suggestion, not a best-move guarantee. Undo restores the previous move. Restart repeats the same deal; New Game deals another. You win by clearing all 28 pyramid cards, and lose when Stock is empty and no legal clear remains.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a card unavailable?
A remaining card still covers it, so it is not exposed yet.
What makes a pair?
The two ranks must total 13; a King clears alone.
Can I use the Waste?
Yes. Pair the Waste card with an exposed pyramid card when their ranks total 13.
What happens when Stock is empty?
No more fresh cards are available, so the visible pyramid route must finish the deal.
Can I undo?
Yes. Undo restores the complete prior state.
How is Score counted?
Score is the number of pyramid cards removed, up to 28. A pair of two pyramid cards adds two; a pyramid–Waste pair or pyramid King adds one.
Does Hint choose the best move?
No. It marks the first legal clear or pair in board order, or Stock if no clear is available and cards remain. You still make the move.
When does the deal end?
Clearing every pyramid card wins even if Stock remains. With Stock empty and no legal clear, the deal ends in a loss; Undo, Restart, or New Game can help you try again.
Is the current deal saved when I reload?
No. The live deal and Undo history are kept in the open page, not restored from an online save. Restart within the page repeats the current deal.
Can I continue the deal on another device?
No. There is no account or cloud transfer for a live deal; another browser or device starts its own page state.
