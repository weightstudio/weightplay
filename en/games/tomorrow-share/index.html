Tomorrow's Share

Fund today's needs, invest in tomorrow, and keep every promise across the seasons.

Stage 1 / 60

Budget strategy · Seasonal planning

Goal

Cover every season, meet the reserve and special goals, and repay outstanding loans. Retry freely or return to the saved stage rail.

How to play

Read the seasonal forecast. Fund island needs or build stations, then close the season to see your plan take effect.

A station costs 3 and provides only 1 service per operating season. Building just before a closure can leave you paying for today and waiting for tomorrow. Compare its remaining useful seasons with immediate funding. Earmarked envelopes expire: use them for their own island rather than counting them toward your reserve. Undo changes within the current season; closing it commits the plan.

Controls and rules

Spend 1 for a service now. Build for 3 to receive one service each future season. Keep enough for announced needs and repayment.

Borrow 3 now and repay 4 two seasons later. Only one loan may be outstanding.

Build a relay for 2. Once per season, transfer unused station service to an unmet need. Earmarked funds can cover the source to free its service.

Two operating stations bring 1 partner grant with the next income. Announced closures count.

Finishing and feedback

A successful clear earns two stars, or three when its announced reserve challenge is met. A missed need, reserve floor or dated promise can end the attempt. The result explains the missing promise and offers Stages, Next Stage and Replay.

News inspiration

Canada released a new national budget on November 5, 2026, combining immediate affordability measures, long-term investment and trade diversification. This fictional island council turns that tension into choices between service today and capacity tomorrow.

Across two chapters

The 60 stages form two chapters of 30. Early seasons introduce funding, stations, earmarked envelopes and closures. Loans add a fixed repayment date; later building deadlines make waiting costly. From Stage 31, the relay moves spare station service between islands. Partner grants, minimum reserves and slower construction then interact with that transfer decision. Each stage starts from its own fair budget; earlier purchases never supply extra strength.

Design note

The council uses announced, turn-based budgets so the pressure comes from choosing priorities rather than fast tapping. The three island trays keep needs visible while the forecast exposes future constraints. There is no live news feed or real national budget simulation.

Player and save information

Tap or click the visible buttons. On desktop, 1–3 fund the islands and Z undoes a change. There is no reaction timer. Cleared stages, best stars, the current plan and chosen appearance stay in this browser. Clearing site storage may remove them. The game needs no sign-in; the shared diamond wallet handles optional permanent appearances.

Questions

Why does a new station produce nothing?

Build times: Homes 1–2, Workshops 1–2, Harbour 1–2 seasons. New stations do not serve immediately.

When is a loan repaid?

Borrow 3 now and repay 4 two seasons later. Only one loan may be outstanding.

What can the relay transfer?
