Build your squad, equip heroes and win automatic battles to advance through the campaign.

GameplayAuto-battle squad RPG
GenreAnimal Games · RPG · Strategy · Idle

How to Play

  1. Start the game: heroes fight automatically. Collect the drops that appear in the arena.
  2. Use the bottom menus to upgrade heroes, equip items and adjust the squad.
  3. After victory, choose Next. After defeat, strengthen the squad and retry.

Campaign and Difficulty Growth

Later stages increase enemy numbers, variety and strength. Upgrade the squad and equip collected items before retrying a difficult boss; entering a new chapter does not automatically grant heroes or equipment.

Player and Save Information

Unlocked stages, heroes, progress, and preferences are saved only in this browser. No account, purchase, online profile, or cloud save is required; clearing site data can remove the local campaign.

FAQ

Is combat automatic?
Yes. Heroes fight through each wave automatically, while you choose the squad, upgrades, equipment, and counters.
What should I upgrade after a loss?
Change one deliberate choice at a time: strengthen a hero, adjust equipment, or revise the squad, then use Retry to compare the result.
