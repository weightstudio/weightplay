The Cloudstep Isles have lost their connecting paths. Spark Paw Fia enters the Aurora Relay to restore each route while friendly rivals race to prove their own bridge plans.

How to play

  1. Tap, drag, or use the arrow keys to move Fia.
  2. Collect cyan tiles and carry them to any bridge dock.
  3. Collect cyan tiles, then walk across a lane to build beneath your feet. Cross the finish line to win. You can always walk back for more tiles.

Choose a route with intent

Middle lanes have two free stepping stones. Reclaiming a rival’s tile costs one tile. Compare the numbers at each bridge, save a full stack, and dash when the route is clear.
