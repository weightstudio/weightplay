How to play

Connected blocks disappear together; remaining blocks stay fixed. Actors fall, then approach the nearest enemy on a continuous platform (ties go left). Win only with greater power; absorb the displayed enemy power. Equal power loses.

Keys open matching gates, runes remove shields, and living banner guards strengthen their linked enemy. Read the visible bonus and boss conditions. Collect the crown after required guards and seals. Spikes destroy necessary targets.

Six castle chapters

Thirty authored puzzles teach routes, stone and spikes, keys, shields, banners and six different bosses. Clear a stage to unlock the next. Earn separate badges for a clear, no hints and the target move count. Free undo restores the entire last action.

Saved in this browser

Progress, badges, best moves and a stable unfinished run stay in this browser. No login, waiting energy or cross-stage power grinding. Restart does not erase unlocked stages.
