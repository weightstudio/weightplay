OWL ARCHITECT'S NIGHT GARDEN

Prism Route Garden

Plan long routes across an expanded garden. Every prism must connect and every open tile must glow.

Difficulty: Very HardSession: 4–10 min
WeightPlay Original Game Guide

How to Play

Connect every matching prism without crossing paths. Prism gates accept only their own color, and every open tile must be filled.

Core rules

  1. Drag from one prism to its matching partner.
  2. Routes move only up, down, left, or right and cannot share tiles.
  3. A marked prism gate can only be crossed by the matching color.
  4. Connect every pair and illuminate every open tile to finish.

A route may begin from either endpoint. If you redraw a color, its previous route is removed so the new plan becomes the only active one. Moving back over the immediately previous tile shortens the route by one step, which lets you correct a corner without clearing the whole board. Endpoints belonging to another color are blocked. A finished set of connections is still incomplete when even one ordinary tile remains dark, so always check both the Links and Filled counters before assuming the layout is solved.

Planning tips

  • Reserve narrow edges and gated corridors before filling the center.
  • Trace longer pairs first when they divide the board.
  • Use Undo or Hint without losing campaign progress.

Begin by reading the whole board rather than drawing the nearest pair. Gates reveal mandatory territory, edge endpoints have fewer legal exits, and a long route can divide the remaining space into separate regions. Sketch those commitments mentally first. After placing a route, check whether every unresolved endpoint still has an open entrance and whether the unused tiles remain connected enough for the remaining colors. When a plan closes a pocket, Undo the latest route and change its shape before disturbing routes that already fit.

Advanced campaign

Thirty authored layouts expand from 7×7 to 10×10, introduce more color pairs and add prism gates in six chapters.

All 30 boards now use fixed but heavily braided routes. Matching endpoints are usually far apart, so long paths and narrow regions must be planned before drawing. Gates begin in Garden 5 and are limited to one to three per board, acting as constraints instead of a map of the full answer.

Controls and feedback

On touch screens, press a prism and slide across adjacent tiles without lifting your finger. With a mouse, hold the primary button while tracing. Bright filled tiles show route ownership, circular crystal flowers mark endpoints, and outlined gate symbols show their reserved color. A red pulse means the next tile is illegal because it is occupied, belongs to another endpoint, or has a different gate color. Undo restores the last board snapshot, Reset clears the current attempt, and Hint reveals one verified unresolved route.

Save and access

Progress stays in this browser. No account or purchase is required, and advertising is not currently active.

Only campaign unlock progress is saved; unfinished paths are not carried between visits or after returning to Stages. This keeps each attempt predictable and prevents an old partial route from appearing unexpectedly. Sound can be toggled from the game controls, and every essential state also has visible text or color-independent structure. The General layout reserves a separate 56-pixel future-banner band below Stage and Battle, but it contains no active advertisement and never covers game controls.

Questions and route recovery

Paths cannot cross or share a normal tile, and diagonal movement is never allowed. A gate is not an extra endpoint; it is a required color reservation along a possible route. Hints are unlimited and do not spend coins, diamonds, or other currency. If every pair is connected but the Result does not appear, inspect the Filled counter and look for one dark tile. If a route seems trapped, shorten it by dragging back one tile, use Undo for the previous board snapshot, or Reset to rebuild the garden from a clean board.
