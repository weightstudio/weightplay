Triple Match

Mimi's greenhouse shelves are overflowing with enchanted keepsakes. Only uncovered objects can move into the tray. Three matching objects vanish, but seven unmatched objects end the attempt.

How to play

  1. Tap an uncovered object to move it into the tray.
  2. Collect three matching objects to clear them automatically.
  3. Clear the complete pile before all seven tray slots are occupied.

Changing shelves

Later shelves add vines, crystal shells, hidden treasures and shifting piles. Read the shelf rule before choosing a trio.

Plan before tapping

A tempting object can uncover a useful match—or bury the tray in singletons. Look for pairs, track the top layers, and keep one emergency slot open.

Progress and privacy

Cleared stages, stars, and best tray space stay in this browser. No login or personal information is needed.

FAQ

Why can't I choose a treasure?
Another active object still covers its selection area.
When does a trio disappear?
As soon as the third matching treasure enters the tray.
Do empty spaces remain after a trio?
No. The remaining treasures shift together automatically.
