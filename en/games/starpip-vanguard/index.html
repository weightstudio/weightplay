Four companions, four dice

Fia, Taro, Orla and Nori patrol a chain of astronomical terraces. Each companion has their own health and a six-sided die: Fia favours attacks, Taro shields, Orla mana and ranged shots, and Nori healing. Their actions can protect any living teammate. An enemy’s forecast names who it will attack and how much damage it will deal. Your task is to defeat every sentry in an expedition while keeping enough of the team standing for the next encounter. There is no movement grid or timed decision: you choose the order of actions.

From the first roll to camp

Start opens the stage rail. Tap an available stage card to enter; drag the rail to browse. Every turn begins with four rolled dice, fewer if a companion has fallen. Tap useful dice to keep them and reroll the rest at most twice. Use dice commits the result. Select an attack and then an enemy, or a shield or healing die and then a companion. For mana, select the die and press Gain mana. Each living companion uses its die once. End turn lets the surviving enemies carry out their announced intentions. Defeating an enemy cancels its attack without moving it to another target.

Protect the person who needs it

Fia begins with 12 health, Taro 16, Orla 10 and Nori 12. Shields absorb incoming damage and disappear after the enemy phase. Healing cannot exceed maximum health or revive a fallen companion during a fight. A fallen companion loses their die until camp. Poison deals health damage after enemy actions and bypasses shields; Cleanse removes it. Undo lets you rearrange actions and spell spending within the current turn, but cannot rewind a revealed reroll or the enemy phase. You can test an order, reconsider, and commit without farming a better random outcome.

One reserve, two competing spells

The team shares a mana reserve capped at 8. Arc costs 2 and deals 3 damage to one enemy. Ward costs 3 and gives 2 shield to every living companion. Mana lasts through turns but resets between encounters. Spending on Arc may remove an enemy before it attacks; saving for Ward can cover several announced targets. From stage 31, actually blocking damage also earns 1 focus per enemy phase, up to 3. Each encounter begins with 2 focus. Spend 2 to advance one unused die to its next face, once per turn. Selecting a mana die does not spend it, so you can turn it first.

Build a plan between encounters

Regular expeditions have two encounters; guardian stages have three. Between them, choose 4 health for everyone or a temporary item. If both item slots are full, choose which item to replace. Fallen companions return at camp with half health before the next fight. Guardian clears unlock permanent blueprints at stages 5,10,15,20,25,30 and 35. Equip any two freely outside battle. Star fang bypasses armour; Moss buckler reflects blocked attacks; Moon mirror increases mana dice; Tide vial adds cleansing to healing; Twin feathers spreads ranged damage; Ember holder rewards the first defeat; Stabilizer keeps an extra die on the first reroll. Camp finds last only for the current expedition.

Learn the sentries before the guardians

The 60 stages form two 30-stage chapters with 12 different guardians. Early stages teach armour, healing enemies and the cost of chasing a better roll. Later encounters combine thorns, poison, reinforcements and mana drain. Chapter 2 adds shared-damage relays, anchored dice, spell reflection, shifted targets and support seals. A guardian is not only a larger health bar: the Gate Ring opens to shields, the Moon Arbiter needs melee then ranged attacks to break its layers, and the Oath Standard changes its required support action each turn. Tap an enemy before selecting a die, or open Help, to read its exact rule.

A shield is not always the best defence

In the opening encounter, one sentry threatens Orla and another threatens Nori. Keeping Taro’s shield can protect Orla, while keeping Fia’s attack preserves a way to remove a threat. If you combine a strike with Arc and defeat the first sentry, its announced hit disappears entirely. Spending that mana on Ward instead protects both targets but leaves both enemies alive. Against a Mirror guard, that first Arc reflects onto Orla: use a ranged die or plan the reflection before committing. The right choice depends on the current faces, health and enemy rules.

Why the patrol uses short expeditions

We designed each stage as a small chain of fights so that recovery and equipment matter without requiring one uninterrupted campaign. Each stage restarts at full team health with its chosen permanent equipment. Fixed stage seeds make a retry useful for learning; selective rerolls and action order still determine how you use those results. Touch, mouse and keyboard activate the same dice and targets. The 3D terrace keeps the four companions visible while the cards state exact health, shields and intentions. There is no purchase that increases damage or removes an enemy rule.

Player and save information

Play is free and no sign-in is required. This browser stores cleared stages, equipment, selected appearance and the last completed action of an unfinished expedition. Reload and choose the same stage to resume or restart. Clearing site data or changing browser can remove that progress. A save warning keeps Next stage disabled until saving succeeds. Free dice appearances unlock after 5, 15 and 40 clears; the optional Aurora appearance costs 8 shared diamonds and changes dice colour only. The shared wallet records that permanent purchase separately from this game’s progress. Each fight has a 12-turn limit, or 18 on guardian stages; losing all companions or reaching that limit ends the attempt, with free replay.

Patrol questions

Does keeping a die use it?
No. Keeping protects its current face from a reroll. Use dice ends the reroll phase; you still choose the order and targets.
Why did my shield disappear?
Shields protect during the current enemy phase. Any unused shield expires when the next turn begins.
Can I reroll after using a die?
No. Use dice ends the reroll phase. Undo changes your action order and spell spending, but never reveals a new random roll.
How do I gain focus?
From stage 31, absorb real damage with a shield to gain 1 focus per enemy phase, capped at 3. Spend 2 to advance an unused die one face, once per turn.
Are camp items permanent?
No. A camp item lasts for that expedition. Guardian blueprints remain available for free equipment selection outside battle; only two items fit.
Why is a guardian taking no damage?
Read its rule in Help or tap it before selecting a die. Some need a support action, a particular attack type, or their escorts defeated first.
