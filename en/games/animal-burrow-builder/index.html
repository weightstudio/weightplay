How to play

Every room must connect to the entrance through an edge. Tunnels are cheap and can be upgraded, paying only the difference.

Place pantries directly beside bedrooms. Gardens need a neighbouring stream; bridges are the only way to build on water. Nurseries must touch a bedroom.

Complete the checklist, then invite the residents. Earn extra stars by building over the crystal and staying within the timber target. Undo and restart are free; hints never spend diamonds.

Arrow keys: move · Enter: build · 1–6: tools · Z: undo

Three-star target

  • A complete home
  • Within timber target
  • Unearth the crystal
