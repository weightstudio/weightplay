Smallwood Lives

Smallwood Lives is a turn-based life story game for mouse, touch and keyboard. There is no timer: the world waits for your decisions. Thirty chapters explore friendship, promises, moving home, teaching and leaving something behind.

How to shape a woodland life

  1. Choose an unlocked chapter. Each life starts with its own animal, friends, resources and daily opportunities.
  2. Spend up to two actions per season. Read the resource preview, then confirm. Rest restores energy; exploration gathers materials and nature; crafting develops skill.
  3. At seasons 2, 6 and 10, answer a life event. Later echoes record what you actually chose. A promise needs an action before its deadline.
  4. After twelve seasons, save the life to your album. The next chapter opens even if its optional wish was not fulfilled.

Actions

Friendship, craft and nature stop at 3; energy stops at 6 and materials at 9. Costs and requirements are shown before confirmation. Moving changes who is nearby. One promise extension adds two seasons, only when the new deadline fits within this life.

Collection

Each chapter has a permanent keepsake with a wish variant. Its album page holds three lives; replacing one preserves discovered branches. Every five chapters opens a free room with five display slots. Diamonds buy only permanent decorations: they never buy stronger choices or extra years.

Chapters

  1. Fern village — Make a flute together and reach friendship A 2.
  2. River bend — Reach friendship B 2 while keeping A at 1.
  3. Hill market — Teach a skill at level 2.
  4. Sea breeze — Reach craft 2 and build the workshop.
  5. Cedar valley — Build shelter and retain at least 2 materials.
  6. Starlight square — Choose a listener, teach them and keep a memory.

How to play

Can I lose a life?
There is no death roll or wealth ranking. Advancing through all twelve seasons completes the chapter. An unmet wish is an alternative story, not a failed life.
Can I resume?
Choices are saved on this browser before the scene changes. Continue resumes the exact choice. Clearing browser data removes local progress; another tab may ask you to reload.
Does replay change the beginning?
Same beginning replays the recorded seed. Selecting a completed chapter starts a fresh life. A seed fixes the beginning and daily opportunities, not your decisions.
Is this connected to 1 in 8 Billion?
Creative inspiration comes from 《80億分之1》 (1 in 8 Billion) and its exploration of different starting points and choices. Smallwood Lives is an original game independently made by WeightPlay, with no affiliation, partnership or authorization from the original work.
