Your goal

Complete each harbor’s timber and meal deliveries, meet its special contract and keep morale at three or higher after the required weather. Each settlement starts with its own terrain, supplies and forecast.

How to play

Place workshops on open sites and assign your limited residents. Lumber shacks gather wood, berry plots gather food, sawmills turn wood into planks and kitchens cook meals. One meal feeds two residents; raw berries feed one each. Press Next day when the plan is ready. Nothing advances while you consider a choice.

Controls and rules

Tap a site or use the keyboard to select it. Build, assign or recall workers through the fixed management controls. Deliveries really remove stock, so a full contract can leave dinner short. Cottages provide two beds. Storms need fuel; exploring opens resources and rescue choices but unresolved events make storms harder.

Finishing and feedback

Supply every contract and weather requirement to open the next harbor. Running out of morale or time ends only the current attempt. The completed board remains visible before the result; Harbors, Next harbor and Replay stay in the same positions. Cleared harbors remain available.

A different harbor each time

Sixty authored harbors form two chapters. The first combines labor, cooking, shelter and expeditions. From Harbor 31, ferries connect separated banks and pumps keep nearby floodplains productive. Later barriers exchange building materials for a worker-free flood defense. The three free permanent charters favor different plans; optional diamond appearances change the actual harbor without affecting production.

Player and save information

This is a single-player browser game with touch and keyboard controls. Progress, the selected charter and the current settlement are saved on this browser. A private window or cleared site storage may not retain them. Saving errors keep the current session available and do not reset the shared diamond wallet. There is no required login, purchase or real-time waiting.

Plan a useful reserve

Before shipping meals, check dinner and the next storm. Recalling a sawmill worker for one day can preserve wood for the harbor fire. A kitchen beside a cottage gives the first cook an extra meal, which may free a worker for a rescue. A ferry opens a distant workshop only while a usable connection remains.

Questions about the harbor

Why did food disappear after delivery?

A shipment spends the shown stock. Residents still eat at the next day’s settlement. Keep meals or berries in reserve.

Why is a distant workshop idle?

Water can separate its bank from the harbor. Build a ferry on a connecting water site and check the route again.

Can I change my charter?

After clearing Harbor 5, choose one of three free permanent charters in the Harbors screen. Change it freely before the next settlement.

Do appearances change the challenge?

No. Copper roofs and tide-blue sails are permanent optional visual choices. Every harbor is designed for the standard charter and requires no diamond spending.
