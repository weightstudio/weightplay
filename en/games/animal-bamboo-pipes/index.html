GameplayPipe-connection puzzle
Campaign30 waterways · 6 chapters
InputTouch, mouse, or keyboard

How to play

  1. Choose an unlocked waterway. Tap a pipe to turn it clockwise; matching openings carry water between neighboring tiles.
  2. Trace the route from the spring to the flower basin. A gap stops the flow. Complete the connection to unlock the next waterway.

Undo reverses your last turn. Hint marks a pipe and its next connection; you still rotate it yourself. Restart restores this puzzle. Replay cleared waterways to improve your turn count. Progress stays in this browser.

Read the waterway

Every tile shows a bamboo shape and its open ends. Follow the wet path from the spring to the flower basin, then inspect the next gap before turning a pipe.

Plan the route

A turn changes the pipe in place, so use the visible neighbors to decide whether the route should continue, bend, or stop. A complete connection is the goal; speed is optional.

Campaign and checkpoints

Thirty authored waterways are arranged across six chapters, with checkpoints at waterways 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30. Clearing a waterway opens the next one while cleared routes remain replayable.

Assist, save, and replay

Undo reverses the last turn, Hint names a useful unresolved pipe without turning it for you, and Restart restores the current puzzle. Unlocks and best turn counts stay in this browser only.

FAQ

How many waterways are included?
There are 30 authored waterways across six chapters, with checkpoints at 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30.
What does one pipe turn do?
It rotates the selected bamboo pipe clockwise so its openings can match neighboring tiles.
What happens if the route is broken?
Water stops at the gap; inspect the neighboring openings, turn a pipe, or use Undo and try a different route.
Does Hint solve the puzzle?
No. Hint identifies a useful pipe and route cue, but you still choose and make every rotation.
Where is progress saved?
Unlocked waterways and best turn counts are saved only in this browser; there is no account or cloud save.
