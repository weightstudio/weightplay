Bankshot Rink

Skate for the puck, bank it off the boards, and open timed goals before the defender closes in.

Game guide

Ice hockeyBank shots

Goal

Put the puck through the lit goal before time runs out.

How to play

Tap the ice to skate. When you have the puck, shoot straight or bank off either board.

Controls and rules

Recover loose pucks by skating to them. A locked goal opens when the puck hits a lit board. On a keyboard, move with the arrow keys and shoot with 1, 2 or 3.

Finish and rewards

Reach the target to unlock the next rink. Finish quickly with fewer turnovers for more stars.

When should I bank a shot?

Aim at a lit board when the gate is shut or a defender is in the direct line.

How do I get the puck back?

Tap ahead of the loose puck to meet it as it slows.
