Your goal

Deliver every scheduled passenger without sustained overcrowding.

Plan your lines

Choose a line and tap stations to connect them. Trams run both ways. Passengers change lines only at a shared station. Check the forecast and assign a tram to every route you need. Redraw only between periods; moving a tram takes time and leaves its passengers safely at the station.

Plan

Each row shows origin → destination and arrival time. A shared station permits transfers. The next period can open new stations.

Gate

A closed tide gate holds the tram before the crossing. Use a ticket to open it for eight seconds, or skip one stop. Skipping a stop never delivers its passengers. Later gates also require the correct direction.

Choosing a fleet

All 60 shifts are free to play. Clearing shifts 10, 20 and 40 unlocks the Shuttle, Carriage and Tide runner. Choose speed, seats or tide tolerance before departure. Optional copper and moon appearances change only the body color.

Collection

Cleared shifts and chosen fleets are stored in this browser. Clearing site data may remove them. No sign-in is needed. Optional appearances use the shared diamond wallet.

An evening in the bay

Orla, the moon-capped owl, dispatches the bay’s copper trams. Harbor workers, gardeners and students share little island platforms. Their destination symbol determines where they need to go; the nearest station is not always the right one.

Designing a working interchange

A short direct line is fast but needs a shared station for transfers. A long line saves vehicles but makes everyone wait longer. Limited bridge spans and growing stations make you reconsider the network between periods.

Questions and answers

Why does a passenger wait despite a connected line?

A drawn line needs an assigned tram. Passengers may also wait for a transfer, a returning tram or an open tide gate. Check the destination symbol and the forecast.

Does skipping a stop deliver passengers?

No. The passenger stays on board and must reach a matching station on a later stop. The ticket is spent even if the shortcut does not help.
