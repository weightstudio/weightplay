Stararc Rendezvous

Fit your shuttle, plan a transfer, and dock with moving stations before the shift ends.

Game guide

Your goal

Meet every marked station and deliver its cargo before the shift ends.

How to fly

Pick a flight kit. Compare each command’s predicted ring, sector, time and fuel. Coast or transfer to catch a moving station.

Controls and rules

Transfers reach an adjacent ring and must be stabilized. Dock when the ship and station share the same ring and sector and the port is open. Watch for debris and shade.

Finishing a shift

Finish all docks to unlock the next shift. Replay for a better fuel result or try a different flight kit. Retries are free.

Why can’t I dock yet?

The ship must be stable and share the station’s ring and sector during an open beat.

Do I need diamonds to complete the campaign?

No. Kits unlock by clearing shifts. Diamonds are only for a permanent cosmetic finish.
