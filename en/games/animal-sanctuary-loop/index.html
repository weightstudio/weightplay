REAL-TIME TERRITORY ARCADE

Animal Sanctuary Loop

Leave safe land, draw a glowing trail, and reconnect before a shadow cuts it. Restore each habitat to finish the mission.

AN ORIGINAL WEIGHTPLAY ARCADE ADVENTURE

Risk a longer trail. Restore a larger sanctuary.

Fia is safe on restored land. Outside it, her lightning trail is vulnerable. Reconnect before a hunter reaches the open line.

How to play

  1. Swipe, drag, or use arrows to steer Fia.
  2. Leave restored land and form a loop through corruption.
  3. Reconnect before a hunter touches the open trail.

Thirty authored missions

Six chapters add rescue beacons, storm lanes, ordered seals, shrinking borders, rival claims, and Guardian hunts.

Local save

Unlocked missions and best stars stay in this browser. No account is required.
