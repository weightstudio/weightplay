Element Paths

Freeze pools, bend light and power wet circuits to recover the wellstones.

Game guide

Elemental puzzleRune routing and wet circuits

Goal

Collect every wellstone and reach the lit gate.

How to play

Pair a rune with a lens. Freeze water, clear seals or move mist to find a way through. In the Copper Depths, carry current across wet channels before freezing them.

Controls and rules

Tap a glowing adjacent tile to walk. Select a rune and tap a dashed tile to cast. Each action advances one beat. Arrow keys walk. Wait, or undo the previous action to recover.

Finishing and feedback

A clear opens the next cave. Save one charge and either take the amber shard or meet the beat target for three stars. Replay to improve your previous result.

Why does the current stop?

Ice breaks a wet connection. Send Spark through the wet pool or a copper node before freezing it.

Do I need diamonds to clear a cave?

No. Fork, Anchor and Relay unlock after caves 5, 15 and 30. Moon-brass trim is a permanent cosmetic.

How do I choose a rune and lens?

Use Rime for water, Flare for seals and Spark on wet or copper tiles. Fork reaches adjacent targets, Anchor extends ice, and Relay reaches farther.

How long does ice last and how do I recover?

Rime holds for four beats, or seven with Anchor. Cross before it thaws. Undo a wasted action and finish with at least one charge in reserve to earn its star.
