FAQ

Is progress saved?
Best results and preferences stay in this browser.
What is the objective?
Reveal safe cells, mark mines, and clear the field without triggering a mine.
How do hints work?
Hints reveal a safe next step and keep the board readable while you learn the pattern.
Can I undo a move?
Yes. Undo reverses the latest reveal or mark so you can recover from a mistaken tap.
