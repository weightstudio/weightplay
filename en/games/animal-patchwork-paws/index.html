How to play

Join matching trails. Keep blank hems on the outside. Tap or press Enter on a tile to turn it clockwise. Arrow keys move keyboard focus. Hover or focus previews every linked tile. Undo reverses the whole last move; Reset restores the starting puzzle. A free hint highlights one useful move. Check marks mismatched seams and outer hems without a penalty. After success, choose Stages, Next or Replay.

Meadow Beginnings

Each tap turns one tile clockwise. Count the little stitches to match trails.

Bramble Winds

The two A tiles turn together clockwise. Other tiles turn alone.

Echoing Creeks

Opposite tiles mirror each other: one clockwise, the other counterclockwise.

Compass Canopy

The compass tile is fixed. Build your weave around it.

Braided Moonrise

A tile also turns the next tile in its row. The last links back to the first.

Guardian Finale

A tile turns itself and every touching neighbour. Preview the cross before turning.
