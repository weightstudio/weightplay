Signal Veil Field Guide

A complete guide to Fia's 57-mission investigation, real-time combat, Neural Vision, equipment, and local progress.

Overview and mission

In 2042, Spark Paw Fia follows a dismissed Lizard Person recording to Signal Town. Ten witnesses, a hidden laboratory, and the stolen Neural Vision project point toward a larger cover-up. The campaign contains 57 authored missions across Signal Town, Veilwood Forest, the neural laboratory, Moonfall Relay, Ashfall Observatory, and the Hollow Moon Archive.

The investigation is one connected action-RPG journey rather than a stage list. Talk to witnesses, follow visible and hidden trails, defeat patrols, recover equipment, decode records, and carry earlier decisions into later chapters. Ashfall's evidence opens a fourth chapter at a lunar refugee archive, where Fia must decide whether to answer its fleet or shield its route.

Chapter One: Break the Veil

Missions 1–23

Begin by speaking with Orla, then interview all ten witnesses in Signal Town; the forest gate stays locked until the complete report is returned. In Veilwood, defeat the first eight patrols and use True Vision around cyan traces to expose cloaked targets and the laboratory entrance. Search the side paths for the Pulse Blade and Signal Charm before entering the lab. Clear all fifteen field enemies, recover Ranger Armor, and approach the Commander. During the Boss fight, circle the volleys, leave its charge line, and punish the brief stun. Recovering the stable device completes mission 23 but does not end the campaign.

Chapter Two: Moonfall Relay

Missions 24–33

After the Commander Result, choose Continue Exploring and speak to Orla again. Her briefing opens the eastern gate to Moonfall Relay. Defeat all eight corrupted sentries before operating the archive: partial kill milestones advance the story, but the displayed objective correctly remains out of eight. Activate Origin, Memory, and Warning in that order. The records reveal that the supposed invasion fleet carried refugees and that Helix weaponized their signal. Return through the western gate and report the complete archive to Orla to finish mission 33.

Chapter Three: Ashfall Witness

Missions 34–45

Speak to Orla after the Moonfall debrief, cross Moonfall again, and take its eastern gate to Ashfall Observatory. Meet Aster Vale, then alternate investigation and combat: defeat two agents, read the evacuation manifest, reach four defeated agents, disable the perception jammer, and clear all six before opening the black box. Broadcasting the evidence grants +2 Attack but exposes the source; protecting Aster grants +8 maximum health and +2 Defense. Carry the chosen outcome back through Moonfall to Orla. Either branch unlocks Chapter Four, and the next briefing remembers what you chose.

Chapter Four: Hollow Moon Archive

Missions 46–57

After completing mission 45, speak to Orla once more. Travel Signal Town → Moonfall → Ashfall and use Ashfall's new eastern gate. At the Hollow Moon Archive, read the Arrival Beacon, defeat two pursuers, decode the Cipher Well, defeat four, open the Lunar Nursery Record, defeat six, and inspect the Hollow Core. Eliminate all eight pursuers before returning to the core for the final route decision. Answering the sanctuary grants +1 Attack and +1 Defense; shielding its coordinates grants +12 maximum health. Return through all three western gates and report to Orla to reach 57/57.

How to play and complete the loop

  1. Move through the current region and read the objective shown above the world.
  2. Tap the nearby interaction prompt, or press E, to talk, open chests, activate records, and use gates.
  3. Face a threat, then use Attack for a fast sword wave or Skill for a ranged signal bolt.
  4. Switch to True Vision when cyan traces suggest a disguised person, cloaked enemy, treasure, or hidden route.
  5. Finish the objective, follow the next mission, and return evidence to Orla when the story asks.

Desktop players move with WASD or the arrow keys, attack with Space or J, fire Skill with K, switch Vision with V, interact with E, and open the menu with Escape. On phones, use the joystick and the three action buttons; every visible world-interaction prompt is also a touch target. Enemies patrol and chase, while the Commander adds volleys, a line charge, and a punishable stun window. Losing all health returns Fia to a safe checkpoint instead of deleting permanent progress.

Progression and difficulty

The opening investigation teaches conversation, melee spacing, and hidden trails before Veilwood and the laboratory combine groups of patrols with vision-dependent targets. Defeating enemies grants EXP; each level automatically improves maximum health, attack, and defense. Three chests unlock the Pulse Blade, Ranger Armor, and Signal Charm, whose abilities can be inspected, equipped, or removed in the Equipment menu. Moonfall asks you to clear eight sentries and decode three records. Ashfall adds six defenders and a witness choice. Chapter Four then opens a sixth playable region with eight stronger pursuers, four new archive records, and a second lasting choice: answer the sanctuary for attack and defense, or shield its route for maximum health.

Practical field tips

  • Watch the objective and Quest FloatBar; mission counters show the complete encounter total even when the story advances through smaller milestones.
  • Pull one patrol away from a group. The melee wave is faster, but the ranged Skill is safer against brutes and neural casters.
  • True Vision costs no energy. Toggle it around cyan sparks, suspicious walls, changed NPC dialogue, and apparently empty paths.
  • Inspect every recovered item. A defensive loadout helps while learning a Boss pattern, and equipment can be removed without losing ownership.
  • During the Commander fight, keep moving through volleys, step away from the charge line, then attack during the short stun.

Developer design note

Signal Veil uses one continuous world because investigation clues are easier to remember when places remain connected. Neural Vision changes what can be seen and targeted without adding an energy meter, so the decision is about observation rather than conserving a consumable. Keyboard shortcuts support quick action on desktop, while the same combat and interaction transactions have visible touch controls on phones. The 57-mission catalog records meaningful discoveries, combat thresholds, equipment, decoded evidence, and returns to Orla; it is not a set of repeated levels with larger numbers.

Player, device, and save information

Signal Veil is free to start and requires no account. Important changes—quests, EXP, levels, health, equipment, opened chests, defeated targets, map checkpoints, and story choices—save automatically in this browser; ordinary movement does not write every frame. The optional five-Diamond Signal Anchor permanently adds maximum health but is never required. Clearing site data or changing browser or device can remove or separate local progress.

Frequently asked questions

How many missions and regions are playable?
The current story has 57 missions across six connected regions, ending in the Hollow Moon Archive. Gates open as the investigation advances.
Does True Vision consume energy?
No. It can be switched freely after the prototype lens is unlocked. Use it to reveal cloaked threats, disguised characters, hidden routes, secret doors, and treasure.
Can equipment be removed or lost?
Owned equipment can be equipped or removed from the menu, and its exact stat effect is shown there. Removing an item does not delete it from the save.
What happens when Fia is defeated?
Fia returns to the latest safe checkpoint with permanent progression intact. Partial combat health and meaningful state changes are also saved automatically.
How long does the investigation take?
Play time depends on exploration and combat, but the four-chapter, 57-mission route is designed for a longer RPG session. You can leave and continue from the locally saved checkpoint.
