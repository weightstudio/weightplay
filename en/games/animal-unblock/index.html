How to play

Unblock Trail

Slide the wooden blocks to clear a path for the green bamboo block to the right-hand gate.

How to play

Choose an unlocked trail. Drag horizontal blocks left or right and vertical blocks up or down. On a keyboard, focus a block and use its arrow keys. Slide the bamboo block marked with a gold arrow to the right exit.

Rules

Blocks cannot rotate, overlap, jump over another block or leave the board. Each accepted slide counts as a move. There is no timer or move-limit defeat. Undo reverses your last move; Restart restores the starting layout.

Trail progression

Clearing a trail unlocks the next. Later layouts combine longer blocks, narrow gaps and several interdependent moves. Replay an earlier trail to match or improve its local best or target move count.

Planning tips

Plan backward from the exit. Sometimes a block must move away from the exit to create room. Hint identifies a useful block and direction; it does not move it for you.

Save and return

Unlocked trails and best move counts are stored in this browser. No account is needed. Leaving an unfinished trail discards its current arrangement, not completed trails. Clearing browser data removes local progress.

Questions

Why does a block stop moving?
Its next cell is occupied or outside the board. Move the blocking piece first. The gold arrow identifies the bamboo block that must reach the gate.
