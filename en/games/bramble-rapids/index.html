Goal

Guide Nori through the required buoys and finish with the goal shown on the stage card. Some courses ask for a place, a landing, an ordered route or remaining stability.

How to play

Steer through branching currents, time ramp jumps and read tide gates across sixty river challenges.

Controls and rules

← → / A D: steer · Space: jump · Hold X: moor with the anchor Drag sideways to steer · Tap Jump · Hold Anchor to moor Jump only at marked ramps. Steering is weaker in the air. At a mooring, hold Anchor for up to two seconds. The tide and rivals keep moving. You start with three stability. Hazard hits or missed landings can cost stability. A missed landing returns you to a validated buoy and adds three seconds to your time. Required buoys must be passed in order; jumping cannot skip them. Closed mandatory gates have a safe waiting point.

Finishing and results

A clear unlocks the next stage. Replay improves stars and best time. Results keep Stages, Next Stage and Replay in fixed places; Next Stage stays disabled after a failure or the last course. A computer rival is local AI, not another online player.

Route strategy

Read the next landing before leaving the water. A long safe line may beat a narrow shortcut after a fall. At a low bridge, staying in the water can be better than jumping. In tidal courses, compare a safe bypass with waiting for the gate; save the anchor for the window where stopping helps.

Free unlocks and appearances

Brook is available from the start. Clear Stage 5 for Driftfin and Stage 15 for Reedguard; clear Stage 30 for the Tide Anchor. These are permanent free unlocks. Every stage is designed for free boards; the diamond appearance changes no ability.

Questions and answers

Why does Jump sometimes do nothing?

At a marked ramp, press Jump and steer toward the landing.

What happens after a missed landing?

You start with three stability. Hazard hits or missed landings can cost stability. A missed landing returns you to a validated buoy and adds three seconds to your time. Required buoys must be passed in order; jumping cannot skip them. Closed mandatory gates have a safe waiting point.

How do I unlock more boards?
