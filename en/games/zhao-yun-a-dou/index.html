How to Play

  1. Tap a troop card to deploy. Soldiers advance and fight automatically.
  2. Protect archers with guards; save the charge for danger.
  3. Supplies recover automatically. Cards show cost and cooldown. Zhao Yun damages and pushes back all enemies.
  4. Protect A Dou and destroy the enemy fortress.

Strategy Tips

  • Protect archers with guards; save the charge for danger.
  • Bombers light a long fuse and hit a group. Interrupt with Zhao Yun’s charge.
  • Drummers speed nearby attacks. Archers prioritize support troops.
  • Heavy crossbows pierce two troops. Use cavalry to close the distance.
