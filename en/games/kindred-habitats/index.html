How to play

Bring food, greet an animal gently, then care for your new friend.

Controls and rules

The next action is shown for you. Place and time are prepared automatically. Care three times to help a friend grow; take a walk between care visits.

Progress and feedback

Your friends and upgrades are saved on this device. The album and optional purchases appear after your first friend grows up.

FAQ

What will I do in this game?
Befriend 36 different animal companions across six habitats.

From first meeting to a growing collection

Read the current animal’s request and use the main action. On a first meeting, bring the pictured food and follow the approach prompt to earn trust. Once a companion joins, the same action guides you through its current need, such as feeding, grooming or playing.

Take the suggested walk between care visits. Three care actions help the companion grow into an adult. The album and optional upgrades appear after the first companion grows up. You can keep discovering friends as habitats open and return to your collection in the same browser.
