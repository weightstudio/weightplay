Dusk Signal

Cross-check echoes, record wildlife and power your next survey.

Game guide

Survey strategy · DeductionStrategy Game

Your goal

Find each sound source and finish the station’s verification, recharge and relay targets before your battery runs out.

In the field

Scan a row and a column. Select the one site that matches both clues, then record. Later, a reference scan can replace a blocked column.

Back at the station

Verify for a seal, recharge for four battery, or relay a clear recording for one seal, two battery and a clue for your next recording.

Progress and results

Complete all recordings and meet the contract. Save power and avoid uncertain recordings for extra marks. Clear milestones to unlock equipment.

Can I play without sound?

Yes. Labels, lit sites and the station counters show every signal and outcome.

Do I need diamonds to finish?

No. Equipment is earned through surveys and diamonds only buy permanent lens appearances.
