Mirror Meadow

Tap mirrors to bend the moonbeam. Light every lantern to restore the meadow.

How to play

  1. Tap a numbered mirror to switch / and \. The beam bends immediately; there is no hidden password.
  2. Light every numbered lantern at once. Light continues through a lit lantern. Walls stop it.
  3. Purple splitters send light straight AND around the corner. Matching ring gates move the beam to their partner without changing direction.
  4. Hints highlight one mirror; they do not play for you. Undo and Replay are free. Clear a stage to save and unlock the next.
