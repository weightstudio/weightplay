Your goal

Place a different tent beside every tree. A tent must touch its own tree by an edge, while tents cannot touch one another, even at a corner. The numbers above and beside the board give the required tents in each column and row. There is no timer, life limit or paid solution.

Pitch a quiet camp

Tap an open plot to pitch or remove a tent. Select Mark to note a plot you have ruled out. Undo and hints are free. Start with a zero line or a tree with very few neighbours. Two trees can share a candidate, but a single tent cannot count for both: leave a separate shelter for each tree.

Rules and controls

Arrow keys move focus; Enter or Space acts, X marks and Z undoes. Direction flags restrict which side of a tree may hold its tent. Ponds are unavailable. Lantern clearings have a local tent quota. In chapter two, linked clearings must contain equal numbers of tents. Trail Lens shows possible tree links. Survey Pin saves one hypothesis for Recall; neither tool reveals a solution.

Finish and return

Check explains a mismatch and leaves your work reversible. A valid camp settles before Result and unlocks the next stage. Your unfinished board, clears and best edit count stay in this browser; clearing site data can remove them. Fern is free, Twilight is earned at stage 5, and the optional Aurora kit costs 12 shared Diamonds permanently. Looks never change the puzzle.

Questions

Can a tent touch more than one tree?
Yes. Every tree still needs a different tent. A complete one-to-one pairing must be possible.
Do camp looks or hints change progress?
Looks are cosmetic and hints are free. Any valid clear unlocks the next stage. Replay for a cleaner solution.
