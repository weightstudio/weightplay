Word Trails

Follow animal clues across ten trails. Find four target words on each board by connecting neighboring letters; diagonals are allowed, but a tile cannot be reused in one word.

World and Mission

Leo’s field album is missing animal and habitat entries. Find each word to return a clue card; finding all four completes the trail and opens the next available one.

How the Systems Work

Tap tiles in order or drag a continuous path, then choose Check Word. Hint reveals the first letter of the next unfound word; Undo removes the last tile, and Clear removes the current path.

The ten source boards are 4×4 and each contains four target words. Later trails add blocked tiles or a one-way leaf entry. There is no timer; clears, stars, and clue cards save in this browser.

How to Play

  1. Choose an unlocked trail.
  2. Read its animal or habitat clue and target words.
  3. Trace adjacent tiles; diagonals are allowed, but do not reuse a tile.
  4. Submit each word and find all four to clear the trail.

Strategy Tips

  • Scan the full target list before choosing a start.
  • Use a diagonal only when the next target letter is adjacent.
  • Enter a one-way leaf tile from its marked direction.

Campaign and Difficulty Growth

All ten source trails have four words. Later boards add blocked tiles or a one-way leaf entry. There is no timer; clears, stars, and clue cards save in this browser.

Developer Design Note

The original word packs are English and Traditional Chinese. The game can generate localized boards from the English pack for supported game languages; that is generated localization, not a separately authored pack for every public route.

Parent Note

This Kids reading puzzle is ad-free and needs no account. Progress and records stay in this browser and may be lost if site data is cleared. It is for practice and entertainment, not a formal reading assessment.

FAQ

How many trails are there?
Ten trails, with four target words on each.
Can I move diagonally?
Yes. Diagonal neighbors count as adjacent; a tile cannot be reused in one word.
What do Hint, Undo, and Clear do?
Hint reveals the first letter of the next unfound word; Undo removes the last tile, and Clear removes the current path.
Does the game use a timer?
No. Progress and clue-card records stay in this browser.
