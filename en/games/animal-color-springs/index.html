Similar gameplay reference

IEC Global’s Ball Sort Color Water Puzzle and Color Springs both ask you to sort colors by moving the top piece into an empty container or onto the same top color until each non-empty container holds one color. Ball Sort Color Water Puzzle presents the familiar mobile tube-sorting format and moves a top ball at a time, with no time limit and an optional extra tube when stuck. Color Springs uses that planning idea in 30 authored forest stages: matching top runs can move together when space allows, later chapters change vessel capacities and add sealed vessels, and each stage tracks a move goal with Hint, Undo and Restart. Choose Color Springs for a fixed 30-stage campaign with chapter-specific constraints and browser-local stars/best-move progress, not as a reproduction of Ball Sort Color Water Puzzle’s mobile progression or helper systems.

These are separate works. This comparison does not indicate an official edition, endorsement or partnership.

World and Mission

Forest fountains have lost their color, and Moon Cap Orla asks the player to restore each spring by sorting its glowing orbs.

How the Systems Work

Select a vessel with a movable top orb, then select a compatible destination with space. The destination must be empty or show the same top color.

Undo restores one exact transfer, Restart restores the authored opening, and Hint identifies a useful legal move. Clears, stars, unlocks, and best move counts are stored locally.

How to Play

  1. Choose an unlocked spring from the horizontal Stage rail.
  2. Select the vessel whose top orb you want to move.
  3. Select an empty vessel or one whose top orb matches.
  4. Finish every color group within the target moves.

Strategy Tips

  • Keep one flexible empty vessel until the middle of the solution.
  • Complete a color only when it will not trap another color below it.
  • Read vessel capacity before moving a long stack.

Campaign and Difficulty Growth

Stages 1-5 teach legal transfers and empty-space planning. Later chapters add more colors, uneven stacks, changing capacities, seals, and tighter routes across all 30 stages.

Developer Design Note

Authored openings make difficulty come from route planning rather than random deals. Touch, mouse, and keyboard share one transfer state, and Stage and Battle follow the shared 920px maximum.

Parent Note

No account is required. Progress stays in this browser and may reset if site storage is cleared. Stars and move summaries are game feedback, not a formal assessment.
