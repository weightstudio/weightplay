GAME GUIDE

Hear the pattern, read the gate

Learn the complete read-and-pulse loop, then restore all thirty handcrafted aurora towers.

The silent aurora towers

Panko's spectrum drums have gone quiet. Each rescued spirit restores one patterned tower and carries its pulse to the crown.

One press, one pulse

Tap, click, Space, Enter, or Arrow Up once for one identical lift. Release before pulsing again.

Colour is never the only clue

Cyan wave, gold sun, violet diamond, and coral paw always keep distinct emblems and textures.

Wait on safe checkpoints

A cleared gate becomes a safe resting beat. Watch the scan marker, then commit when your emblem arrives.

Six chapters of rising pressure

Towers 1–5 use one rotating ring; 6–10 add counter-rotating pairs; 11–15 add oscillating bars and crosses; 16–20 add petal spirals; 21–25 change the tempo; 26–30 combine crowns, petals, and reversals. Each cleared gate becomes a checkpoint.

Designed around one readable beat

The spirit always rises by the same pulse while each gate moves independently. Read the bottom marker, match the current colour and emblem, then commit. Pulse Control, Foresight, and Echo Guard ease recovery without changing the timing rule.

Player and save information

Tap, click, Space, Enter, or Arrow Up use the same pulse action. Tower unlocks, medals, best times, resonance upgrades, language, and the cosmetic trail stay in this browser. No account or purchase is required; the trail is cosmetic only.

FAQ

What is the safest way to pulse?

Use one short press, release fully, and wait for the bottom scan marker to meet your colour and emblem before pulsing again.

Do upgrades change the timing rules?

No. Pulse Control, Foresight, and Echo Guard make recovery easier, but every gate still uses the same colour-and-emblem timing rule.

What happens after a wrong gate?

The run ends unless Echo Guard or the practice beat returns you to a safe checkpoint; Result explains the next retry.

Is progress saved?

Tower unlocks, medals, best times, upgrades, and preferences are stored locally in this browser.

How do the six chapters change the gates?

Towers 1–5 use a rotating ring; later chapters add counter-rotating pairs, bars and crosses, petal spirals, tempo changes, and combined crown patterns.

Can I identify a gate without relying on colour?

Yes. Wave, sun, diamond, and paw each have a distinct emblem and texture as well as a colour.

What happens if I leave during a tower?

Continue resumes the same run, including its gate angles, timer, and Resonance. Returning to Towers ends that run.
