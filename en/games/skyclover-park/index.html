Skyclover Park

Lay a roller coaster loop, load waiting guests and reinvest the fares.

Game guide

Roller coaster managementTrack building and queues

Goal

Serve the required number of guests before the park closes.

How to play

Join both halves of the coaster. Read the queue, choose a speed and dispatch. Spend the fares on new rails or maintenance.

Controls and rules

A thrill rating of 2 suits both groups. Longer tracks and worn rails reduce seats. Wind blocks fast trains.

Finishing and feedback

Meet the stage targets by the last run. Replay to serve more guests or close with a higher balance.

How can both groups ride?

Set the track and speed to a thrill rating of 2.

When should I service the track?

Service before wear reaches 6 if the next run needs every seat.

Do I need diamonds?

All stages are playable for free. Diamonds buy a permanent train finish.
