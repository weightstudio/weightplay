How the Systems Work

From court 7, choose Direct or Bank before holding Shoot. A rim shutter blocks Direct; a board shutter blocks Bank. The green zone and dotted flight guide change with your route.

Focus slows one released shot to 62% speed. Tokens are limited per match. Cancelling a charge spends no token.

From 31, Pass spends one attempt and gives the rival its scheduled points, then stores two relay charges (capacity two). Assist spends one charge even on a miss, crosses every shutter and scores 2, or 3 for perfect. Pass breaks your scoring chain.

How to Play

  1. Hold. Find the green zone. Release.
  2. Keep the marker inside the green zone. The thin gold line is perfect.
  3. Space / Enter also work. Leaving the tab cancels your charge.
