Make a working production line

Each dock asks for a particular material. Place and rotate conveyors, then add the machine that transforms the supply into the requested cargo. Press Run to watch materials move one cell at a time.

Build, test, revise

Choose a tool, then an empty cell. Tap a placed piece to select it; Rotate changes its outlet and Remove returns its permit. Run begins a fresh shift. Pause freezes cargo; Edit keeps the layout and restarts the test. On a focused board, arrows move selection, Enter places, R rotates, Delete removes and Space runs or pauses. Speed switches between normal and double pace.

Read the bottleneck

A smelter turns ore into plates. Gear presses use two plates for one gear; assemblers combine a gear with a plank. A machine with one missing ingredient cannot work faster by receiving more of the other. Check the output space before adding another machine. From stage 36, docks accept cargo only at high tide (power 5 or higher). A raised brass barrier marks a closed dock. Store cargo before the next opening; buffers can feed a dock on consecutive beats.

Finishing and feedback

Complete every displayed dock order before the shift limit. You earn one mark for delivery, another for the time target and another for the piece budget shown in the stage details. All three are optional beyond completing delivery. A failed shift keeps your layout for another attempt. Next continues the campaign; Replay lets you improve an earlier record.

Two chapters, linked systems

The 60 stages form two 30-stage chapters. Early orders introduce ore smelting, timber sawing and the two-plate gear recipe. Later lines combine gears and planks, divide output between docks, sort mixed supplies and cross independent streams. Tidal power varies by beat: high-priority machines receive energy first. Research unlocked at stages 10 and 30 offers slower low-power processing or larger input storage with one fewer machine permit. Clear 5, 15 and 30 distinct stages for workshop plaques. The optional eight-diamond colour set is permanent and does not change production rules.

Why the workshop works this way

We designed the dock as a small space with interacting constraints. The useful question is whether a line has the right ingredients, a free outlet and enough energy at the right moment. Materials stay on the board when blocked, so you can inspect the cause instead of losing cargo without explanation. Reefs and fixed stations change routing while the later orders reuse familiar recipes in combined networks. Portrait and landscape show the same logical cells in different orientations to keep touch targets usable. Local records reward a shorter, simpler line without requiring a perfect mark to continue.

Progress and controls

Clears, best records, layouts and workshop choices stay in this browser. Clearing a stage unlocks the next; Replay keeps earned progress. Touch and keyboard use the same rules. Local storage may be unavailable in private browsing. There is no cloud save.

Workshop questions

Why does a machine stop even with cargo nearby?
Only its recipe inputs count. Check the selected machine with Inspect. A full outlet or insufficient tidal power also stops processing. Give priority to a critical machine or edit the route and test again.
How do the two branching tools differ?
The alternator sends successive packets to its two outlets in strict alternation; a blocked chosen outlet waits. The sorter sends timber and planks along its branch, while other materials continue through the main outlet.
What passes through a crossing bridge?
The two axes carry separate packets. Traffic continues along its incoming direction, with one packet per axis. Turning an ordinary belt does not create an independent second lane.
What is saved and what can be bought?
Clears, best records, layouts, research and appearance choices are saved locally. No account is required and there is no cloud save. Clearing browser data can remove them. Diamonds can buy the permanent colour set; all stage orders can be completed with the standard appearance.
