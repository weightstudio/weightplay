Game guide

ActionGuard

Goal

Face clay sentinels in a 3D kiln. Step out of marked lanes, guard to gather heat and land a charged blow.

How to play

Strike the sentinel in your lane. Orange chevrons show where attacks will land. Step away or guard just before impact. Fill all three Heat segments to shatter armor with Burst.

Controls

Tap a floor lane or use ← and →. Tap the action buttons or press J to strike, K to guard and L for Burst.

Victory and rewards

Defeat all sentinels. Keep your Health high for more stars. Clear stages 5, 15 and 30 for permanent gear. Diamonds buy optional cosmetic glazes.
