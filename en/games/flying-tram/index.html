How to play

  1. Hold Boost to lift off and add a little height during the flight.
  2. Hold left or right to tilt. A full flip scores points, but strong spinning makes the tea slosh.
  3. Counter-steer to level the tram. In the landing window, release BOOST to descend; hold it to slow a fast drop, then release to settle onto the pad.

Flight and landing

Use BOOST to gain height and left or right to rotate. Wind and thermals affect the flight; change altitude to avoid marked obstacles. Bring the tram onto the landing pad while keeping the tea in its cup. The result shows the landing quality or the reason to retry.

The tram can fly; the bubble tea still needs a steady hand.

Routes and records

Completing a route opens the next flight. Unlocked routes remain available for another attempt at your best score.

Steering choices

More flips can earn points, but leave time to counter-steer and settle the tea before landing.

Frequently asked questions

When should I land?
Watch the route timer and landing cue. Descend toward the pad during the landing window.
How do I flip?
Hold left or right to rotate the tram through a full turn.
Why is the cup shaking?
Sharp tilts and fast spins increase the spill meter. Ease off and steady the cabin.
How do I stop spinning?
Hold the opposite direction to counter-steer, then release when the tram settles.
How do I land gently?
Line up with the pad and level the tram. Release BOOST to descend; use short boosts to slow a fast descent.
How do I unlock another route?
Complete the current flight. The result shows whether the route is cleared and offers the next flight when unlocked.
Is progress saved online?
No. Best scores and unlocked routes are stored in this browser. Clearing site data removes that save.
What happens if I fail?
Retry the current route. A failed flight does not remove your saved scores or unlocked routes.
