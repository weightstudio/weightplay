Rally Darts

Aim at the ring, set up a double finish, and beat your rival to zero.

Game guide

DartsDouble finish

Goal

Reach zero before the rival finishes.

How to play

Pick a sector and ring, then throw. Leave a score you can finish with one double.

Controls and rules

Tap the board or use the number keys to aim. R switches rings and Enter throws. From Match 16, use the green timing band for doubles.

Finish and rewards

Hit the required stamps, then finish on zero. Fewer darts and misses earn more stars.

The story behind the game

On 18 November 2026, Jermaine Wattimena beat defending champion Luke Littler 10–8 in the Grand Slam of Darts last 16. That close match inspired the race to leave yourself one good finishing dart.

How do I double my score?

Aim at the outer ring and throw in the green band on timed stages.

Why did the score stay the same?

A bust leaves the score unchanged. Avoid overshooting, leaving one point, or reaching zero with the wrong ring.
