SportsTiming

How to play

Glide from the starting gate and press Jump in the green band. From run 6, level your skis when the landing cue lights up.

Spread the skis for distance. Tuck when the windsock turns sideways. In the Gorge Cup, land on the near or far marked strip.

Finish runs 10 and 20 to unlock landing boots and wind skis. Choose equipment between runs. The wind brake works once each Gorge jump.

FAQ

Why was a jump invalid?

A takeoff outside the green band, no landing setup, poor balance or missing both marked strips can void a jump.

Which starting gate should I choose?

After run 20, the high gate adds speed but punishes an open stance in a crosswind. The low gate is easier for near landings.
