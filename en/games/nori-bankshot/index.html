Goal

Help Nori clear each harbor table. Choose a pocket, transfer the white ball’s motion into a target and plan the position left for your next shot.

How to play

Aim on the table, set power and shoot. White shows the first contact; gold shows the target direction. After the balls stop, choose your next line. Later tables add number order, rail contact, locked pockets and relay groups.

Controls and rules

Tap or drag to aim; use the Power slider and Shoot. Arrow keys adjust aim and power, and Space shoots. Pocketing the white ball returns it safely. A numbered ball played too early or a ringed ball pocketed before touching a cushion also returns. A lantern ball finishes its group after the other targets.

Finishing and feedback

Clear within the shot allowance to unlock the next table. Replay to improve your stars and best shot count. First clears earn stamps for permanent styles. The optional diamond style uses the shared wallet and changes appearance only. Progress stays in this browser; there is no account or cloud save.

Questions

Why did a pocketed ball return?

The white cue ball is a scratch. A target returns if its number was too early, its cushion requirement was missed or a lantern was played before the other balls. The status explains the actual rule.

Does a new cue make shots stronger?

No. Every collection style uses the same physics, pocket size and shot allowance. Earned stamps unlock the Coral Cue and Moonlit Cloth; diamonds can unlock the permanent Sea-glass Set.

How does the campaign save?

Cleared tables, best records, stamps and equipped styles save locally on this device. Clearing browser storage removes local progress. Wallet ownership uses its own permanent purchase receipt.
