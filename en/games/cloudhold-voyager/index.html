Your goal

Lead a three-person crew through three connected cloudship encounters. Bring the ship home with its crew safe. Later voyages add a rescue pod that must also survive. Hull, oxygen, rooms and materials carry between legs, so winning one fight can still leave a difficult next decision.

How to play

Select a crew portrait and then a room. Crew move one adjacent room per command; a distant order takes several commands. On arrival, they clear hazards or repair damage before operating a healthy room. Rux repairs two integrity points instead of one. Balance the four powered systems within the available capacity, choose an enemy system and advance. Nothing progresses while you plan.

Controls and rules

Use touch, a mouse, or Tab and Enter. A powered lens attacks, shields absorb ordinary hits, the oxygen garden replenishes air and the engine charges an escape. Towing consumes one extra oxygen per pulse. Targeting enemy systems can interrupt the next threat; their repairs are forecast. Vent an empty burning room for two oxygen. Hull repair needs a working staffed workshop and two materials. An ion lock expires; a bridge battery restores a disabled system for two pulses. Choose a tool first, then its target room. Selecting a crew portrait returns to crew orders.

Finishing and feedback

Clear the final encounter to save the voyage and unlock the next. Some rescue finales also need a charged engine and staffed helm. Running out of hull, crew or rescue-pod integrity ends the attempt. Read the final state, then use Voyages, Next voyage or Replay. Retries are free and permanent unlocks remain.

New responsibilities

The first chapter introduces fire, leaks, intruders and ion locks through distinct captains. The second adds towing and a limited bridge battery. Rescue and Swift hulls unlock through ordinary play and favor different power and repair plans. Optional diamond finishes permanently change the ship’s materials without adding combat strength.

Player and save information

Progress, current voyage, selected hull and owned finishes stay in this browser. A private window or cleared site storage may not retain them. No account, purchase, advertisement or real-time waiting is required. A saving warning means the current session is still available but persistence has not been confirmed.

Create a repair window

A damaged lens can be repaired quickly by Rux, but moving him away from the workshop temporarily removes hull repairs. Disabling the opposing weapon can create the safe pulse needed to recover. Before turning oxygen off for more attack power, inspect the air reserve and the next incoming leak. The command-by-command pace leaves time to read threats on a phone. Six rooms share three people, so sending help is also a decision about which station to leave. Power alone cannot put out a fire or remove an intruder. Captains change the priority: Bellshield Captain alternates protected systems, while Magnetring Captain reflects hits aimed at the shield room on the announced beat. The forecast, surviving supplies and two dock choices make the whole voyage matter, not just the last shot.

Questions aboard

Why is a powered room idle?

Fire, intruders, an ion lock or zero integrity can disable it. Clear the hazard, repair the room or use a bridge battery where applicable.

Why can I not vent a burning room?

Venting requires an empty room and at least two oxygen. Move crew out first, or let a crew member fight the fire on the next pulse.

Can I change ships?

Clear Voyage 3 for the Rescue hull and Voyage 8 for Swift. Choose freely in Crew & hulls before starting another voyage.

Do paid finishes make the ship stronger?

No. Porcelain and Sunrise finishes are permanent appearances only. All encounters are designed for free hulls.
