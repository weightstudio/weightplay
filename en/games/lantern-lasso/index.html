How to play

Drag across the board to aim, then release to cast. Collect lights and bounce off the edges. Pull numbered switches to open matching gates.

Some lights require an ordered catch or a set number of reflections. Later, hold the moving beam for two casts. Arrow keys aim; Space casts.
