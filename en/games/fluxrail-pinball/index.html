Pinball

Time your flippers, charge the center rail, and light the workshop before you run out of balls.

Game guide

Magnetic pinballTimed flippers

Goal

Light the marked rails in order before you run out of balls.

How to play

Press either flipper to serve. Strike as the ball approaches a flipper. The point of contact changes the shot angle.

Controls and rules

Use the two flipper buttons or A and D. On magnetic tables, the center rail charges the battery. Flip the gate polarity for one charge or arm a ball save for two.

Finishing and feedback

After the last target lights, the result shows your rails, remaining balls, saves and stars. Retry to try a different part or improve your timing.

How do I aim at the center?

Try to catch the approaching ball near the middle of a flipper. Adjust your timing as you watch where the ball rebounds.

What closes the side gates?

A left or right rail may need a matching pole and an open shutter. Take a center shot to refill charge and advance the shutter.

Can I recover a missed ball?

On magnetic tables, use charge to arm the drain guard before missing a shot. The next missed ball will be sent back into play.
