House the required species and deliver the listed dew, seeds and feathers to the windtree. Some stages also require a bridge, plate or anchor action. Deliver in batches: the produce bag holds 30. Falling spends one of three rescue beacons; the third fall ends the expedition. There is no countdown.

Help

WASD or left stick: move. Drag the scene or use arrow keys: look. E: interact. Q: tool. F: food. Space: jump. H: help. L: mouse lock.

Gather food, aim at an animal and feed its preferred kind. Lead it along a safe route; use Guide to wait or follow.

Habitats

Grass houses rabbits and turtles; flowers house birds, deer and foxes; water houses otters. Bring fed animals close, then interact. Use Guide on an empty habitat to change its recipe.

Tools

Use seeds to weave bridges. Chapter two adds wind anchors: spend feathers, aim at nearby ground and place up to two. Recall an anchor to move it; feathers are not refunded. Light paths guide twilight foxes.

Stages

The campaign has 60 stages in two chapters. Begin with a berry-fed rabbit; Stage 8 introduces turtle weight plates, Stage 18 water habitats and Stage 21 lantern-guided foxes. From Stage 31, feather-powered wind anchors add shelter and floating-bridge routing. Later missions combine these rules and larger deliveries.

Research

Every third feeding grants one extra product.

The second anchor placement is free once per expedition.

Saved on this device

No sign-in is required. Cleared stages, research, selected equipment and the current expedition are stored in this browser. Clearing site data removes this progress. First clears award research; replaying does not award it again. Diamond cosmetics are optional and never required to clear a stage.

Design note

We designed the wrist tool to keep animals in the landscape. You feed and lead them instead of carrying them in an inventory. A bird can cross a gap, a turtle needs a safe path, and a twilight fox follows light. Route planning connects exploration to the habitats at camp.
