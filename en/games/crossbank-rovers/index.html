Crossbank Rovers

Fit a rover, choose a trail and deliver supplies before charge or armor runs out.

Game guide

Supply delivery strategyFit wheels and tools for the terrainChoose lanes and manage battery charge

Goal

Bring the required supplies to the trail depot with armor intact.

How to play

Choose a lane and drive one leg at a time. Later routes add wheels, grip mode, a frame and a tool.

Controls and rules

Both lanes show how charge, armor and cargo will change. Switching lanes costs one charge. Grip mode uses one extra charge to reduce wear.

Finishing and feedback

Delivering all crates unlocks the next route. If you come up short, retry with another lane choice.

Do I need diamonds to finish the campaign?

No. Clearing routes earns all functional parts. Diamonds buy permanent paint only. Your progress is saved in this browser.
