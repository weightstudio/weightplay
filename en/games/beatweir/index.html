Beatweir

Drive a railcar to the beat, switch tracks for cargo, and keep enough charge for the next gate.

Game guide

Rhythm gameSwitch tracks

Goal

Drive a railcar to the beat, deliver the required cargo, and reach the far station.

How to play

Press Drive as a beat meets the marker to build charge. At a junction, spend a charge to reach the other track.

Controls and rules

Tap Drive or Switch, or press Space or Shift. Coast on a rest.

Finish and rewards

Reach the depot with enough deliveries. Clean beats and remaining stability earn more stars.

FAQ

When can I switch tracks?
Switch on the gold crossing when its beat arrives. Keep driving to charge up on any other beat.
