Controls

Hold a goal zone to build power, then release to shoot. While defending, choose the zone shown by the bright real cue.

Win the shootout

Score more goals after five turns each. A tie continues into sudden death.

Cup journey

Clear six chapters and 30 unique rivals. Each win unlocks the next match.

Fair-play tips

Vary your corners, release inside the gold power window, and ignore dim feints.
