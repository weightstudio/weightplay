Puppy Haven

Select a dog in the yard or the list. Feed it, pet it and clear droppings. Buy three grades of food in the pantry. Adopt male or female dogs of six breeds. Healthy unrelated adults can have puppies; pregnancy takes 45 seconds of active play. Feed and care for puppies to help them grow. Progress saves on this device. Nothing decays while you are away. When the yard is full, open Family or Adopt → Manage rooms. Dogs can rest indoors and return without losing their needs, growth or family.
