Mist Canopy

Forecast the weather, route power and turn barren terraces into self-sustaining habitats.

Mist Canopy

Goal

Root the required terraces before the shift limit. Marked terraces are required when the contract specifies particular plots.

How to play

Check the two forecasts. Choose equipment, tap a terrace, then run the shift. Undo removes a queued job for free.

Controls and rules

Weather acts first. Two stable shifts make a terrace rooted. Roots supply parts and power. Later, save power in the battery and link two neighbouring plots.

Finishing and feedback

Clear a contract to unlock the next. The journal tracks your progress. Finishes only change appearance.

Questions

Are diamonds required?
No. All equipment is free. Five shared diamonds unlock a permanent optional Aurora finish.
