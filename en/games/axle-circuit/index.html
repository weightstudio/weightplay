Axle Circuit

Drive into the arena, knock parts off rival cars and bring the scraps back to repair yours.

How to play

Goal

Stop every rival car and keep yours running.

How to play

Move into range, read the orange warnings, and pulse the exposed parts. Sweep around a rival to knock off its wheels.

Controls and rules

Tap empty ground to drive. Tap a car to target it. Use Pulse and Boost. On a keyboard, use arrows or WASD, Space and Shift.

Finishing and feedback

Finish with enough time and hull for three stars. Collect rivets and drive over the green repair pad to fix your car.

Questions

How do I slow rivals?
Aim at a rival from the side to detach a wheel. Remove a front probe to stop its orange beam attacks.
How do I repair my car?
Take two dropped rivets to the green repair pad. You restore a missing wheel or two hull points.
How do kits unlock?
Win Rounds 5, 20, and 40, then fit the newly unlocked kit in the Workshop.
