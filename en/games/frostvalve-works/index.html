Frostvalve Works

Read the next surge, switch pumps and valves, and keep the winter waterworks flowing.

Game guide

Waterworks simulationPumps & pressure

Goal

Deliver the requested water before the shift ends. Keep tanks below the overflow line.

How to play

Read the next surge and tap the tank to work on. Change mode when you need to recover charge or protect a full tank.

Controls and rules

Pump delivers water and spends charge. Recharge moves water to the next tank and restores charge. Isolate blocks one incoming surge. Thaw unfreezes a tank.

Finishing and feedback

Overflow costs a mark. Four overflows end the shift; you can replay for free. The first clear unlocks the next shift.

How do I get more charge?

Switch to Recharge and select a tank at least two units higher than its linked tank.

What can I do when a tank freezes?

Use Thaw or take water from another tank.

Do I need diamonds to finish the campaign?

No. Tools earned from shifts are free; diamonds only buy a permanent finish.
