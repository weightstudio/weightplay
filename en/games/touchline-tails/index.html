Football tactics

Win the fixture by finding space when you have the ball and protecting the goal when you don't.

Training

Before kick-off, choose an attacking, defensive or stamina drill. Select a lane, open the bench and assign a starter whose strengths suit the job.

Formation

A wide shape helps both wings. Short passes help the centre. Pressing can win a stronger counterattack, but it costs more stamina. A fresh substitute can turn the final play.

Full time

If your team concedes, read which lane they broke through. On a retry, change the defender there or save energy for the late play.

Do I need to buy anything?

No. The starting team can win all 60 fixtures. The clubhouse sells permanent looks; academy players come from match clears.

What changes in the Harbor League?

Wind changes the value of wide plays. Wet lanes use more stamina, the scout checks one false signal, and some windows use set plays.

Where is progress stored?

Clears and your best scores stay in this browser. Leaving a live fixture ends that attempt without changing completed fixtures.
