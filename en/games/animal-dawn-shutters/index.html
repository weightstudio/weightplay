How to play

  1. Compare the target light bars with the three shutters.
  2. Tap a shutter to cycle closed, slit, and open.
  3. A wrong try stays visible, so adjust calmly and try again.

Results and saves

Six five-room arcs make 30 rooms. Completing a room unlocks the next and keeps it replayable. Progress stays in this browser; completing Room 30 also saves its tap count as your best when storage is available.

Practical tips

Read the three bars from left to right. Each shutter cycles closed, slit, and open, and a wrong check never hides the attempted glow.

Room mission

Each room asks you to match three requested glow bars by tuning three independent shutters. The visible target is the rule, and there is no hidden countdown.

Three light states

Closed blocks the light, slit gives a narrow middle glow, and open gives the broadest glow. Compare the three positions from left to right; all values must match.

Campaign path

The 30 authored rooms are grouped into six five-room dawn arcs. Finishing a room unlocks exactly the next room, while completed rooms stay available on the Dawn map for replay.

Correction loop

A wrong check keeps all three settings visible and shows a general message; it does not identify a shutter to change. Compare each bar with its target, change one shutter, then check again. Reset returns this room to all three shutters closed; completed rooms stay saved.

Save boundary

Completed rooms are saved in this browser when local storage is available. Completing Room 30 also saves that room's tap count as the best result; neither value syncs to another device, and clearing site data may remove progress.

Room 1 example

Room 1 starts with all three shutters closed. Its target reads open, slit, closed from left to right: tap Shutter 1 twice, tap Shutter 2 once, and leave Shutter 3 closed. All three bars then match in three taps.

FAQ

How many rooms are included?
There are 30 authored rooms across six five-room dawn arcs.
What do the three light states mean?
Closed gives no light, slit gives a narrow middle value, and open gives the broadest glow.
What happens after a wrong check?
The message only says the room is not ready. It does not identify a shutter. Your settings stay visible, so compare the bars, adjust one shutter, and check again.
How does unlocking work?
A correct room unlocks exactly the next room, and completed rooms remain replayable from the Dawn map.
Where is progress saved?
Completed rooms are saved in this browser when local storage is available. Completing Room 30 also saves that room's tap count as your best; progress and best do not sync to other devices.
