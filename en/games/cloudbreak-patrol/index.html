Cloudbreak Patrol

Steer through cloud gaps, hit stormwings with light pulses, and escort the courier craft.

How to fly

Goal

Drive off the stormwings and escort the couriers through the cloud gap.

How to play

Slide across the flight field to line up a target. Fire a light pulse when its ring lights up. Leave the cloud to regain a clear line.

Controls and rules

Touch and drag to steer, or use WASD and the arrow keys. Space fires, Shift veers, E switches wing duty, and R activates a relay when equipped.

Finishing and feedback

Clear the stormwings, get every courier home, and keep your hull intact for more stars. Replay flights to improve your record.

Choosing your approach

Pursuit adds a shot near your glider. Escort can absorb a hit meant for a courier. Use the one that solves the next threat, then keep flying while the wingmate acts.

Clouds break both lines of sight. Cool down, then cross an edge for a clear shot.

Fly ahead of a courier to speed it up.

Questions

Why does my target ring disappear?
A cloud interrupts the line to the target. Fly out of cover or along its edge to find a clear shot.
How do I earn better wings?
Clear Flights 5, 20, and 40, then fit the unlocked wings in the Hangar.
