World and Mission

Wordle is a compact word route built from six authored puzzles. Each board gives you a five-letter target, a limited number of guesses, and enough feedback to turn one result into the next useful question.

The round is intentionally short. Finish the current puzzle, review the result, and use Play again to move through the deck instead of treating one solved word as the whole experience.

How the Systems Work

Correct place means the letter is at this position; Other place means a copy exists elsewhere. No extra copy means this guess has no remaining match for that letter after exact and misplaced copies are counted, so a repeated letter can receive a miss even when another copy is in the answer.

The six English puzzles can be played freely or with Clue lock. Choose before your first guess or hint; locked guesses must match every previous clue, including duplicate-letter counts and revealed positions.

Invalid or repeated guesses cost no attempt. Up to two hints each reveal one unresolved position without spending a guess.

Letter combinations are accepted without a full dictionary check.

How to Play

  1. Start the round and inspect the five empty positions.
  2. The six English puzzles can be played freely or with Clue lock.
  3. Enter a five-letter word using the on-screen keys or your keyboard.
  4. Correct place means the letter is at this position; Other place means a copy exists elsewhere. No extra copy means this guess has no remaining match for that letter after exact and misplaced copies are counted, so a repeated letter can receive a miss even when another copy is in the answer.
  5. Up to two hints each reveal one unresolved position without spending a guess.
  6. Solve within six guesses, review Result, then choose Play again for the next puzzle or return to Main.

Strategy Tips

  • Use an early guess to test several common letters, then let the feedback remove impossible patterns.
  • A near letter is still useful only after you move it; do not repeat the same placement without a reason.
  • When the pattern is narrow, prefer a word that confirms the uncertain letters instead of chasing a broad guess.
  • Save Hint for a genuine fork in the deduction, and read every color before typing again.

Campaign and Difficulty Growth

Earn three stars by solving within three guesses without hints, two within four guesses using at most one hint, otherwise one for a solve. Each puzzle stores its best stars separately for both modes: 36 stars in total, not repeat-play farming.

A solve scores 500 plus 100 per unused guess, minus 50 per hint, plus 100 with Clue lock; an unsolved puzzle scores zero. Records stay in this browser; blocked storage keeps them only for this visit.

Developer Design Note

Wordle makes deduction visible through a five-letter board, three kinds of feedback, a limited six-guess budget, and an optional Hint. The six-puzzle route gives a solved word a next step instead of ending the value at the first answer.

Player and Save Information
