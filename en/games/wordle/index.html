World and Mission

Wordle is a compact word route built from six authored puzzles. Each board gives you a five-letter target, a limited number of guesses, and enough feedback to turn one result into the next useful question.

The round is intentionally short. Finish the current puzzle, review the result, and use Play again to move through the deck instead of treating one solved word as the whole experience.

How the Systems Work

Enter a five-letter word and read the feedback: an exact letter is in the correct place, a near letter belongs in the answer but needs another place, and a miss is not used by that puzzle.

Hint is advisory rather than automatic. It can narrow the search, but you still choose the word and spend a guess only when the information is worth the trade.

You have up to six guesses per puzzle. Result records the outcome and lets you continue through the six-puzzle deck or return to Main. Best results and preferences stay in this browser.

How to Play

  1. Start the round and inspect the five empty positions.
  2. Enter a five-letter word using the on-screen keys or your keyboard.
  3. Read exact, near, and miss feedback before choosing the next guess.
  4. Use Hint when its information is more valuable than keeping the guess unused.
  5. Solve within six guesses, review Result, then choose Play again for the next puzzle or return to Main.

Strategy Tips

  • Use an early guess to test several common letters, then let the feedback remove impossible patterns.
  • A near letter is still useful only after you move it; do not repeat the same placement without a reason.
  • When the pattern is narrow, prefer a word that confirms the uncertain letters instead of chasing a broad guess.
  • Save Hint for a genuine fork in the deduction, and read every color before typing again.

Campaign and Difficulty Growth

The six-puzzle deck changes the target after each Result. There is no account skill tree or online ranking; progress comes from reading clues more efficiently and making cleaner guesses.

Play again keeps the loop moving through the authored set, while Main lets you leave the round without losing the local result state.

Developer Design Note

Wordle makes deduction visible through a five-letter board, three kinds of feedback, a limited six-guess budget, and an optional Hint. The six-puzzle route gives a solved word a next step instead of ending the value at the first answer.

Player and Save Information

Best results and preferences are saved only in this browser. No account, purchase, online profile, or cloud save is required; clearing site data can remove local results.
