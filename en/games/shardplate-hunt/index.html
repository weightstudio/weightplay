Shardplate Hunt

Circle mechanized beasts, break their armor and turn salvaged parts into new gear.

Game guide

ActionHeavy hit

Goal

Circle mechanized beasts, break their armor and turn salvaged parts into new gear.

How to play

Get off the marked pads before the attack lands. Move beside an intact module and strike it as the machine recovers. Try breaking the fan first when it covers both paths to the cannon.

Controls and rules

Tap a neighboring pad or press an arrow key to move. J strikes, K winds up a heavy hit, and Q grounds a relay in Chapter 2. A heavy hit needs time to land.

Victory and rewards

Destroy modules to uncover the core, then hit it to stop the machine. Each kind of module pays out once per hunt. Salvage buys permanent tools. Stars and equipment are saved on this browser.
