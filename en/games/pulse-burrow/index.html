Pulse Burrow

Move to the beat, read enemy warning tiles, and save your drum pulse for the next fight.

Game guide

Goal

Clear the spirits, then step on the lit exit.

How to play

Tap a nearby tile or press an arrow key. Step toward a spirit to strike it.

Timing and terrain

Queue one move per beat. Land it in the bright window to charge the drum. Hatched tiles show the next threat. A wall stops a pulse.

Clearing a room

Stay healthy and land clean beats to earn more stars. Craft drum upgrades between rooms.

Frequently asked questions

How do I charge the drum?
Make a legal move or land a hit in the bright section of the beat track.
Do I need diamonds?
All rooms and functional upgrades are earnable through play. Diamonds buy the optional permanent drum rim.
