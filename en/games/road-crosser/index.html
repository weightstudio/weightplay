How to play

Controls

  1. Use arrow keys or WASD to move one lane.
  2. Swipe or tap the direction pads on mobile.
  3. Watch car lanes and float on a moving log in the river.
  4. Lit outlines show occupied vehicle space. The next-step outline suggests a landing: solid means open, dashed means wait or move sideways.

Crossing rhythm

Stage 1 teaches the route. Stages 2 and 3 add denser traffic, faster water, rocks, and tighter safe windows.

  1. Moonlit Crossing
  2. Countercurrent Wharf
  3. Star Gate Express
