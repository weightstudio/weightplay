Bellshift Atelier

Plan two crews’ commands and ring the requested notes before the tower runs out of power.

Game guide

Goal

Play every requested note with two artisans and one power supply.

How to play

Pick a beat in either lane. Add a command, then rehearse the score. Follow the playhead to see how the two artisans cooperate.

Controls and rules

Move before ringing. Both lanes share power. Cranks charge before the beat spends power. Later, wind, locked bells and echoes change which notes can play.

Finishing and feedback

Match every beat to finish an order. If a note fails, check the highlighted beat and revise your score. All orders can be solved without diamonds or sound.

Do I need to hear the bells?

No. Shapes, lanes and the playhead show every requested and played note.
