The lunar salvage mission

Moon Cap Orla uses a controlled recovery ring to remove corrupted nonliving debris and uncover stranded rescue cores.

Read size before moving

Teal debris fits now, amber is close, and red is too large. Build mass through a safe route instead of colliding blindly.

Plan chains and objectives

Fast consecutive recoveries raise the Moon Chain. Later zones add currents, shields, rivals, rescue cores, and a closing storm.

Understand each salvage target

Recover the quota shown above the arena, then finish any rescue-core or rival objective. Prism shards open shielded wreckage, while the Observatory upgrades acceleration, size reach, and Stability.

Six chapters, thirty zones

The first chapter teaches safe size routes; later chapters add moving currents, shielded targets, rival rings, and the closing storm. Clearing a zone unlocks the next one and awards Star Gears for permanent tuning.

Designed for direct control

Touch, mouse, and keyboard use one simulation. Movement, collision, score, timer, rivals, and rewards never change with screen size.

Player and save information

Use the lower-left stick on touchscreens; release to stop. On desktop, use arrows/WASD or click a destination. Zone unlocks, best times, Star Gears, Observatory upgrades, language, sound, and the cosmetic Constellation Wake stay in this browser; no account or purchase is required.

FAQ

Why is some debris too large?

Recover smaller teal debris first so the ring grows. Amber is close to your current size; red still needs a safer route.

How do I clear a shielded wreck?

Collect three prism shards, then return to the marked wreckage. The shards open the shield; they do not replace the zone quota.

What ends a salvage run?

The timer can close the rift, Stability can reach zero, or an objective can remain incomplete. Result tells you whether to retry or continue to the next zone.

Is progress saved?

Yes. Unlocks, best times, Star Gears, Observatory upgrades, and preferences are stored locally in this browser.

How do Observatory upgrades help?

Spend Star Gears on Drive, Compression, and Stability upgrades. Each can reach level 3; they permanently improve acceleration, the size needed to collect debris, and maximum Stability.
