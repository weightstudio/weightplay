Hill Route Bus

Drive a hillside bus, brake smoothly and get passengers to their stops on time.

Game guide

Goal

Bring your passengers to their stops comfortably and on time.

How to play

Use Drive to build speed, Coast before bends and Brake in the yellow bay. Open the doors after the bus stops.

Controls and rules

Use W to drive, Space to coast, S to brake, A and D to change lanes, and Enter to open the doors. Around bends, slow down early.

Finishing and feedback

Deliver every required group. A smooth and punctual route earns more stars. Replay to improve your drive.
