GenreClassic · Arcade · Action

World and Mission

You control the mint paddle at the bottom. Press Serve.

How the Systems Work

Drag across the court, move your mouse, or hold ← / → (A / D). Return the moving ball; missing your end gives your opponent one point. First to 7 wins.

How to Play

  1. Drag across the court, move your mouse, or hold ← / → (A / D). Return the moving ball; missing your end gives your opponent one point. First to 7 wins.
  2. The centre of your paddle returns straight shots. Hit near the edges to angle the ball past the opponent. Space serves or pauses.

Strategy Tips

  • The centre of your paddle returns straight shots. Hit near the edges to angle the ball past the opponent. Space serves or pauses.

Campaign and Difficulty Growth

Wins for each opponent are stored on this device. Practice, Club and Expert are match difficulties, not a stage campaign.

Developer Design Note

Follow the ball. Move left or right to return it.

Player and Save Information

FAQ

How do I move the paddle?
Drag across the court, move the mouse, or hold ← / → (A / D).
How does scoring work?
If the ball passes your end, the opponent gets a point; the first player to 7 wins.
What does the paddle edge do?
The centre returns straight shots; edge hits change the angle and make the next return harder.
What do the difficulties change?
Practice, Club, and Expert change the computer opponent's challenge; they are match difficulties, not stages.
Is progress saved?
Best results, wins, and sound preferences stay in this browser; no account or purchase is required.
