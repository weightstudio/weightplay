Pong

Move the lower paddle left and right, return the ball, and reach 7 points before the computer.

Real-time paddle arcadeAngle controlWall reboundsFirst to 7

Goal

You control the lower paddle. A point is scored only when the ball crosses an end line; the first side to 7 wins the match.

Controls

Drag across the court, move the mouse, or hold ← / → (A / D). Space serves or pauses the match.

Reading returns

Centre hits send straighter returns. Contact near a paddle edge changes the angle. Side-wall rebounds do not score by themselves, so move toward the ball's future crossing point rather than its current horizontal position.

Difficulty and pace

Practice, Club and Expert change the computer challenge. Longer rallies become faster as paddle contacts accumulate, but rally length does not add extra points.

Save information

Wins for each difficulty are stored in this browser. Leaving an active match discards that match score while saved wins remain. No account or online multiplayer is required.

FAQ

How do I move the paddle?
Drag or move the mouse across the court, or hold ← / → (A / D).
How is a point scored?
A point is awarded when the ball passes an end line. First to 7 wins.
What do edge hits do?
They change the return angle; they do not guarantee a point.
Do longer rallies score more?
No. Rally contacts increase pace, while points still come only from end-line misses.
What is saved?
Wins by difficulty are stored locally in this browser.
