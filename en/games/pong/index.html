World and Mission

You control the mint paddle at the bottom. Press Serve.

How the Systems Work

Drag across the court, move your mouse, or hold ← / → (A / D). Return the moving ball; missing your end gives your opponent one point. First to 7 wins.

How to Play

  1. Drag across the court, move your mouse, or hold ← / → (A / D). Return the moving ball; missing your end gives your opponent one point. First to 7 wins.
  2. The centre of your paddle returns straight shots. Hit near the edges to angle the ball past the opponent. Space serves or pauses.

Strategy Tips

  • The centre of your paddle returns straight shots. Hit near the edges to angle the ball past the opponent. Space serves or pauses.

Campaign and Difficulty Growth
