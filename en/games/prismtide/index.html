Prismtide

First-person puzzleLight routingExploration

Your goal

Restore the light network of a submerged city. Walk through each chamber, reconnect the ancient devices and reach the illuminated arch.

How to play

Pick up a portable relay, then place it on an engraved socket. A relay passes its light to visible devices of the same color. Walls block the beam. Moving a relay can open one route while closing another, so consider where you need to walk next.

Controls and rules

Use WASD to walk and drag the view to look around. Arrow keys also turn the view. Press E or use the action button to pick up, place or activate the device you are looking at. On touch screens, move with the shared joystick on the left and drag on the right to look. Carry one device at a time. Q undoes a placement; Reset restores the room. There is no timer or falling penalty.

Finishing and feedback

Light every exit receiver, cross the restored route and activate the arch. Your cleared chambers and cosmetic choices remain in this browser. Revisit a room to try a cleaner solution. No purchase is required to solve any chamber.

A city in two chapters

There are 60 chambers. Early rooms teach sightlines, tool recovery, pressure plates and separate light colors. Later in the first chapter, turning lenses, shutters, bridges and raised viewpoints combine. Chapter two adds a single replaceable echo, counterweights and dry-to-wet tide changes. Earlier rooms in a multi-room chamber use persistent brass locks: after a route opens, you can return for its tools. The final route still needs its own stated conditions. Every fifth clear reveals a memory seal. Clearing chambers 30 and 60 unlocks free equipment finishes.

Designed around a visible decision

We built these rooms around the relationship between where light can travel and where an explorer can walk. The shortest-looking connection can be blocked by a wall, while a higher viewpoint reveals another route. Stable sockets make placement readable on a phone; looking and walking still matter. There is no countdown, combat or falling punishment. Undo restores the last arrangement and the explorer’s position, allowing you to test a hypothesis without losing earlier clears. Replay is a chance to understand the room and reduce unnecessary carrying.

Player and save information

No account or purchase is required. Cleared chambers, selected equipment and preferences are kept in this browser. Clearing site storage or using a private window may remove them. Optional diamond purchases are permanent appearances only. All 60 chambers can be solved with the free equipment. Local practice does not change normal progress or rewards. Your unfinished chamber, undo history and best action counts are also saved. Reset starts the current chamber over.

Explorer questions

Why is the relay dark?

It must stand on a socket with a clear view of an active source or relay of the matching color. Walls and closed shutters block light.

Can I get stuck?

Undo restores the previous device arrangement and your position. You can also reset the chamber without losing earlier progress.

Why did a bridge stay open after I took the relay?

Earlier routes in a multi-room chamber have brass locks. Once their conditions are met, the lock stays open for that attempt, so you can recover tools. The final route still needs its own conditions.

Why did the low socket stop working?

Raising the tide covers a low socket. Charge the receiver while the room is dry first. Its locked energy remains available when you change the tide.

Do equipment finishes change the solutions?

No. Copper, coral, pearl, dawn and starlight finishes change materials only. They never change tool count, light rules or movement.
