Space Rocks

Flight manual

Move with WASD, arrows or the joystick. Weapons aim and fire automatically. Fly near crystals to collect them. Carry pods to the central beacon. Shield blocks damage for 2 seconds; Pulse clears nearby threats. Both recharge. Clear missions to unlock stages. Stars: completion, full hull, full hull with half the time remaining. Progress is stored in this browser.

FAQ

How do I clear a stage?
Complete the objective shown for that stage to unlock the next one. The campaign has 30 stages and six bosses.
What do Shield and Pulse do?
Shield blocks damage for 2 seconds. Pulse clears nearby threats. Both recharge after use.
Is progress saved?
Cleared stages and earned stars are saved in this browser. Leaving during a stage loses that attempt.
