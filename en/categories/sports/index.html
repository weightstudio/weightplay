Free Browser Sports Games

Aim a pool shot, take a football penalty, or time a basketball shot. Choose a sport and check the controls for its shot, save or rally.

  • Rainline Chase

    Pick a gap, time your swing and beat the rain line before play stops.

  • Nori Bankshot

    Aim the cue ball, set shot power and pocket the targets. Some billiards tables require a set order or a shot off the cushion.

  • Penalty Cup

    Hold to aim and release your penalty at the right moment. Then switch to goalkeeping and react to the shot cue.

  • Pong

    Move your paddle, return the ball, and beat the computer to 7 points.

  • Hoop League

    Read the defense, aim your shot and hold to build power. Release in the green zone or use a relay pass to set up an opening across 60 basketball courts.

  • Putt Trails

    Plan putts across 30 holes: pull back to set direction and power, bank around walls, avoid water, and save each hole’s best strokes in this browser.

  • Meadow Touchdown

    Read the defense, lead your receivers with a pass, then weave toward the end zone across 30 challenges.
