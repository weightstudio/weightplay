Free Path and Route Puzzles

Draw one line through every open cell, turn pipes to carry water, or plan a route through ice walls. Choose a puzzle by the kind of path you want to build.

  • Stararc Rendezvous

    Fit your shuttle, plan a transfer, and dock with moving stations before the shift ends.

  • Silicon Shift

    Trace test pulses and wire one circuit to pass every case.

  • Tidegate Relay

    Dispatch vehicles across a coastal interchange. Read the lanes, plan ahead and get priority traffic through on time.

  • Panko's Bus Jam

    Route passengers to matching buses, manage the FIFO holding lane, and clear 30 stops. Earn three stars by minimizing holds without assistance.

  • Prism Route Garden

    Connect matching colors without crossing paths and fill every tile. A gate only lets a path of the matching color through.

  • Panko's Bamboo Waterway

    Tap to rotate the pipes and connect the spring to the basin. On later boards, check the arrows on one-way sluices.

  • Skyport Dispatch

    Draw a flight path from each airship to its matching dock. Pass the numbered beacons in order and route around storms when they block the way.

  • Starlink

    Trace every trail in a constellation exactly once. Pick the right starting point and follow the direction of comet arrows.

  • One Line

    Start at the lit footprint and draw one continuous path through every open square. Move between adjacent squares without visiting any twice.

  • Frost Maze Rescue

    Solve a 30-room ice maze: break and build walls to reach every berry, avoid the snow drifter, then return to the start in the final rooms.

  • Moonlight Workshop Escape

    Restore 60 rooms: light the switches, collect the key and reach the door. Plan lantern charges and counterweights in two chapters.

  • Kite Keeper

    Choose each gust to steer Taro's kite through three sky windows and reach the lantern dock.

  • Wind-Sown Garden

    Choose a seed and send one gust at a time to bloom every flower without brushing a thorn.

  • River Gate Water Routing

    Open gates to route water to gardens and reservoirs. Plan each release around tide phases and how much water each destination can hold.

  • Firefly Direction Codes

    Program Mica’s path through 30 forest maps. Collect fireflies, unlock gates and bring the lantern home.

  • Habitat Domino Chain

    Match dominoes to the open habitat and plan a route to the moon. Use every required tile and save bridge tokens and flips for the right spots.

  • Traffic Jam Diner

    Guide customers through one-way lanes, leave room in the waiting bays, and fill snack orders in queue order.

  • Robot Dog Showtime

    Plan both performers' stage cues, then rehearse their routes and gestures together.

  • Glowspool

    Slide in one direction until you hit an obstacle. Light every open tile while keeping a path around the trail you leave behind.
