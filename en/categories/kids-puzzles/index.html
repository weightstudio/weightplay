Free Puzzle Games for Kids

Match cards from memory, build pictures with tangram pieces or work out a route home. Check the suggested age and controls on each game page.

  • Panko's Tangram Trail

    Drag the seven tangram pieces into place. Tap a piece to rotate it until it fits the outline.

  • Color Link Garden

    Connect matching garden seeds without crossing paths, then fill every tile.

  • Color Lunchbox

    Sort the foods shown in the pictures into lunchboxes of the matching color. Later puzzles use similar shades and boxes that change places.

  • Bubble Bakery

    Clear connected groups of matching bubbles to fill recipe orders. Plan the order of your moves when several trays need ingredients.

  • Vine Rescue

    Cut vines and move a leaf trampoline to bounce fruit into a basket. Use the wind and time your bounces as the targets move.

  • Star Memory

    Flip cards and remember their positions to find matching pictures. Use previews and follow the target order in later puzzles.

  • Snack Blocks

    Swap neighboring snacks to line up at least three of the same kind. Set up cascades and meet each stage’s score or collection goal.

  • Merge Tower

    Drop and merge matching pieces. Aim for the target zones as narrow spaces, wind and gravity change each physics puzzle.

  • Pet Garden Tiles

    Flip tiles to find matching pictures. Remember their positions when mist hides the board or tiles move, with no countdown.

  • Rescue Trail

    Guide animals home in 30 saved 5×5 route puzzles. Six regions add fruit goals, mud that costs two moves, key gates and one-use tiles; there is no countdown.

  • Hidden Safari

    Find six listed animals in each of 30 saved habitats. Ordered searches, pairs, camouflage, decoys and moving patrols change the hunt; two hints help and time never causes failure.

  • Shape Train

    Shape Train has 30 shape-matching routes for kids. Match each passenger to one of six train cars; later routes add changing positions, memory checks and passenger-first ticket checks. Each route has four to eight passengers, and a wrong choice lets you try again.

  • Helper Quest

    Solve 30 animal-care missions by matching picture clues to eight tools, choosing priorities, recalling hidden needs, and adapting when the tool tray moves.

  • Block Grove

    Place three block groups on an 8×8 forest board, clear rows and columns, and finish 30 habitat missions or continue in Infinite Grove.

  • Color Springs

    Sort colors across 30 stages by moving matching top orbs into vessels with room. Plan around seals, changing capacities, and move goals.

  • Bubble Safari

    Aim through 30 bubble-rescue levels with bank shots, four blocker types, wind, moving rows, four power bubbles, and six checkpoints.

  • Screw Workshop

    Move brass screws into open holes to release layered wooden plates across 30 fixed puzzles. Plan around shared supports and each puzzle’s move limit.

  • Forest Parking Patrol

    Choose which carts to move first so the parking lanes clear. Watch signals, convoys and exits before committing to a route.

  • Habitat Mahjong

    Match free tiles on layered Mahjong solitaire boards. Use key pairs to open seals and plan around the routes that change which tiles are available.

  • Word Trails

    Explore 60 word trails with leaf bridges, animal and nature groups, and reusable wild leaves.
