Free Match-3 and Tile-Clearing Games

Swap gems to make a row of three, collect three identical pieces in a tray, or clear pairs of numbers that add to 10. Choose the clearing rule you want to play.

  • Castle Cascade

    Swap neighboring gems to match three or more. Combine special pieces to clear blockers and reach each stage’s goal.

  • Triple Match

    Pick uncovered treasures and match three identical pieces in the tray. Later stages shrink the tray from seven slots to six, so plan your picks.

  • Panko's Number Grove

    Clear pairs that add to ten, opening sight lines across 30 calm groves.

  • Crownfall

    Clear connected rune blocks, choose the battle order and reclaim the crown across 30 castle puzzles.

  • Block Blast

    Place three shapes on an 8×8 board and clear complete rows and columns.

  • Hexa Sort

    Stack colored hex chips, merge matching top colors, and trigger chain clears.

  • Mahjong Solitaire

    Match all identical available pairs to clear the board. Clear each board to unlock the next of 30 stages. Completed stages remain available. Progress is saved in this browser when storage is available.

  • Tetris

    Fill horizontal rows with falling blocks, use the landing outline, and keep the stack low as speed rises.
