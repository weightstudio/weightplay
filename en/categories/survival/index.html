Free Browser Survival Games

Survive enemy waves in a dungeon, find food in the wild, or steer a growing snake around obstacles. Pick a game by the danger you want to learn to handle.

  • Wildborn

    Forage to grow, hide in tall grass and dash away from predators. Evolve during each life, then carry some of your gains into the next one.

  • Fantasy Lion Defense

    Defend the wall from enemy waves. Move to meet each attack and choose fire, storm or fortress talents to change how you fight.

  • Dungeon Venture

    Action survival: dodge hazards while magic attacks automatically. Collect XP crystals, choose spells and develop permanent talents.

  • Relic Hunters

    Explore ruins, dodge enemies and build a relic loadout.

  • Spectrum Pulse

    Auto-fire at nearby enemies and trigger chain explosions with Pulse. Break three cores and reach the exit, defeating a guardian every fifth mission.

  • Rift Salvage

    Guide Orla’s lunar recovery ring through 30 salvage zones: collect smaller debris, rescue stranded cores, and leave before the closing storm.

  • Hero Trials

    Pick a hero with a different skill and fight through connected rooms. Dodge enemy attacks and build mastery for the next trial.

  • Alien Defender

    Pilot the otter gunship through 60 authored battles in two chapters. Break formations, earn upgrades, then cross the Aurora Stormway.

  • Snake

    Steer the snake to collect food and grow. Cross a board edge to enter from the opposite side, and keep clear of your own trail and any glowing obstacles.

  • Maze Chase

    Guide Orla through three increasingly fast maze stages, collect every star mote, learn four Wisp patterns, and activate Beacons to turn pursuit into a counterattack.

  • Blade Orbit

    Collect blades and command a growing sword orbit. Keep moving, evolve your build, and sweep through the horde.
