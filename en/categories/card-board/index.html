Free Card and Board Games

Pick a quick card game, a solitaire variant, or a strategy match on a board. Check each game's rules for opponent and mode options.

  • Hearthloop

    Roll the dice, buy properties and collect rent in a board game. Own matching lots to build houses and keep enough cash for rent and taxes.

  • Klondike Solitaire

    Build four suited foundations from Ace to King. Arrange cards in descending order with alternating colors, and choose to draw one or three at a time.

  • Spider Solitaire

    Clear the board by building sequences from King to Ace in the same suit. Choose a one-, two- or four-suit Spider game.

  • FreeCell Solitaire

    Use four empty cells to plan moves and build four foundations by suit from Ace to King. Keep temporary spaces free for rearranging longer runs.

  • Pyramid Solitaire

    Clear the pyramid by removing exposed pairs that add to 13. Kings come off on their own. Draw from the stock when you need another card.

  • TriPeaks Solitaire

    Clear three peaks by playing an exposed card one rank above or below the discard. Ace and King do not connect. Draw from the stock when you run out of moves.

  • Golf Solitaire

    Clear seven columns by moving exposed cards one rank higher or lower than the top discard. Ace and King do not connect. Use the stock when no card fits.

  • Yukon Solitaire

    Move an exposed group even if its cards are out of order. To place it on another card, the first card must be one rank lower and the opposite color. Build four suited foundations.

  • Beast Deck: The Mist Forest

    Build a deck for turn-based battles. Read the enemy’s next move, spend energy on attacks or blocks, and draft cards between fights.

  • Checkers

    Play a full 8×8 Checkers match: plan legal diagonals, make every available capture, continue multi-jumps, and promote pieces to kings.

  • Tic-Tac-Toe

    Place X and read the rival's immediate O reply across nine squares; make three in a row, block the threat, or settle a draw.

  • Hearts

    Pass three cards, follow suit, and avoid penalty hearts across thirteen tricks.

  • Spades

    Play Spades free in your browser: bid a team contract, follow suit, and use spades as trump.

  • Gin Rummy

    Draw one card and discard one each turn. Build sets and suited runs, then choose when to Knock or finish with Gin.

  • Crazy Eights

    Play Crazy Eights against three AI rivals. Match the active suit or rank, choose the next suit with any Eight, and empty your hand first to win.

  • Cribbage

    Set aside two cards for the crib, then take turns playing cards without going over 31. Score pairs, runs and fifteens on the way to 121.

  • Go Fish

    Ask for a rank in your hand. Take every matching card or draw one, then build four-card books with 2–4 players in this browser card game.

  • War: High Card

    Play solo War: each side reveals one card; the higher rank takes the pile. Ties add three face-down cards per side.

  • Speed Solitaire

    Race the AI in Speed: start with five cards, match either center card by one rank, and refill from a 20-card stock. Clear your cards first.

  • Old Maid

    Pair matching ranks, draw from the next hand, and avoid being left with the Old Maid.

  • Casino

    Play a card to capture a table card of the same value or several table cards whose values add to it.

  • Reversi

    Reversi is an eight-by-eight strategy board game: place a disc to surround opponent discs, flip the chain, and finish with more discs than the AI.

  • Four in a Row

    Drop discs into a column and connect four horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

  • Chess

    Move your pieces to checkmate the king. Choose a tactical challenge or a full match.
