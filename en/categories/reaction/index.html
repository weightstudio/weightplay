Free Reaction and Timing Games

Watch the cue and act at the right moment. Cross moving traffic, time a jump, or catch falling objects, with each game asking for a different kind of quick response.

  • Leafskip

    Time each jump to collect seed packets above the platforms. Jump again in midair when you need more height, then line up a safe landing.

  • Skyspire Drop

    Rotate crystal spires, guide Fia through 30 descents, align shifting gaps, and charge Comet Break before cursed rings stop the fall.

  • Orbit Orchard

    Aim and launch seeds to hit orbiting fruit. Read the wind and wait for a clear shot past the branches.

  • Canopy Cut

    Swipe safe fruit across 30 canopy stages, avoid dark seed pods, and adapt to crosswinds, weaving branches, light cues, and pulse timing.

  • Flip Foundry

    Deliver a crystal through 60 stages. Aim, pass the rings and land; four deliveries clear each stage.

  • Traffic and River Crossing

    Deliver lanterns through 30 handcrafted crossings. Read the traffic, ride the current, and find your own route.

  • Raindrop Catcher

    Move left or right to catch enough raindrops. Follow the landing cues and avoid leaves that break your streak.

  • Skybound Tram

    Fly a tram with a cup of bubble tea aboard. Choose when to gain height, spin for points and steady the cabin for landing.

  • Signal Snap

    A solid diamond means tap that side; a crossed hollow echo means do nothing. Use the two buttons or ← and →.
