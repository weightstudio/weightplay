Animal Honey Shield

Draw glowing nectar lines, repair weak spots during each wave, and keep Clover Pup Pip safe.

WeightPlay Original Game Guide

Protect Pip with the shortest strong line

Pip has wandered beside Cloverlight Meadow's hives. Draw barriers while the startled bees fly, then reinforce openings before they reach the puppy.

How to play

  1. Drag across the meadow to draw a glowing nectar barrier.
  2. Release to begin the bee wave; keep drawing new segments while it runs.
  3. Survive until the timer reaches zero without letting a bee touch Pip.

Rules and results

Each line spends nectar and weakens when bees collide with it. A touch on Pip fails the attempt. A safe countdown clears the stage; shorter lines and unused nectar earn more stars.

Thirty meadow routes

Six chapters add extra hives, split approaches, stone anchors, moving gusts, fragile gaps, and mixed mastery waves. Every clear unlocks the next route.

Practical tips

Anchor short curves between stones, leave room around Pip, and watch where the barrier glows red. A small repair is often stronger than drawing a second full circle.

Save and privacy

Unlocked stages and best stars stay only in this browser. No account, upload, or personal information is required.

FAQ

Can I draw after the bees start?
Yes. Live repairs are the heart of the game, as long as nectar remains.
Do bees get hurt?
No. Nectar barriers gently bounce the startled bees back toward the flowers.
What should I play next?
Try One Line for careful tracing or Animal Guard Yard for a larger defense challenge.
