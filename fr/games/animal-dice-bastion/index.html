Bastion des Dés Animaux

Summon random animal guardians, merge matching ranks, and command the right power before each wave reaches the crystal.

WeightPlay Original Game Guide

Build a stronger board from every surprise

Moss Shell Taro guards the crystal bastion with living rune dice. Every summon changes the board, but careful merges and timely orders keep luck under your control.

How to play

  1. Choisissez un gardien initial, puis dépensez de la charge pour invoquer des gardiens de rang un dans les runes vides.
  2. Drag two guardians of the same affinity and rank together to create one stronger guardian.
  3. Use {rally}, {reroll}, and {burst} while guardians automatically stop each wave.

Rules and results

A creature that reaches the bastion removes core integrity. Clear every wave and Boss to win. Keep at least half the core and avoid Reroll to earn all three stars.

Thirty authored defenses

Six chapters add armor, swarms, healers, haste, slot locks, champion escorts, and six distinct Boss defenses.

Practical tips

Do not merge every pair immediately. Keep a useful low-rank pair until an empty slot or a charged order gives you a safer next move.

Save and privacy

Unlocked Stages, stars, rune dust, and Workshop upgrades stay only in this browser. No account, upload, or personal information is required.

FAQ

Is this a gambling game?
No. Dice only show guardian rank. There is no betting, paid random draw, cash prize, or real-money chance mechanic.
Can a bad draw end the run?
A drought breaker guarantees a useful matching affinity after seven summons without a merge candidate.
What should I play next?
Try Beast Guardian for route-building defense or Animal Rune Tactics for squad preparation.
