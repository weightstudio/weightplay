Jeu public

Découvrez un puzzle logique apaisant : sur chaque carte, placez un chat Moonwhisker dans chaque ligne, colonne et territoire à motif. Les chats ne doivent pas se toucher, même en diagonale, chaque placement réduit donc les choix suivants. Commencez par un territoire avec une seule case légale, utilisez Marquer X pour noter vos déductions et Annuler pour tester une hypothèse. Indice éclaire une case légale sans la placer à votre place. Résolvez la carte 1 pour débloquer la carte 2 et parcourez 30 cartes en six chapitres. La progression reste dans ce navigateur.

How to play

  1. Each row contains exactly one cat.
  2. Each column and each colored territory contains exactly one cat.
  3. Cats cannot touch, even diagonally.
  4. Choose Cat or X, then tap a cell. Undo and Restart are always free.

Complete the atlas

Place all cats correctly to solve the map. A conflict is rejected immediately, but a tentative assumption can be undone.

Practical tips

Start with a territory that has one legal cell. After placing a cat, mark its row, column, and all eight neighboring cells.

FAQ

Do colors carry the only clue?
No. Every territory also has a pattern, symbol, and accessible name.
Is this numeric Sudoku?
No. It is a cat placement puzzle using row, column, territory, and no-touch constraints.
What does Hint show?
Hint highlights a cell that can hold the next correct cat; it does not fill the board.
What happens if a move breaks a rule?
A placement that conflicts with a row, column, patterned territory, or no-touch rule is rejected. Undo restores your previous move.
How do I complete a map?
Place every cat so each row, column, and patterned territory has one, with no cats touching—even diagonally. Solving a map unlocks the next one.
